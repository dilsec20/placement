# ACKO SWE Intern – Developer Operations & Experience

Preparation for the ACKO General Insurance campus process at LPU, based on the job description and the selection email supplied by Dilip.

## Confirmed process

- **18 Nov 2026:** pre-placement talk, then a **100-minute coding-based online test on POD.AI**, then a **5–6 hour buildathon/hackathon**.
- AI tools are allowed as assistance during the buildathon. The email does not say they are allowed during the coding test.
- **19 Nov 2026:** two technical interview rounds.
- Exact test question count, allowed languages, buildathon prompt, scoring rubric and environment are not included in the email; follow the next placement email for those details.

## How this guide was built

The role emphasizes algorithms, OS/networking, OOP, readable code and unit tests, Git/Linux/debugging, and developer platform work (CI checks, service templates, pipeline steps and agentic tooling). The selection email makes the buildathon a major part of the day, so the guide includes a timed implementation practice and a design/review routine.

Candidate reports are useful for picking practice topics, not predicting this year’s paper. Reports cited here span different years, roles and campuses. This year’s confirmed process and role description should take priority.

3. Explain every line of your resume projects and trade-offs.
4. Revise OOP, OS, DBMS, networking, Git and Linux fundamentals.
