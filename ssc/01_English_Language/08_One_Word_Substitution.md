## Introduction

One-Word Substitution means replacing a group of words or a phrase with a single word that conveys the same meaning. This is one of the **most frequently asked** topics in SSC CHSL English.

**Weightage in SSC CHSL Tier 1:**
- Questions: 2-3 out of 25
- Marks: 2 marks each
- Negative marking: 0.50 marks

## Tips and Tricks

### ⚡ Root Word Analysis
Many one-word substitutions share common roots:
- **-cide** = killing (homicide, suicide, genocide)
- **-phobia** = fear (claustrophobia, hydrophobia)
- **-mania** = obsession (bibliomania, kleptomania)
- **-phile** = love (bibliophile, philanthropist)
- **-ist** = person who does (pianist, dentist, optimist)
- **-logy** = study of (biology, psychology, zoology)
- **omni-** = all (omniscient, omnipresent, omnipotent)
- **mono-** = one (monogamy, monopoly, monotone)
- **poly-** = many (polygamy, polyglot, polygon)
- **bi-** = two (bilingual, bigamy, bicycle)

### 🎯 Exam Strategy
1. Learn 10 one-word substitutions daily
2. Focus on the root words — they help in guessing unknown words
3. Pay attention to phobias and manias — very frequently asked
4. Learn the "-cide" words together as a group
5. Practice previous year papers — many words are repeated
