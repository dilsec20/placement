## Introduction

Idioms and Phrases are **regularly asked** in SSC CHSL English. An idiom is a group of words whose meaning is different from the individual words. Understanding idioms requires memorization since they cannot be understood literally.

**Weightage in SSC CHSL Tier 1:**
- Questions: 2-3 out of 25
- Marks: 2 marks each
- Negative marking: 0.50 marks

**Question Format:** "Select the meaning of the idiom/phrase: 'To burn the midnight oil'"

---

## What are Idioms and Phrases?

### Idiom
A group of words with a figurative meaning that differs from the literal meaning.
```
"Kick the bucket" ≠ literally kicking a bucket
"Kick the bucket" = to die
```

### Phrase
A small group of words that forms a meaningful unit but is not a complete sentence.
```
"In the nick of time" = just in time
"At the eleventh hour" = at the last moment
```

### Proverb
A short, well-known saying expressing a general truth or advice.
```
"A stitch in time saves nine" = Fix problems early to prevent bigger issues
```

## Tips and Tricks

### ⚡ How to Remember Idioms
1. **Visual Association** — Create a mental image for each idiom
2. **Story Method** — Use the idiom in a short story
3. **Grouping** — Learn idioms by theme (body parts, animals, colors)
4. **Daily Practice** — Learn 5-10 idioms daily
5. **Use in conversation** — Try using idioms in daily English

### 🎯 Exam Strategy
1. Don't take idioms literally — they have figurative meanings
2. If you don't know the idiom, look for context clues
3. Eliminate clearly wrong options
4. Some idioms have similar themes — be careful to distinguish

### 🧠 Memory Trick: Group by Keywords
- **Color idioms:** Red tape, blue blood, white elephant, green-eyed, black sheep
- **Animal idioms:** Dark horse, crocodile tears, let the cat out, bull in china shop
- **Body part idioms:** Cold shoulder, all ears, bite the bullet, foot the bill
- **Food idioms:** Piece of cake, spill the beans, egg on face, bread and butter
