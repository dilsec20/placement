## Introduction

Fill in the Blanks is a **very important** topic in SSC CHSL English. It tests your knowledge of grammar, vocabulary, prepositions, conjunctions, and overall understanding of the English language. In each question, a sentence has one or more blanks, and you must choose the most appropriate word(s) to complete it.

**Weightage in SSC CHSL Tier 1:**
- Questions: 2-4 out of 25
- Marks: 2 marks each
- Negative marking: 0.50 marks

## Types of Questions

### Type 1: Single Blank (Grammar-Based)
> "She _____ to school every day."
> (a) go (b) goes ✅ (c) going (d) gone

### Type 2: Single Blank (Vocabulary-Based)
> "The new policy will _____ the economy."
> (a) boost ✅ (b) reduce (c) ignore (d) delay

### Type 3: Double Blanks
> "The _____ of the evidence clearly _____ his innocence."
> (a) weight, proves ✅ (b) wait, provides (c) light, shows (d) might, proves

### Type 4: Preposition-Based
> "She is interested _____ music."
> (a) on (b) in ✅ (c) at (d) for

### Type 5: Conjunction-Based
> "_____ he was tired, he continued working."
> (a) Although ✅ (b) Because (c) So (d) And

## Grammar-Based Fill in the Blanks

### A. Tense-Based Questions

#### Simple Present
```
Use for: habits, routines, universal truths, scheduled events
Signal words: always, usually, often, sometimes, daily, every day

Example: He _____ (go) to office daily.
Answer: goes
```

#### Present Continuous
```
Use for: actions happening now, temporary actions, future plans
Signal words: now, at present, currently, right now, these days

Example: She _____ (read) a novel at present.
Answer: is reading
```

#### Present Perfect
```
Use for: actions completed with present relevance, experiences
Signal words: already, just, yet, ever, never, recently, so far, since, for

Example: I _____ (visit) Paris twice.
Answer: have visited
```

#### Simple Past
```
Use for: completed actions in the past
Signal words: yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020, when

Example: She _____ (buy) a car last month.
Answer: bought
```

#### Past Perfect
```
Use for: action completed before another past action
Signal words: before, after, by the time, already, until

Example: He _____ (leave) before I arrived.
Answer: had left
```

#### Future Tenses
```
Simple Future: will + V1 → She will come tomorrow.
Going to: am/is/are + going to + V1 → It is going to rain.
Future Perfect: will + have + V3 → I will have finished by then.
```

### B. Voice-Based Questions
```
Active → Passive transformation:
"The letter _____ (write) by her yesterday."
Answer: was written

"The bridge _____ (build) by the government next year."
Answer: will be built

"The homework _____ already _____ (complete)."
Answer: has already been completed
```

### C. Modal Verb Questions

| Modal | Use | Example |
|-------|-----|---------|
| Can | Ability (present) | He **can** swim well. |
| Could | Ability (past) / Polite request | She **could** sing beautifully. |
| May | Permission / Possibility | You **may** leave now. |
| Might | Less certain possibility | It **might** rain tonight. |
| Must | Obligation / Strong necessity | You **must** obey the rules. |
| Should | Advice / Recommendation | You **should** study harder. |
| Would | Polite request / Past habit | He **would** walk daily in the park. |
| Shall | Offer / Suggestion (I/We) | **Shall** we go now? |
| Need | Necessity | You **need** not worry. |
| Ought to | Moral obligation | You **ought to** help the poor. |

```
Example: You _____ consult a doctor immediately.
Answer: should / must

Example: _____ I come in, sir?
Answer: May

Example: He _____ read when he was four years old.
Answer: could
```

### D. Gerund vs. Infinitive Questions

#### Verbs followed by Gerund (-ing)
```
enjoy, avoid, mind, finish, suggest, deny, admit, consider, 
delay, keep, miss, practice, risk, imagine, postpone, resist, quit

✅ She enjoys dancing.
❌ She enjoys to dance.

✅ He avoids eating junk food.
❌ He avoids to eat junk food.
```

#### Verbs followed by Infinitive (to + V1)
```
want, wish, hope, decide, plan, promise, refuse, agree, 
learn, afford, manage, pretend, expect, need, offer, fail

✅ He wants to play cricket.
❌ He wants playing cricket.

✅ She decided to leave early.
❌ She decided leaving early.
```

#### Verbs that take BOTH (with different meanings)
| Verb | + Gerund | + Infinitive |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Remember | remember doing (past) | remember to do (future) |
| Forget | forget doing (past) | forget to do (future) |
| Stop | stop doing (quit) | stop to do (pause to do) |
| Try | try doing (experiment) | try to do (attempt) |
| Regret | regret doing (past) | regret to inform (present) |

## Conjunction-Based Fill in the Blanks

### Types of Conjunctions

#### Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS)
```
F - For (because/since)
A - And (addition)
N - Nor (negative addition)
B - But (contrast)
O - Or (choice)
Y - Yet (contrast)
S - So (result)

Examples:
"He was tired, _____ he continued working."  → but/yet
"She studied hard, _____ she passed the exam."  → so
"You can have tea _____ coffee."  → or
```

#### Subordinating Conjunctions
| Purpose | Conjunctions | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Time | when, while, before, after, until, since, as soon as | **After** he left, I cried. |
| Cause/Reason | because, since, as | **Because** it rained, we stayed home. |
| Contrast | although, though, even though, whereas, while | **Although** he is rich, he is unhappy. |
| Condition | if, unless, provided that, as long as | **If** you study, you will pass. |
| Purpose | so that, in order that | He works hard **so that** he can succeed. |
| Result | so...that, such...that | He is **so** tall **that** he can touch the ceiling. |
| Comparison | as...as, than | She is **as** smart **as** her brother. |

#### Correlative Conjunctions (Pairs)
```
either...or         →  Either you or I will go.
neither...nor       →  Neither tea nor coffee was served.
both...and          →  Both Ram and Shyam are doctors.
not only...but also →  She is not only smart but also kind.
whether...or        →  I don't know whether to laugh or cry.
hardly...when       →  Hardly had I slept when the bell rang.
scarcely...when     →  Scarcely had he arrived when it rained.
no sooner...than    →  No sooner did he come than it started raining.
```

## Tips and Tricks

### ⚡ Quick-Solving Strategy
1. **Read the entire sentence** — understand context
2. **Identify the type** — grammar, vocabulary, or preposition?
3. **Look for clues** — tense markers, preposition pairs, tone
4. **Eliminate wrong options** — usually 2 are clearly wrong
5. **Use the process of elimination** — narrow to 2, then choose

### 🎯 Common Traps to Avoid
1. Don't choose a word just because it sounds good — check grammar
2. Watch for similar-sounding words (affect/effect, complement/compliment)
3. Check subject-verb agreement in the blank
4. Verify tense consistency
5. In double blanks, BOTH words must be correct together

### 🧠 Vocabulary Building Tips
1. Learn **10 new words daily** with sentences
2. Use flashcards (physical or Anki app)
3. Read The Hindu editorial — note difficult words
4. Maintain a "Word of the Day" journal
5. Group words by themes (positive, negative, formal, informal)

## Last 10 Years PYQs

### PYQ 1 — SSC CHSL 2024
**Q.** Fill in the blank: "He has been working here _____ 2015."
- (a) for (b) since ✅ (c) from (d) by
- **Explanation:** "2015" is a point of time → use "since"

### PYQ 2 — SSC CHSL 2024
**Q.** "She is _____ honest woman."
- (a) a (b) an ✅ (c) the (d) no article
- **Explanation:** "honest" has a silent 'h' → vowel sound → "an"

### PYQ 3 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** "The government has decided to _____ the old policy."
- (a) do away with ✅ (b) do up with (c) do in with (d) do for with
- **Explanation:** "Do away with" means to abolish

### PYQ 4 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** "If I _____ a bird, I would fly to you."
- (a) am (b) was (c) were ✅ (d) be
- **Explanation:** Unreal condition (Type 2) → "were" (subjunctive)

### PYQ 5 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** "He is _____ intelligent that he can solve any problem."
- (a) very (b) too (c) so ✅ (d) such
- **Explanation:** "so + adjective + that" is the correct structure

### PYQ 6 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** "She insisted _____ going to the party."
- (a) at (b) for (c) on ✅ (d) to
- **Explanation:** "Insist on" is the correct preposition

### PYQ 7 — SSC CHSL 2021
**Q.** "Hardly had he reached home _____ it started raining."
- (a) than (b) when ✅ (c) that (d) then
- **Explanation:** "Hardly...when" is the correct pair

### PYQ 8 — SSC CHSL 2021
**Q.** "The teacher made the students _____ the exercise again."
- (a) to do (b) do ✅ (c) doing (d) did
- **Explanation:** "Make + object + V1 (base form)" — no "to"

### PYQ 9 — SSC CHSL 2020
**Q.** "She is looking forward _____ meeting her friend."
- (a) for (b) at (c) to ✅ (d) in
- **Explanation:** "Look forward to + gerund" is correct

### PYQ 10 — SSC CHSL 2020
**Q.** "_____ the rain, the match continued."
- (a) Inspite (b) Despite ✅ (c) Although (d) Because
- **Explanation:** "Despite + noun/gerund" (no "of"); Also "In spite of" (two words with "of")

### PYQ 11 — SSC CHSL 2019
**Q.** "He is _____ European, not _____ Asian."
- (a) a, an ✅ (b) an, a (c) a, a (d) an, an
- **Explanation:** European = /juː/ (consonant sound) → "a"; Asian = /eɪ/ (vowel sound) → "an"

### PYQ 12 — SSC CHSL 2019
**Q.** "Neither Ram _____ Shyam has come."
- (a) or (b) and (c) nor ✅ (d) but
- **Explanation:** "Neither...nor" is the correct pair

### PYQ 13 — SSC CHSL 2018
**Q.** "He _____ smoking last year."
- (a) gave in (b) gave up ✅ (c) gave away (d) gave off
- **Explanation:** "Give up" = quit/stop a habit

### PYQ 14 — SSC CHSL 2018
**Q.** "The news _____ broadcast every evening at 9 pm."
- (a) are (b) is ✅ (c) were (d) have been
- **Explanation:** "News" is uncountable → singular verb "is"

### PYQ 15 — SSC CHSL 2017
**Q.** "I am accustomed _____ getting up early."
- (a) for (b) in (c) to ✅ (d) with
- **Explanation:** "Accustomed to" is the correct phrase

### PYQ 16 — SSC CHSL 2017
**Q.** "The child _____ asleep while studying."
- (a) fell ✅ (b) fallen (c) felt (d) falling
- **Explanation:** "Fall asleep" = go to sleep; past tense = "fell"

### PYQ 17 — SSC CHSL 2016
**Q.** "She is _____ to win the competition."
- (a) enough talented (b) talented enough ✅ (c) too talented (d) very talented
- **Explanation:** "Adjective + enough + to" is the correct structure

### PYQ 18 — SSC CHSL 2016
**Q.** "He takes _____ his father in looks."
- (a) on (b) after ✅ (c) up (d) to
- **Explanation:** "Take after" = resemble

### PYQ 19 — SSC CHSL 2015
**Q.** "_____ he is poor, he is honest."
- (a) Because (b) Since (c) Although ✅ (d) So
- **Explanation:** The sentence shows contrast → "Although" (despite being poor, he is honest)

### PYQ 20 — SSC CHSL 2015
**Q.** "By the time he arrived, we _____ our lunch."
- (a) have finished (b) had finished ✅ (c) finished (d) will finish
- **Explanation:** Past Perfect (had + V3) for action completed before another past action
