## Introduction

Synonyms (words with similar meanings) and Antonyms (words with opposite meanings) are **consistently asked** in SSC CHSL. You can expect 2-4 questions on this topic. The key to scoring is building a strong vocabulary.

**Weightage in SSC CHSL Tier 1:**
- Questions: 2-4 out of 25
- Marks: 2 marks each
- Negative marking: 0.50 marks

---

## What are Synonyms and Antonyms?

### Synonyms
Words that have **similar or nearly the same meaning**.
```
Happy → Joyful, Delighted, Elated, Cheerful, Blissful
Big → Large, Huge, Enormous, Massive, Gigantic
```

### Antonyms
Words that have **opposite meanings**.
```
Happy ↔ Sad, Unhappy, Miserable, Sorrowful
Big ↔ Small, Tiny, Minute, Diminutive
```

---

## How to Solve Synonym/Antonym Questions

### Step-by-Step Method
1. **Read the word carefully** — make sure you understand it
2. **Think of its meaning** in your own words
3. **Recall similar/opposite words** you know
4. **Look at all options** — don't jump to the first match
5. **Use context** if the word is used in a sentence
6. **Eliminate clearly wrong options** first
7. **Use root word knowledge** if the word is unfamiliar

### Common Question Formats
```
Format 1: "Select the synonym of PRUDENT"
Format 2: "Choose the word most similar in meaning to BENEVOLENT"
Format 3: "The word EPHEMERAL means..." (hidden synonym question)
Format 4: "Select the antonym of GENEROUS"
Format 5: "Choose the word most opposite in meaning to BRAVE"
```

### 🎯 Exam Day Tips
1. **If you don't know the word**, try breaking it into prefix + root + suffix
2. **Eliminate options** that are synonyms of each other (for antonym questions)
3. **Look at the tone** — positive word → antonym is negative word
4. **Don't confuse similar-looking words** (emigrate/immigrate, elicit/illicit)
5. **Context matters** — the same word can have different synonyms in different contexts

### 🧠 Memory Techniques
1. **Associate words with images** — visual memory is stronger
2. **Create word groups** — learn 5 synonyms together
3. **Use sentences** — "The BENEVOLENT (kind) king helped the poor"
4. **Antonym pairs** — learn words in opposite pairs
5. **Daily revision** — revise 50 words daily before exam
