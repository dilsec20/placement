### Q001 · Vocabulary

Choose the antonym of “incessant”.

A. superficial
B. counterfeit
C. intermittent
D. lengthy

**Answer:** C. intermittent
**Explanation:** incessant means continuous; its antonym is intermittent.
**Shortcut / rule:** Learn the word in a synonym–antonym pair: incessant → continuous / intermittent.

### Q002 · Grammar

Choose the grammatically correct word or phrase: Hardly had the bell rung ___ the students left.

A. than
B. when
C. while
D. then

**Answer:** B. when
**Explanation:** The paired construction is "hardly...when."
**Shortcut / rule:** Find the true subject first; ignore phrases between the subject and verb.
