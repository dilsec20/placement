## Introduction

Error Detection (Spotting Errors) is a **core topic** in the SSC CHSL English section. In this question type, a sentence is divided into parts (usually 3-4), and you need to identify which part contains a grammatical error. Sometimes, "No Error" is also an option.

**Weightage in SSC CHSL Tier 1:**
- Questions: 2–4 out of 25
- Marks: 2 marks each
- Negative marking: 0.50 marks

**Key Areas Tested:**
- Subject-Verb Agreement
- Tense Usage
- Articles (a, an, the)
- Prepositions
- Pronouns
- Adjectives & Adverbs
- Conjunctions
- Parallelism
- Redundancy
- Commonly Confused Words

## Types of Error Detection Questions

### Type 1: Sentence Divided into Parts
> **(a)** Each of the students / **(b)** have completed / **(c)** their assignments / **(d)** No error
> **Answer:** (b) — "have" should be "has" (Each → singular subject)

### Type 2: Spot and Correct the Error
> Find the error in the sentence and select the correct option.

### Type 3: Error in a Word/Phrase
> Identify which word/phrase is incorrect.

## Grammar Rules for Error Detection

## Subject-Verb Agreement Rules

### Rule 1: Singular Subject → Singular Verb
| Subject | Correct Verb | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| He/She/It | is, was, has, does | He **is** a teacher. |
| I | am, was, have, do | I **am** a student. |
| You/We/They | are, were, have, do | They **are** engineers. |

### Rule 2: "Each," "Every," "Either," "Neither" → Singular Verb
```
✅ Each of the boys has a book.
❌ Each of the boys have a book.

✅ Every student was present.
❌ Every student were present.

✅ Either of the two is correct.
❌ Either of the two are correct.

✅ Neither of them was selected.
❌ Neither of them were selected.
```

### Rule 3: "Either...or," "Neither...nor" — Verb agrees with the NEAREST subject
```
✅ Either the teacher or the students are wrong.
✅ Either the students or the teacher is wrong.

✅ Neither he nor they have come.
✅ Neither they nor he has come.
```

### Rule 4: Collective Nouns — Singular when acting as a unit, Plural when acting as individuals
```
✅ The team is playing well. (as a unit)
✅ The team are divided in their opinions. (as individuals)

✅ The committee has made its decision.
✅ The jury were unable to agree among themselves.
```

### Rule 5: Uncountable Nouns → Always Singular Verb
```
Uncountable nouns: information, advice, furniture, luggage, 
knowledge, news, bread, money, hair, scenery, poetry, 
machinery, stationery, crockery
```
```
✅ The news is shocking.
❌ The news are shocking.

✅ The furniture was expensive.
❌ The furnitures were expensive.

✅ His luggage has arrived.
❌ His luggages have arrived.
```

### Rule 6: "A number of" (Plural Verb) vs. "The number of" (Singular Verb)
```
✅ A number of students have passed. (= Many students)
✅ The number of students has increased. (= The total count)
```

### Rule 7: Subjects joined by "and" → Plural Verb
```
✅ Bread and butter are on the table. (two separate things)
BUT
✅ Bread and butter is a healthy breakfast. (single concept)
```

### Rule 8: Titles, Books, Countries → Singular Verb
```
✅ "The Arabian Nights" is an interesting book.
✅ The United States is a powerful country.
✅ Mathematics is my favorite subject.
```

### Rule 9: "One of" + Plural Noun + Singular Verb
```
✅ One of the boys is missing.
❌ One of the boys are missing.

✅ One of the reasons is clear.
❌ One of the reasons are clear.
```

### Rule 10: Distances, Weights, Amounts → Singular Verb
```
✅ Five kilometers is a long distance.
✅ Two thousand rupees is a big amount.
✅ Ten kilograms is too heavy.
```

## Tense Errors

### Rule 1: Don't Mix Tenses Unnecessarily
```
❌ He went to the market and buys vegetables.
✅ He went to the market and bought vegetables.

❌ She is reading a book and wrote notes.
✅ She is reading a book and writing notes.
```

### Rule 2: Time Expressions and Their Tenses
| Time Expression | Tense | Example |
|----------------|-------|---------|
| Yesterday, last week, ago | Simple Past | He **came** yesterday. |
| Tomorrow, next week | Simple Future | She **will come** tomorrow. |
| Now, at present, currently | Present Continuous | I **am working** now. |
| Already, just, yet, ever, never | Present Perfect | He **has already left**. |
| Since, for (with duration) | Present Perfect/Past Perfect | I **have lived** here since 2010. |
| Before, after, by the time | Past Perfect | He **had left** before I arrived. |
| While | Past Continuous | She was reading **while** I was cooking. |
| Daily, always, usually, often | Simple Present | He **goes** to school daily. |

### Rule 3: "Since" vs. "For"
```
Since → Point of time (since 2010, since Monday, since morning)
For → Period of time (for 5 years, for 3 days, for 2 hours)

✅ I have been waiting since morning.
✅ I have been waiting for 3 hours.
❌ I have been waiting since 3 hours.
❌ I have been waiting for morning.
```

### Rule 4: Conditional Sentences
| Type | If Clause | Main Clause | Example |
|------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Type 0 (General truth) | Simple Present | Simple Present | If you heat ice, it melts. |
| Type 1 (Real/Possible) | Simple Present | will + V1 | If it rains, I will stay home. |
| Type 2 (Unreal Present) | Simple Past | would + V1 | If I were rich, I would travel. |
| Type 3 (Unreal Past) | Past Perfect | would have + V3 | If he had studied, he would have passed. |

```
❌ If I will go there, I will meet him.
✅ If I go there, I will meet him.

❌ If he would have come, I would have helped.
✅ If he had come, I would have helped him.

❌ If I was you, I would not do this.
✅ If I were you, I would not do this.
```

## Article Errors

### Rule 1: Use of "A" and "An"
```
"A" → before consonant SOUNDS: a book, a university, a European, a one-rupee note
"An" → before vowel SOUNDS: an apple, an hour, an honest man, an MBA, an MLA

Key: It depends on SOUND, not spelling!

✅ He is an honest man. (h is silent → vowel sound)
✅ He bought a uniform. (u sounds like 'yu' → consonant sound)
✅ She is an M.A. student. (M sounds like 'em' → vowel sound)
✅ He is a European. (E sounds like 'yu' → consonant sound)
```

### Rule 2: Use of "The" (Definite Article)
```
Use "THE" before:
1. Unique things: the Sun, the Moon, the Earth, the sky
2. Superlatives: the tallest, the best, the most beautiful
3. Ordinals: the first, the second, the last
4. Musical instruments: the piano, the guitar, the flute
5. Rivers, oceans, seas: the Ganga, the Pacific, the Arabian Sea
6. Mountain ranges: the Himalayas, the Alps, the Andes
7. Groups of islands: the Andamans, the Maldives
8. Religious books: the Quran, the Bible, the Ramayana
9. Newspapers: the Times of India, the Hindu
10. Directions: the east, the west (when used as nouns)
11. Deserts: the Sahara, the Thar
12. Countries with "Republic," "Kingdom," "States": the USA, the UK
```

### Rule 3: No Article (Zero Article)
```
No article before:
1. Proper nouns (usually): India, Delhi, Ram
2. Languages: English, Hindi, French
3. Meals (general): breakfast, lunch, dinner
4. Sports: cricket, football, chess
5. Subjects: Mathematics, Science, History
6. Single mountains/peaks: Mount Everest, Mount Fuji
7. Lakes: Lake Superior, Dal Lake
8. Continents: Asia, Europe, Africa
9. Abstract nouns (general): happiness, love, beauty

❌ The India is a democratic country.
✅ India is a democratic country.

❌ I like the cricket.
✅ I like cricket.

❌ The honesty is the best policy.
✅ Honesty is the best policy.
```

## Preposition Errors

### Common Preposition Usage
| Correct Usage | Incorrect Usage |
|--------------|----------------|
| Interested **in** | Interested ~~on/for~~ |
| Agree **with** (person) | Agree ~~to~~ (person) |
| Agree **to** (proposal) | Agree ~~with~~ (proposal) |
| Differ **from** | Differ ~~with/than~~ |
| Different **from** | Different ~~than~~ |
| Consist **of** | Consist ~~in~~ |
| Depend **on/upon** | Depend ~~over~~ |
| Die **of** (disease) | Die ~~from~~ (disease) |
| Die **from** (external cause) | Die ~~of~~ (wound) |
| Congratulate **on** | Congratulate ~~for~~ |
| Prefer A **to** B | Prefer A ~~than~~ B |
| Superior/Inferior **to** | Superior/Inferior ~~than~~ |
| Senior/Junior **to** | Senior/Junior ~~than~~ |
| Prior **to** | Prior ~~than~~ |
| Married **to** | Married ~~with~~ |
| Angry **with** (person) | Angry ~~on~~ (person) |
| Angry **at** (thing) | Angry ~~for~~ (thing) |
| Good **at** (skill) | Good ~~in~~ (skill) |
| Arrive **at** (small place) | Arrive ~~to~~ |
| Arrive **in** (big city/country) | Arrive ~~at~~ (country) |
| Abstain **from** | Abstain ~~of~~ |
| Accuse **of** | Accuse ~~for~~ |
| Acquitted **of** | Acquitted ~~from~~ |
| Eligible **for** | Eligible ~~of~~ |
| Entrust **with** | Entrust ~~to~~ |

### Prepositions of Time
```
AT → specific time: at 5 o'clock, at noon, at night, at dawn
ON → specific day/date: on Monday, on 15th August, on Diwali
IN → month/year/season: in January, in 2024, in summer, in the morning

❌ He comes in Monday.
✅ He comes on Monday.

❌ I wake up on 6 a.m.
✅ I wake up at 6 a.m.
```

### Prepositions of Place
```
AT → specific point: at the bus stop, at the door, at the corner
IN → enclosed area: in the room, in India, in the box
ON → surface: on the table, on the wall, on the floor

❌ He lives at India.
✅ He lives in India.

❌ The book is in the table.
✅ The book is on the table.
```

## Pronoun Errors

### Rule 1: Pronoun-Antecedent Agreement
```
✅ Every student must bring his/her book.
❌ Every student must bring their book. (Every = singular)

✅ Neither of the boys has done his homework.
❌ Neither of the boys have done their homework.
```

### Rule 2: Subjective vs. Objective Pronouns
```
Subjective: I, we, he, she, they, who
Objective: me, us, him, her, them, whom

✅ It is I who am responsible. (NOT "me who is")
✅ Between you and me, this is wrong. (NOT "between you and I")
✅ He is taller than I (am). (NOT "taller than me" — formal)
✅ Let him and me go. (NOT "Let he and I go")
```

### Rule 3: Reflexive Pronouns
```
myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, themselves

✅ He hurt himself.
❌ He hurt hisself.

✅ They enjoyed themselves.
❌ They enjoyed theirselves.

Don't use reflexive pronouns as subjects:
❌ Myself and Ram went to school.
✅ Ram and I went to school.
```

### Rule 4: Relative Pronouns
```
Who → for persons (subject)
Whom → for persons (object)
Which → for things/animals
That → for persons and things
Whose → possessive

✅ The man who came here is my uncle.
✅ The man whom I met is a doctor.
✅ The book which I bought is interesting.
✅ This is the house whose roof is red.
```

## Adjective and Adverb Errors

### Rule 1: Comparative and Superlative Degrees
```
Positive: tall, beautiful, good
Comparative: taller, more beautiful, better (comparison of TWO)
Superlative: tallest, most beautiful, best (comparison of THREE or more)

❌ He is the tallest of the two.
✅ He is the taller of the two.

❌ She is more prettier than her sister.
✅ She is prettier than her sister.

❌ This is the most unique design.
✅ This is a unique design. (unique = absolute adjective, no degree)
```

### Rule 2: Absolute Adjectives (Cannot be compared)
```
unique, perfect, complete, universal, infinite, 
supreme, round, square, dead, empty, full, 
impossible, unanimous, ultimate, eternal

❌ most unique, very perfect, more complete
✅ unique, perfect, complete (use as they are)

BUT you can say: "nearly perfect," "almost complete"
```

### Rule 3: Adjective vs. Adverb
```
Adjective → modifies noun: She is a good dancer.
Adverb → modifies verb: She dances well.

❌ He runs quick.
✅ He runs quickly.

❌ She sings beautiful.
✅ She sings beautifully.

❌ I feel badly about it.
✅ I feel bad about it. (feel = linking verb → adjective)
```

### Rule 4: Order of Adjectives (OSASCOMP)
```
O - Opinion (beautiful, ugly)
S - Size (big, small)
A - Age (old, young, new)
S - Shape (round, square)
C - Color (red, blue)
O - Origin (Indian, American)
M - Material (wooden, silver)
P - Purpose (sleeping bag, running shoes)

✅ A beautiful large old round red Indian wooden dining table
```

## Parallelism Errors

### Rule: Elements in a series must be in the same grammatical form
```
❌ She likes dancing, singing, and to read.
✅ She likes dancing, singing, and reading.

❌ He is smart, handsome, and has talent.
✅ He is smart, handsome, and talented.

❌ The teacher told the students to sit down, open their books, and they should start reading.
✅ The teacher told the students to sit down, open their books, and start reading.

❌ Not only he is intelligent but also hardworking.
✅ He is not only intelligent but also hardworking.
```

## Tips and Tricks

### ⚡ Quick Solving Strategy
1. **Read the entire sentence first** — don't jump to parts
2. **Check Subject-Verb Agreement** first — most common error
3. **Look at tenses** — ensure consistency
4. **Check prepositions** — very frequent errors
5. **Verify articles** — a, an, the usage
6. **Look for redundancy** — very common in SSC
7. **If everything seems fine**, choose "No Error" (but be cautious!)

### 🎯 Most Frequently Tested Rules
1. Subject-Verb Agreement (Each, Every, Neither, Either)
2. Preposition errors (interested in, married to, superior to)
3. Tense consistency
4. Article usage (a/an based on sound)
5. Redundancy (return back, repeat again)
6. Pronoun errors
7. Comparative/Superlative degree

### 🧠 Memory Tricks
- **STAIN** for words taking "to" not "than": **S**uperior, in**T**erior, **A**nterior, **I**nferior, se**N**ior, junior, prior, prefer
- **"NO THANKS"** for uncountable nouns: **N**ews, **O**ffspring, **T**rousers, **H**air, **A**dvice, **N**onsense, **K**nowledge, **S**cenery

## Last 10 Years PYQs (SSC CHSL)

### PYQ 1 — SSC CHSL 2024
**Q.** Select the part of the sentence that has an error.
"Each of the players (a) / have been given (b) / a new jersey. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (b) — "have" should be **"has"** (Each = singular)

### PYQ 2 — SSC CHSL 2024
**Q.** "He is (a) / one of the tallest boy (b) / in the class. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (b) — "boy" should be **"boys"** (one of the + plural noun)

### PYQ 3 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** "Neither the teacher (a) / nor the students (b) / was present in the class. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (c) — "was" should be **"were"** (verb agrees with nearest subject "students" → plural)

### PYQ 4 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** "She prefers tea (a) / than coffee (b) / in the morning. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (b) — "than" should be **"to"** (prefer A to B)

### PYQ 5 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** "The news (a) / are very shocking (b) / for the whole family. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (b) — "are" should be **"is"** (news = uncountable → singular verb)

### PYQ 6 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** "He asked me (a) / that why I was (b) / absent from the meeting. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (b) — Remove "that" (In indirect speech, "that" is not used with question words)

### PYQ 7 — SSC CHSL 2021
**Q.** "Despite of being ill, (a) / he attended (b) / the important meeting. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (a) — "Despite of" should be **"Despite"** or **"In spite of"**

### PYQ 8 — SSC CHSL 2021
**Q.** "The furniture in the office (a) / are very old (b) / and needs to be replaced. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (b) — "are" should be **"is"** (furniture = uncountable noun)

### PYQ 9 — SSC CHSL 2020
**Q.** "He is senior (a) / than me (b) / in the organization. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (b) — "than" should be **"to"** (senior to, not senior than)

### PYQ 10 — SSC CHSL 2020
**Q.** "I have been living (a) / in Delhi since (b) / five years. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (c) — "five years" requires **"for"** not "since" (since = point of time; for = period)

### PYQ 11 — SSC CHSL 2019
**Q.** "If I was you, (a) / I would not (b) / accept this offer. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (a) — "was" should be **"were"** (Subjunctive mood: If I were you)

### PYQ 12 — SSC CHSL 2019
**Q.** "He told me (a) / that he will come (b) / the next day. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (b) — "will" should be **"would"** (Indirect speech: will → would)

### PYQ 13 — SSC CHSL 2018
**Q.** "One of the boy (a) / has not done (b) / his homework. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (a) — "boy" should be **"boys"** (One of the + plural noun)

### PYQ 14 — SSC CHSL 2018
**Q.** "A honest man (a) / is respected (b) / everywhere. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (a) — "A" should be **"An"** (honest → silent 'h' → vowel sound)

### PYQ 15 — SSC CHSL 2017
**Q.** "He returned back (a) / the book to the library (b) / on time. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (a) — Remove "back" (return = go back; "return back" is redundant)

### PYQ 16 — SSC CHSL 2017
**Q.** "She is more taller (a) / than her (b) / elder sister. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (a) — Remove "more" (taller is already comparative; don't use "more" with "-er")

### PYQ 17 — SSC CHSL 2016
**Q.** "The committee (a) / have decided to (b) / postpone the meeting. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (b) — "have" should be **"has"** (committee = collective noun, acting as unit → singular)

### PYQ 18 — SSC CHSL 2016
**Q.** "He congratulated me (a) / for my success (b) / in the examination. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (b) — "for" should be **"on"** (congratulate on, not for)

### PYQ 19 — SSC CHSL 2015
**Q.** "The teacher asked (a) / the students to (b) / keep quite. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (c) — "quite" should be **"quiet"** (quite = rather; quiet = silent)

### PYQ 20 — SSC CHSL 2015
**Q.** "Unless you do not (a) / work hard, you will (b) / not succeed. (c) / No error (d)"
- **Answer:** (a) — Remove "not" (Unless = If not; using "not" makes it double negative)

## Quick Revision Formula Sheet

### Top 20 Rules to Remember Before Exam

| # | Rule | Example |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | Each/Every + Singular Verb | Each **has** |
| 2 | One of the + Plural Noun + Singular Verb | One of the boys **is** |
| 3 | Neither/Either + Singular Verb (when alone) | Neither **is** correct |
| 4 | Neither...nor / Either...or → Verb with nearest | Neither he nor they **are** |
| 5 | Prefer A **to** B (not "than") | Prefer tea **to** coffee |
| 6 | Superior/Senior **to** (not "than") | Senior **to** me |
| 7 | News/Furniture/Luggage = Uncountable → Singular | News **is** |
| 8 | Since + Point of time | Since 2020 |
| 9 | For + Period of time | For 5 years |
| 10 | Unless = If not (don't add "not") | Unless you work hard |
| 11 | Despite (no "of") / In spite of | Despite being ill |
| 12 | Return (no "back") | Return the book |
| 13 | If I **were** (subjunctive) | If I were you |
| 14 | An + vowel SOUND | An honest man |
| 15 | A + consonant SOUND | A university |
| 16 | Between you and **me** (object form) | Between you and me |
| 17 | No double comparatives | Taller (not more taller) |
| 18 | Congratulate **on** | Congratulate on success |
| 19 | Indirect speech: will → would | He said he would come |
| 20 | Parallel structure in lists | Dancing, singing, reading |
