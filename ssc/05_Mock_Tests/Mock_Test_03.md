- **Q3:** Subjunctive mood in hypothetical conditionals takes *were* $\rightarrow$ *If I were the Prime Minister*.
- **Q15:** A collective noun acting with one unanimous mind takes a singular verb $\rightarrow$ *The committee has reached...*.
- **Q18:** Phrases with *along with, together with, as well as* take the verb according to the first subject (*The principal* is singular $\rightarrow$ *is*).
- **Q28:** Letter shift $+2$: $H(+2)=J$, $O(+2)=Q$, $R(+2)=T$, $S(+2)=U$, $E(+2)=G \rightarrow$ **JQTUG**.
- **Q29:** Prime squares: $2^2=4, 3^2=9, 5^2=25, 7^2=49, 11^2=121, 13^2=169 \rightarrow 17^2=289$.
- **Q30:** 39 is composite ($3 \times 13$), while 17, 23, 31 are prime numbers.
- **Q31:** $P \times Q \rightarrow P$ is brother of $Q$; $Q + R \rightarrow Q$ is son of $R$; $R - S \rightarrow R$ is wife of $S$ (so $S$ is father). Thus $P$ is the son of $S$.
- **Q32:** Net angle $= +90^\circ - 135^\circ + 90^\circ = +45^\circ$ clockwise from North $\rightarrow$ **North-East**.
- **Q35:** $36 \div 6 \times 3 + 5 = 6 \times 3 + 5 = 18 + 5 = 23$.
- **Q36:** Adjacent faces to 3 are 1, 2, 5, 6. The only remaining face is **4**, so 4 is opposite to 3.
- **Q38:** $64 \div 4 \times 8 + 16 - 24 = 16 \times 8 + 16 - 24 = 128 + 16 - 24 = 120$.
- **Q39:** Rank from right $= 30 - 14 + 1 = 17\text{th}$.
- **Q40:** In 100 years, ordinary century years are not leap unless divisible by 400. In a 100-year span (like 1901–2000), if non-leap century: 24; normal centuries have **24** leap years.
- **Q41:** Angle $= |30(10) - \frac{11}{2}(25)| = |300 - 137.5| = 162.5^\circ$. Reflex angle $= 360^\circ - 162.5^\circ = 197.5^\circ$.
- **Q46:** $3^2 + 2^2 + 1^2 = 9 + 4 + 1 = 14$; $4^2 + 3^2 + 2^2 = 16 + 9 + 4 = 29$; $5^2 + 4^2 + 3^2 = 25 + 16 + 9 = 50$.
- **Q47:** Students between $= 50 - (12 + 15) = 50 - 27 = 23$.
- **Q48:** A 5-pointed star can be drawn with **5** intersecting straight lines.
- **Q50:** Mirror time $= 11:60 - 8:35 = 3:25$.
- **Q51:** Cyclicity of 7 is 4: $95 \pmod 4 = 3 \Rightarrow 7^3 = 343 \rightarrow 3$. Cyclicity of 3 is 4: $58 \pmod 4 = 2 \Rightarrow 3^2 = 9$. Unit digit $= 13 - 9 = 4$.
- **Q52:** Majority $= 58\% - 42\% = 16\%$. $16\% \text{ of } V = 1600 \Rightarrow V = 10,000$.
- **Q53:** Let CP of 1 L milk = ₹1. He sells mixture at ₹1/L and gains $25\% = 1/4$. Water to milk ratio $= 1 : 4$. Water percentage in mixture $= \frac{1}{1+4} \times 100 = 20\%$.
- **Q54:** Profit $\% = \frac{1000 - 900}{900} \times 100 = \frac{100}{900} \times 100 = 11\frac{1}{9}\%$.
- **Q55:** $\text{CP of mixture} = \frac{68.20}{1.10} = ₹62$. By alligation: $(65 - 62) : (62 - 60) = 3 : 2$.
- **Q56:** $R = 5\%$ per half-year, $n = 3$ periods. $\text{Amount} = 10000 \times (1.05)^3 = 10000 \times 1.157625 = ₹11,576.25 \Rightarrow \text{CI} = ₹1,576.25$.
- **Q57:** In 2 days, A and B do $2 \times (\frac{1}{15} + \frac{1}{10}) = 2 \times \frac{5}{30} = \frac{1}{3}$ of work. Remaining $= \frac{2}{3}$. Time for A $= \frac{2/3}{1/15} = 10\text{ days}$.
- **Q58:** 1 day's work of C $= \frac{1}{3} - (\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{8}) = \frac{1}{3} - \frac{7}{24} = \frac{1}{24}$. Ratio of work $A : B : C = \frac{1}{6} : \frac{1}{8} : \frac{1}{24} = 4 : 3 : 1$. C's share $= \frac{1}{8} \times 600 = ₹75$.
- **Q59:** When A runs $100\text{ m}$, B runs $90\text{ m}$, C runs $81\text{ m}$. In $90\text{ m}$, B beats C by $9\text{ m}$. In $180\text{ m}$, B beats C by $\frac{9}{90} \times 180 = 18\text{ m}$.
- **Q60:** $(10 - 2)^2 + (y - (-3))^2 = 10^2 \Rightarrow 64 + (y+3)^2 = 100 \Rightarrow (y+3)^2 = 36 \Rightarrow y+3 = \pm 6 \Rightarrow y = 3$ or $-9$.

- **Q62:** $a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc = (a+b+c)[(a+b+c)^2 - 3(ab+bc+ca)] = 6[36 - 3(11)] = 6[36 - 33] = 6 \times 3 = 18$.
