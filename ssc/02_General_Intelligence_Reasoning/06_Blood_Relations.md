## Introduction

Blood Relations test your ability to understand **family relationships**. You're given a statement describing how people are related, and you must determine a specific relationship.

**Weightage in SSC CHSL Tier 1:** 2-3 questions

## Types of Blood Relation Questions

### Type 1: Direct Statement Questions
```
Q: A is the father of B. B is the sister of C. How is A related to C?

Solution:
A (father) → B (daughter)
B (sister) → C (C is B's sibling)
So A is C's father (if C is male/female, A is still father)
Answer: Father
```

### Type 2: Chain Relationship Questions
```
Q: A is the son of B. B is the sister of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?

Solution (draw family tree):
C (father) ──── B (C's sister, so B is D's aunt)
    |                  |
    D                  A (B's son)

C is B's brother → B is C's sister
B is A's mother → A is B's son
C is D's father → D is C's child
A and D are cousins (A is B's son, D is C's child, B and C are siblings)
Answer: Cousin
```

### Type 3: Puzzle/Riddle-Based Questions
```
Q: Pointing to a photograph, A said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the person in the photo related to A?

Solution:
Grandfather's only son = A's father
Father's daughter = A's sister
Answer: Sister
```

### Type 4: Coded Relationship Questions
```
Q: If A + B means A is the father of B
     A - B means A is the mother of B
     A × B means A is the brother of B
     A ÷ B means A is the sister of B

Then what does P + Q × R mean?
P is the father of Q, Q is the brother of R
So P is R's father too (Q and R are siblings)
Answer: P is R's father
```

## Solving Techniques

### Technique 1: Family Tree Method (BEST Method)

**Always draw a family tree!**

```
Conventions:
□ = Male
○ = Female
── = Marriage/Spouse
| = Parent-Child
- - - = Sibling

Example: A is the husband of B. C is the son of A. D is the daughter of B. E is the brother of C.

        □A ──── ○B
        |       |
    □C  □E    ○D
    
(C, E are sons; D is daughter; all children of A and B)
```

### Technique 2: Generation Analysis

| Generation | Relationship |
|-----------|-------------|
| +2 | Grandfather, Grandmother |
| +1 | Father, Mother, Uncle, Aunt |
| 0 | Self, Brother, Sister, Cousin, Spouse |
| -1 | Son, Daughter, Nephew, Niece |
| -2 | Grandson, Granddaughter |

```
Count generations:
"Father's mother" = +1 (father) + +1 (mother of father) = +2 = Grandmother
"Son's wife" = -1 (son) + 0 (wife) = -1 = Daughter-in-law
"Brother's daughter" = 0 (brother) + -1 (daughter) = -1 = Niece
```

### Technique 3: Key Phrase Identification

| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| "Only son" | He has no brothers |
| "Only daughter" | She has no sisters |
| "Only child" | No siblings at all |
| "Only son of my father" | Referring to himself |
| "Only daughter of my mother" | Referring to herself |
| "My father's only son's son" | My son (if I'm male) |
| "My mother's daughter" | My sister (or myself if female) |
| "Grandfather's only son" | Father |
| "Son of my father" | Brother (or self) |

| $ | Husband of |
| # | Wife of |

### Solving Coded Questions

```
Example: A + B - C × D
Step 1: A + B → A is father of B
Step 2: B - C → B is mother of C
Step 3: C × D → C is brother of D

Family tree:
    A (father) ─── B (mother)
         |
    C (son) ─── D (C's sibling)

So A is D's father, B is D's mother
```

## Important Concepts

### 1. "Pointing to a photograph" Questions
```
These are the trickiest! Pay attention to:
- Who is pointing?
- What gender is the person in the photo?
- Follow the relationship chain carefully

Q: Pointing to a man in a photograph, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the woman related to the man?

Solution:
"Only daughter of my mother" = the woman herself
"His mother" = the woman
So the woman is the man's mother.
Answer: Mother
```

### 2. "Only" Keyword Questions
```
Q: "He is the son of my father's only brother."
"My father's only brother" = My uncle (one uncle only)
"Son of my uncle" = My cousin
Answer: Cousin

Q: "She is the wife of my father's only son."
"My father's only son" = Myself
"Wife of myself" = My wife
Answer: Wife
```

### 3. Spouse Relationships
```
Remember:
- Husband's father = Father-in-law
- Wife's mother = Mother-in-law
- Spouse's sibling = Brother-in-law / Sister-in-law
- Sibling's spouse = Brother-in-law / Sister-in-law (same term)
```

## Tips and Tricks

### ⚡ Quick-Solving Strategy
1. **ALWAYS draw a family tree** — don't try to solve mentally
2. **Use symbols** — □ for male, ○ for female
3. **Go step by step** — don't skip relationships
4. **Watch for "only"** — it simplifies the chain
5. **Count generations** — helps identify the relationship type

### 🎯 Common Traps
1. **Gender confusion** — "cousin" can be male or female
2. **"Only son/daughter"** — critical clue, don't ignore
3. **Self-reference** — "my father's son" can mean "myself"
4. **In-law confusion** — carefully distinguish blood vs. marriage relations
5. **Two possible answers** — if gender is not specified

### 🧠 Memory Aid: Relationship Chains
```
Father's father = Grandfather (+2)
Mother's father = Grandfather (+2)
Father's mother = Grandmother (+2)
Mother's mother = Grandmother (+2)

Father's brother = Uncle (+1)
Father's sister = Aunt (+1)
Mother's brother = Uncle (+1)
Mother's sister = Aunt (+1)

Brother's son = Nephew (-1)
Sister's son = Nephew (-1)
Brother's daughter = Niece (-1)
Sister's daughter = Niece (-1)
```

**Q.** Pointing to a woman, Ram said, "She is the daughter of my father's only son." How is the woman related to Ram?
- "Father's only son" = Ram himself; "Daughter of Ram" = Ram's daughter
- Answer: **Daughter** ✅

### PYQ 3 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** If A + B means A is the mother of B, A - B means A is the brother of B, then P + Q - R means?
- P is mother of Q; Q is brother of R → P is mother of R
- Answer: **P is the mother of R** ✅

### PYQ 4 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** A is the son of B. C is the daughter of B. D is the brother of B. How is A related to D?
- B is parent of A and C; D is B's brother (uncle of A)
- Answer: **Nephew** ✅ (A is D's nephew)

### PYQ 5 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** Pointing to a boy, Sita said, "He is the son of my husband's father's only son." How is the boy related to Sita?
- Husband's father = father-in-law
- Father-in-law's only son = Sita's husband
- Son of Sita's husband = Sita's son
- Answer: **Son** ✅

### PYQ 6 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** A is the brother of B. C is the daughter of A. D is the husband of B. How is D related to C?
- A is brother of B → B is A's sibling
- C is A's daughter → C is B's niece
- D is B's husband → D is C's uncle (by marriage)
- Answer: **Uncle** ✅

### PYQ 7 — SSC CHSL 2021
**Q.** If P is the brother of Q, Q is the sister of R, and R is the father of S, how is P related to S?
- P is brother of Q; Q is sister of R → P, Q, R are siblings
- R is father of S → P is S's uncle
- Answer: **Uncle** ✅

### PYQ 8 — SSC CHSL 2021
**Q.** A's mother is the sister of B. B has a daughter C. How is A related to C?
- A's mother is B's sister → A's mother and B are siblings
- C is B's daughter → A and C are cousins
- Answer: **Cousin** ✅

### PYQ 9 — SSC CHSL 2020
**Q.** Pointing to a man, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my father." How is the woman related to the man?
- "Only daughter of my father" = the woman herself
- "His mother" = the woman → Woman is the man's mother
- Answer: **Mother** ✅

### PYQ 10 — SSC CHSL 2020
**Q.** B is the father of C and D. A is the mother of C. E is the husband of A. How is E related to D?
- A is mother of C; E is husband of A → E is father of C
- But B is father of C and D → E = B (same person)
- So E is father of D
- Answer: **Father** ✅

### PYQ 11 — SSC CHSL 2019
**Q.** If A is the son of B, and B is the sister of C, and C is the father of D, what is A's relationship with D?
- B and C are siblings; A is B's son; D is C's child
- A and D are cousins
- Answer: **Cousin** ✅

### PYQ 12 — SSC CHSL 2019
**Q.** M is the father of N. N is the son of O. O is the sister of P. How is P related to M?
- N is son of both M and O → M and O are married
- O is sister of P → P is O's sibling
- P is M's sister-in-law (or brother-in-law)
- Answer: **Brother-in-law or Sister-in-law** ✅

### PYQ 13 — SSC CHSL 2018
**Q.** A man said to a woman, "Your mother's husband's sister is my aunt." How is the man related to the woman?
- Woman's mother's husband = woman's father
- Father's sister = woman's aunt (paternal)
- This aunt is also the man's aunt → man and woman share the same aunt
- They are **cousins** or **siblings** (brother)
- Answer: **Brother** (most likely) ✅

### PYQ 14 — SSC CHSL 2018
**Q.** Pointing to a girl, Ravi said, "She is the daughter of the only child of my grandmother." How is the girl related to Ravi?
- Grandmother's only child = Ravi's parent (mother or father)
- Parent's daughter = Ravi's sister
- Answer: **Sister** ✅

### PYQ 15 — SSC CHSL 2017
**Q.** A is the father of B and C. B is the mother of D. E is the husband of A's daughter. How is E related to A?
- A's daughter = C (or B, but B is mother of D, so B is female)
- A's daughters: B and/or C
- E is husband of A's daughter → E is A's son-in-law
- Answer: **Son-in-law** ✅

### PYQ 16 — SSC CHSL 2017
**Q.** If X is the brother of Y, Y is the sister of Z, and Z is the mother of W, how is X related to W?
- X, Y, Z are siblings (brothers/sisters)
- Z is mother of W → X is W's uncle (or aunt)
- Since X is brother → X is W's uncle
- Answer: **Uncle** ✅

### PYQ 17 — SSC CHSL 2016
**Q.** P is the father of Q and R. S is the mother of R. T is the husband of P's daughter. How is T related to S?
- P is father, S is mother of R → P and S may or may not be married
- P's daughter: Q or R (let's say Q is daughter)
- T is Q's husband → T is S's son-in-law (if S is Q's mother too)
- Answer: **Son-in-law** ✅

### PYQ 18 — SSC CHSL 2016
**Q.** A man pointing to a woman says, "She is the sister of my father's only son." Who is the woman?
- Father's only son = the man himself
- Sister of the man = his sister
- Answer: **Sister** ✅

### PYQ 19 — SSC CHSL 2015
**Q.** How is your mother's only brother's only sister related to you?
- Mother's only brother = maternal uncle (mama)
- Maternal uncle's only sister = my mother
- Answer: **Mother** ✅

### PYQ 20 — SSC CHSL 2015
**Q.** If A is the brother of B, B is the daughter of C, and C is the husband of D, how is A related to D?
- C is husband of D → C (male) and D are married
- B is daughter of C → B is child of C and D
- A is brother of B → A is also child of C and D
- Answer: **Son** ✅ (A is D's son)
