### Q004 · Direction and Distance

A person walks 10 km east and then 8 km north. What is the straight-line distance from the starting point?

A. 18 km
B. 12.81 km
C. 80.00 km
D. 2 km

**Answer:** B. 12.81 km
**Explanation:** The route forms a right triangle: distance = √(10² + 8²) = 12.81 km.
**Shortcut / rule:** For perpendicular movements, use the Pythagorean theorem; do not add the route lengths.
