## Introduction

Odd One Out is essentially a **classification** question presented differently. Instead of grouping items, you identify which one doesn't belong. It's closely related to Similarities and Differences but specifically focuses on finding the outlier.

**Weightage in SSC CHSL Tier 1:** 2-3 questions

## Number-Based Odd One Out

### Pattern 1: Prime vs Composite
```
Q: 2, 3, 5, 9, 11, 13
Answer: 9 (composite: 3×3; others are prime)

Q: 4, 6, 8, 11, 14, 16
Answer: 11 (prime; others are composite)
```

### Pattern 2: Even vs Odd
```
Q: 12, 24, 36, 45, 48
Answer: 45 (odd; others are even)

Q: 3, 7, 11, 14, 17
Answer: 14 (even; others are odd)
```

### Pattern 3: Perfect Squares
```
Q: 4, 9, 16, 25, 32, 36
Answer: 32 (not a perfect square; others are 2², 3², 4², 5², 6²)

Q: 1, 8, 27, 64, 100, 125
Answer: 100 (10² = perfect square; others are cubes)
```

### Pattern 4: Divisibility Patterns
```
Q: 6, 12, 18, 21, 24, 30
Answer: 21 (not divisible by 6; others are multiples of 6)

Q: 5, 15, 25, 30, 35, 45
Answer: 30 (not divisible by 5... wait, 30÷5=6, it IS divisible)
Check: all are multiples of 5 → look for another pattern
Maybe: 5, 15, 25, 35, 45 are 5×odd → 30 = 5×6 (5×even)
Answer: 30 (5 × even number; others are 5 × odd numbers)
```

### Pattern 5: Number Relationships
```
Q: 8-27, 64-125, 216-343, 400-512
8=2³, 27=3³, 64=4³, 125=5³, 216=6³, 343=7³, 400=20², 512=8³
Answer: 400-512 (400 is not a perfect cube; all others are consecutive cubes)

Q: 1-1, 4-8, 9-27, 16-65, 25-125
Pairs should be n²-n³: 1-1, 4-8(2²-2³), 9-27(3²-3³), 16-64(4²-4³), 25-125(5²-5³)
Answer: 16-65 (should be 16-64)
```

### Pattern 6: Sum of Digits
```
Q: 11, 22, 33, 44, 54, 66
Sum of digits: 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12
Pattern: All should have digit sum that's even OR all same digit repeated
11, 22, 33, 44, 66 → repeated digits; 54 doesn't have repeated digits
Answer: 54
```

## Letter-Based Odd One Out

### Pattern 1: Vowels vs Consonants
```
Q: A, E, I, O, B
Answer: B (consonant; others are vowels)
```

### Pattern 2: Position Patterns
```
Q: BD, FH, JL, MO, PR
Gaps: B-D(2), F-H(2), J-L(2), M-O(2), P-R(2)
Starting positions: 2, 6, 10, 13, 16
Gaps between starts: 4, 4, 3, 3
Answer: MO (should start at 14=N, not 13=M) or check another pattern
```

### Pattern 3: Symmetry
```
Q: A, H, M, O, P, T
Vertically symmetrical: A, H, M, O, T ✅; P ✗
Answer: P (not vertically symmetrical)
```

## Tips and Tricks

### ⚡ Quick-Solving Steps
1. **For numbers:** Check prime/composite, even/odd, squares/cubes, divisibility
2. **For words:** Identify the category (fruits, animals, etc.) and find the outlier
3. **For letters:** Check position values, vowel/consonant, symmetry

### 🎯 Most Common Patterns in SSC
1. **Biological classification** (mammal among birds, etc.)
2. **Prime number identification** (composite among primes)
3. **Perfect squares/cubes** (non-square among squares)
4. **Category mismatch** (city among countries, color among shapes)
5. **Measurement units** (weight unit among length units)
