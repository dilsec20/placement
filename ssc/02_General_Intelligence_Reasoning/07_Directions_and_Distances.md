## Introduction

Direction and Distance problems test your ability to track a person's movement through various directions and determine their final position, direction, or distance from the starting point.

**Weightage in SSC CHSL Tier 1:** 2-3 questions

## Direction Basics

### The Direction Map (ALWAYS Draw This!)
```
                N (North)
                ↑
                |
W (West) ← ―――― + ―――― → E (East)
                |
                ↓
                S (South)
```

### Eight Directions
```
                N
            NW  |  NE
              \ | /
    W ―――――――― + ―――――――― E
              / | \
            SW  |  SE
                S
```

### Turn Rules
| Turn | Result |
|------|--------|
| Face North, turn Right | Facing East |
| Face North, turn Left | Facing West |
| Face East, turn Right | Facing South |
| Face East, turn Left | Facing North |
| Face South, turn Right | Facing West |
| Face South, turn Left | Facing East |
| Face West, turn Right | Facing North |
| Face West, turn Left | Facing South |

### Clockwise Rotation (Right Turn)
```
N → E → S → W → N (90° each)
Each right turn = 90° clockwise
```

### Anti-Clockwise Rotation (Left Turn)
```
N → W → S → E → N (90° each)
Each left turn = 90° anti-clockwise
```

### Degree Turns
| Degree | Result from North |
|--------|------------------|
| 90° clockwise | East |
| 180° | South (opposite) |
| 270° clockwise | West |
| 360° | North (full circle) |
| 45° clockwise | North-East |
| 135° clockwise | South-East |
| 225° clockwise | South-West |
| 315° clockwise | North-West |

### Opposite Directions
```
North ↔ South
East ↔ West
North-East ↔ South-West
North-West ↔ South-East
```

## Direction Sense Test

### Type 1: Finding Final Direction

**Example:**
> A person faces North. He turns 90° clockwise, then 180°, then 90° anti-clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?

```
Start: North
90° clockwise: East
180°: West (opposite of East)
90° anti-clockwise: South
Answer: South
```

### Type 2: Following a Path

**Example:**
> Ram starts from point A, walks 5 km North, then turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right and walks 5 km. How far is he from point A?

```
Draw the path:
Start A → 5 km N → turn right (East) → 3 km E → turn right (South) → 5 km S

      B ―――3km――→ C
      ↑              |
    5km            5km
      |              ↓
      A              D

A to D = 3 km (East)
He walked North 5km, East 3km, South 5km.
Net N-S displacement = 5 - 5 = 0
Net E-W displacement = 3 km East
Answer: 3 km East of starting point
```

## Distance Problems

### Pythagorean Theorem for Direction Problems
When a person walks in two perpendicular directions, the shortest distance is calculated using:

**Distance = √(horizontal² + vertical²)**

```
Common Pythagorean triplets:
3-4-5    (3²+4²=5²)
5-12-13  (5²+12²=13²)
6-8-10   (6²+8²=10²)
7-24-25  (7²+24²=25²)
8-15-17  (8²+15²=17²)
9-12-15  (9²+12²=15²)
```

### Example:
> A man walks 4 km North, then 3 km East. How far is he from the starting point?

```
      B ―――3km――→ C
      ↑
    4km
      |
      A

AC = √(4² + 3²) = √(16 + 9) = √25 = 5 km
Answer: 5 km
Direction: North-East from A
```

### Example with Multiple Turns:
> Starting from point O, a man walks 6 km North, turns right and walks 8 km, turns right and walks 3 km. How far is he from point O?

```
      B ――――8km―――→ C
      ↑                |
    6km              3km
      |                ↓
      O                D

Net N-S: 6 - 3 = 3 km North
Net E-W: 8 km East

Distance = √(3² + 8²) = √(9 + 64) = √73 ≈ 8.54 km
```

## Shadow-Based Questions

### Sun Position Rules
| Time | Sun Position | Shadow Direction |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Morning/Sunrise** (6 AM) | East | West |
| **Afternoon** (12 PM) | South (in Northern Hemisphere) | North |
| **Evening/Sunset** (6 PM) | West | East |

### Shadow Rules:
```
Shadow falls OPPOSITE to the Sun:
- Sun in East → Shadow in West
- Sun in West → Shadow in East
- Sun in South → Shadow in North

Morning (Sun rises in East):
- Face East → Shadow behind you (West)
- Face North → Shadow on your LEFT (West)
- Face South → Shadow on your RIGHT (West)

Evening (Sun sets in West):
- Face West → Shadow behind you (East)
- Face North → Shadow on your RIGHT (East)
- Face South → Shadow on your LEFT (East)
```

### Example:
> At 8 AM, a man is facing East. Where is his shadow?

```
Morning → Sun is in the East
If facing East, Sun is in front → Shadow is behind him → West
Answer: Behind him (West side)

But wait: if facing East, shadow is behind = West
His shadow falls to the WEST
```

### Example:
> At 5 PM, Ram is facing North. His shadow falls to his right. Which direction is he facing?

```
Evening → Sun is in West → Shadow falls to East
If shadow is to his right, East is to his right.
When East is to your right → You face North ✅
Answer: North (confirmed)
```

## Complex Direction Problems

### Type: Starting Point to Ending Point
```
Q: A man walks 10 km South, then turns left and walks 6 km, then turns left again and walks 10 km. In which direction and how far is he from the starting point?

Solution:
Start A → 10 km South → B
B → Turn left (from South, left = East) → 6 km East → C
C → Turn left (from East, left = North) → 10 km North → D

    A ―――――――――――――――― D
    |                    |
  10km                10km
    |                    |
    B ――――6km――――→ C

Net N-S: 10S + 10N = 0 (same level)
Net E-W: 6 km East
Distance: 6 km
Direction: East

Answer: 6 km East of starting point
```

## Tips and Tricks

### ⚡ Quick-Solving Strategy
1. **ALWAYS draw a diagram** — mark N, S, E, W
2. **Mark the starting point** and trace the path
3. **Right turn from North = East**, Right from East = South, etc.
4. **Calculate net displacement** separately for N-S and E-W
5. **Use Pythagorean theorem** for shortest distance
6. **For shadow questions**, determine Sun's position first

### 🎯 Common Traps
1. **Confusing left and right** — depends on which direction you're facing!
2. **Forgetting the starting direction** — always track current facing direction
3. **Shadow direction** — opposite to sun, not same
4. **Northeast ≠ North or East** — it's 45° between them
5. **Right from South = West** (not East!)

### 🧠 Memory Trick: Right Turns
```
"NEWS" clockwise: N → E → W → S? NO!
Correct clockwise: N → E → S → W

Remember: "NESW" = Never Eat Soggy Waffles
Clockwise from North: N → E → S → W
Right turn always goes clockwise one step
Left turn always goes anti-clockwise one step
```
