## Highest Civilian Awards of India

### 1. Bharat Ratna
- **Instituted on:** **January 2, 1954** by Dr. Rajendra Prasad.
- **Highest Civilian Award** of the Republic of India; awarded for exceptional service/performance of highest order in any field.
- **Design:** Peepal leaf shaped medallion made of bronze with Sun embossed in platinum and words *"Bharat Ratna"* engraved in Devanagari script; reverse features State Emblem and motto *"Satyameva Jayate"*.
- **No monetary grant** is associated with Bharat Ratna.
- **First Recipients (1954):**
  1. **Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan** (Philosopher, 1st Vice-President of India)
  2. **C. Rajagopalachari** (Last Governor-General of India)
  3. **Dr. C.V. Raman** (Nobel Laureate in Physics)
- **First Foreigner Recipient:** **Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan ("Frontier Gandhi")** (1987, Pakistan).
- **Second Foreigner Recipient:** **Nelson Mandela** (1990, South Africa).
- **Naturalized Indian Recipient:** Mother Teresa (1980).
- **First Posthumous Recipient:** **Lal Bahadur Shastri** (1966).
- **First & Youngest Sportsman:** **Sachin Tendulkar** (2014, at age 40).
- **First Musician:** **M.S. Subbulakshmi** (1998).
- **Notable 2024 Conferments:** Karpoori Thakur, L.K. Advani, P.V. Narasimha Rao, Chaudhary Charan Singh, M.S. Swaminathan.

### 2. Padma Awards (Announced annually on Republic Day Eve)
- **Padma Vibhushan:** Second-highest civilian award (exceptional and distinguished service).
- **Padma Bhushan:** Third-highest civilian award (distinguished service of high order).
- **Padma Shri:** Fourth-highest civilian award (distinguished service).

## Gallantry & Defence Awards

### Wartime Gallantry Awards
1. **Param Vir Chakra (PVC):** Highest wartime military decoration for conspicuous bravery in the presence of the enemy.
   - *First Recipient:* **Major Somnath Sharma** (posthumously, 1947 Indo-Pak War).
   - *Youngest Recipient:* Yogendra Singh Yadav (Kargil War 1999, age 19).
2. **Maha Vir Chakra (MVC):** Second highest wartime decoration.
3. **Vir Chakra (VrC):** Third highest wartime decoration.

### Peacetime Gallantry Awards
1. **Ashoka Chakra:** Highest peacetime gallantry award (equivalent to PVC). First recipients (1952): Havildar Bachittar Singh and Naik Narbahadur Thapa.
2. **Kirti Chakra:** Second highest peacetime gallantry award.
3. **Shaurya Chakra:** Third highest peacetime gallantry award.

## Cinema & Entertainment Honours

### Dadasaheb Phalke Award
- **Highest award in Indian Cinema**, presented annually at the National Film Awards by Directorate of Film Festivals.
- **Instituted in:** **1969** (commemorating Dadasaheb Phalke, who directed India's first full-length feature film *Raja Harishchandra* in 1913).
- **First Recipient:** **Devika Rani** (1969, the "First Lady of Indian Cinema").
- **Recent Winners:** Rajinikanth (51st), Asha Parekh (52nd), Waheeda Rehman (53rd), Mithun Chakraborty (54th).

## National Sports Awards

- **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award:** Highest sporting honour in India (earlier Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna; renamed in 2021). Cash prize: ₹25 Lakh. First recipient: **Viswanathan Anand** (1991–92, Chess). First woman: Karnam Malleswari (1994–95).
- **Arjuna Award:** Instituted 1961; awarded for consistent outstanding performance for 4 years. Cash prize: ₹15 Lakh.
- **Dronacharya Award:** Instituted 1985; given to **sports coaches** (Regular category: ₹10 Lakh; Lifetime: ₹15 Lakh). First recipients (1985): O.M. Nambiar (Athletics), Om Prakash Bhardwaj (Boxing), Bhalchandra Bhaskar Bhagwat (Wrestling).
- **Maulana Abul Kalam Azad (MAKA) Trophy:** Running trophy awarded to top performing university in inter-university tournaments.

## International Prestigious Awards

### Nobel Prize
- Instituted from bequest of Swedish inventor **Alfred Nobel** (inventor of dynamite); first awarded in **1901**.
- Six fields: **Physics, Chemistry, Physiology or Medicine, Literature, Peace, and Economic Sciences** (Economics added in 1968 by Sveriges Riksbank).
- Awarded in **Stockholm, Sweden**, except the **Nobel Peace Prize**, which is awarded in **Oslo, Norway**.

#### Indian & Indian-Origin Nobel Laureates:
| Laureate | Year | Category | Contribution / Field |
|----------|------|----------|----------------------|
| **Rabindranath Tagore** | **1913** | **Literature** | *Gitanjali* (**First Asian to win a Nobel Prize**) |
| **Sir C.V. Raman** | **1930** | **Physics** | Raman Scattering Effect of light (Feb 28 = National Science Day) |
| **Dr. Har Gobind Khorana** | 1968 | Medicine | Genetic code interpretation and protein synthesis |
| **Mother Teresa** | 1979 | Peace | Humanitarian work with Missionaries of Charity |
| **Dr. Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar**| 1983 | Physics | Chandrasekhar Limit ($1.44 M_\odot$) & star evolution |
| **Dr. Amartya Sen** | 1998 | Economic Sciences| Welfare economics and poverty/famine mechanisms |
| **Venkatraman Ramakrishnan** | 2009 | Chemistry | Structure and function of the ribosome |
| **Kailash Satyarthi** | 2014 | Peace | Struggle against suppression of children (*Bachpan Bachao Andolan*) |
| **Abhijit Banerjee** | 2019 | Economic Sciences| Experimental approach to alleviating global poverty |

### Ramon Magsaysay Award
- Known as the **"Nobel Prize of Asia"**; established 1957 in memory of 7th President of the Philippines.
- First Indian recipient: **Acharya Vinoba Bhave** (1958, Community Leadership / Bhoodan Movement).

## Last 10 Years PYQs with Detailed Explanations

### PYQ 1 — SSC CHSL 2024
**Q.** Who among the following was the first recipient of the prestigious Jnanpith Award?
- A) Ashapoorna Devi
- B) G. Sankara Kurup
- C) Umashankar Joshi
- D) Sumitranandan Pant
- **Answer:** **B) G. Sankara Kurup** ✅
- *Explanation:* Malayalam poet G. Sankara Kurup won the first Jnanpith Award in 1965 for his poetry collection *Odakkuzhal* (The Bamboo Flute).

### PYQ 2 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** Who was the first Indian woman to win the Booker Prize for Fiction?
- A) Kiran Desai
- B) Anita Desai
- C) Arundhati Roy
- D) Jhumpa Lahiri
- **Answer:** **C) Arundhati Roy** ✅
- *Explanation:* Arundhati Roy won the 1997 Booker Prize for her debut novel *The God of Small Things*.

### PYQ 3 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** Who wrote the historic treatise "Arthashastra"?
- A) Megasthenes
- B) Chanakya (Kautilya)
- C) Patanjali
- D) Banabhatta
- **Answer:** **B) Chanakya (Kautilya)** ✅
- *Explanation:* Chanakya wrote the *Arthashastra*, an ancient Sanskrit treatise on statecraft, economic policy, and military strategy.

### PYQ 4 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** "Playing It My Way" is the autobiography of which legendary cricketer?
- A) Kapil Dev
- B) Sourav Ganguly
- C) Sachin Tendulkar
- D) Rahul Dravid
- **Answer:** **C) Sachin Tendulkar** ✅
- *Explanation:* Published in November 2014, written by Sachin Tendulkar with sports journalist Boria Majumdar.

### PYQ 5 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** Who was the first Indian to be awarded the Nobel Prize?
- A) Sir C.V. Raman
- B) Rabindranath Tagore
- C) Mother Teresa
- D) Dr. Amartya Sen
- **Answer:** **B) Rabindranath Tagore** ✅
- *Explanation:* Rabindranath Tagore won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1913 for *Gitanjali*, becoming the first non-European / Asian laureate.

### PYQ 6 — SSC CHSL 2021
**Q.** The famous historical book "Rajatarangini" detailing the history of Kashmir was authored by:
- A) Kalidasa
- B) Kalhana
- C) Bilhana
- D) Banabhatta
- **Answer:** **B) Kalhana** ✅
- *Explanation:* Kalhana composed *Rajatarangini* ("River of Kings") in Sanskrit verse during 1148–1149 CE.

### PYQ 7 — SSC CHSL 2020
**Q.** Who was the first recipient of the Dadasaheb Phalke Award in 1969?
- A) Prithviraj Kapoor
- B) Devika Rani
- C) Birendranath Sircar
- D) Satyajit Ray
- **Answer:** **B) Devika Rani** ✅
- *Explanation:* Devika Rani Roerich received the inaugural Dadasaheb Phalke Award for her immense pioneering contribution to Indian cinema.

### PYQ 8 — SSC CHSL 2019
**Q.** Who is the author of the book "Poverty and Un-British Rule in India"?
- A) Gopal Krishna Gokhale
- B) Dadabhai Naoroji
- C) Romesh Chunder Dutt
- D) Bal Gangadhar Tilak
- **Answer:** **B) Dadabhai Naoroji** ✅
- *Explanation:* Published in 1901 by the "Grand Old Man of India", exposing how British colonialism systematically drained Indian wealth to Britain.

### PYQ 9 — SSC CHSL 2018
**Q.** Who among the following is the first individual Olympic gold medalist of independent India?
- A) K.D. Jadhav
- B) Abhinav Bindra
- C) Neeraj Chopra
- D) Sushil Kumar
- **Answer:** **B) Abhinav Bindra** ✅
- *Explanation:* Abhinav Bindra won gold in the Men's 10m Air Rifle event at the 2008 Beijing Olympics. Neeraj Chopra won in 2020 Tokyo (Javelin).

### PYQ 10 — SSC CHSL 2017
**Q.** The song "Vande Mataram" is taken from which famous Bengali novel?
- A) Durgeshnandini
- B) Anandmath
- C) Kapalkundala
- D) Devi Chaudhurani
- **Answer:** **B) Anandmath** ✅
- *Explanation:* Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay wrote *Anandmath* in 1882 set during the Sanyasi Rebellion; "Vande Mataram" was adopted as India's National Song.
