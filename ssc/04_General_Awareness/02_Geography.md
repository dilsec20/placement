## Physical Geography

### Solar System
```
Sun → Mercury → Venus → Earth → Mars → Jupiter → Saturn → Uranus → Neptune
Mnemonic: "My Very Educated Mother Just Served Us Nachos"

Key Facts:
- Largest planet: Jupiter
- Smallest planet: Mercury
- Hottest planet: Venus (greenhouse effect)
- Coldest planet: Neptune (earlier Pluto, now dwarf planet)
- Red Planet: Mars
- Earth's twin: Venus (similar size)
- Ringed planet: Saturn
- Blue Planet: Earth
- Largest satellite: Ganymede (Jupiter's moon)
- Earth's satellite: Moon
```

### Earth Facts
```
Shape: Geoid (slightly flattened at poles)
Age: ~4.6 billion years
Diameter: 12,742 km
Circumference: 40,075 km
Rotation: 24 hours (day-night)
Revolution: 365.25 days (seasons)
Tilt: 23.5° (causes seasons)
71% water, 29% land
Layers: Crust → Mantle → Outer Core → Inner Core
```

### Atmosphere Layers
| Layer | Height | Key Feature |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Troposphere | 0-12 km | Weather; temperature decreases with height |
| Stratosphere | 12-50 km | Ozone layer; jets fly here |
| Mesosphere | 50-80 km | Meteors burn here |
| Thermosphere | 80-700 km | Aurora; ISS orbits here |
| Exosphere | 700+ km | Merges with space |

### Types of Rocks
```
Igneous: Formed from cooling magma/lava (Granite, Basalt)
Sedimentary: Formed from deposits (Sandstone, Limestone, Shale)
Metamorphic: Changed by heat/pressure (Marble, Slate, Quartzite)

Rock Cycle: Igneous → Sedimentary → Metamorphic → Igneous
```

## Indian Geography

### Physical Features
```
India: 7th largest country; 2nd most populous
Area: 3.287 million km²
Latitude: 8°4'N to 37°6'N
Longitude: 68°7'E to 97°25'E
Standard Meridian: 82°30'E (passes through Mirzapur, UP)
Coastline: ~7,516 km
Land border: ~15,200 km
Neighbours: Pakistan, Afghanistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Sri Lanka (maritime)
```

### Mountain Ranges
```
Himalayas (Young Fold Mountains):
├── Greater Himalayas (Himadri): Highest; Everest (8,849m), K2, Kangchenjunga
├── Lesser Himalayas (Himachal): Pir Panjal, Dhauladhar
└── Outer Himalayas (Shiwaliks): Lowest; Dun valleys

Other ranges:
- Aravalli: Oldest mountain range in India (Rajasthan)
- Vindhya: Divides North and South India
- Satpura: Between Narmada and Tapti rivers
- Western Ghats (Sahyadri): Higher, continuous; biodiversity hotspot
- Eastern Ghats: Lower, discontinuous
- Nilgiri Hills: Meeting point of Western & Eastern Ghats
```

```
East-flowing rivers: Ganga, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri (Bay of Bengal)
```

## World Geography

### Continents (Largest to Smallest)
```
Asia → Africa → North America → South America → Antarctica → Europe → Australia
"All Animals Need Some Altering Every Afternoon"
```

### Oceans (Largest to Smallest)
```
Pacific → Atlantic → Indian → Southern → Arctic
```

### Important Facts
```
Longest river: Nile (6,650 km) / Amazon (by water volume)
Largest lake: Caspian Sea
Deepest lake: Lake Baikal
Highest mountain: Mt. Everest (8,849 m)
Largest desert: Sahara (hot) / Antarctic (overall)
Largest island: Greenland
Smallest country: Vatican City
Largest country: Russia
Most populous: India (2023 onwards) / China
Longest wall: Great Wall of China
Deepest ocean point: Mariana Trench (Challenger Deep, ~11 km)
```
