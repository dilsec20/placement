## Making of the Constitution & Sources

### Key Milestones & Dates
```
1934 ── Idea of Constituent Assembly first proposed by M.N. Roy
1935 ── Indian National Congress (INC) officially demanded it
1940 ── August Offer accepted the demand in principle
1942 ── Cripps Mission proposed framing of Constitution after WWII (rejected)
1946 ── Cabinet Mission Plan (Lord Pethick-Lawrence, Sir Stafford Cripps, A.V. Alexander)
        Constituent Assembly constituted in November 1946
        First Meeting: December 9, 1946 (Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha as Interim President)
        December 11, 1946: Dr. Rajendra Prasad elected permanent President; H.C. Mukherjee VP
        December 13, 1946: 'Objective Resolution' moved by Jawaharlal Nehru (later became Preamble)
1947 ── Drafting Committee appointed on August 29, 1947 (Chairman: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar)
1949 ── Constitution adopted & enacted on November 26, 1949 (Constitution Day / Samvidhan Divas)
1950 ── Constitution came into full effect on January 26, 1950 (Republic Day)
```

## Preamble, Parts & Schedules

### The Preamble
```
"WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a 
SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC and to secure to all its citizens:
JUSTICE, social, economic and political;
LIBERTY of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship;
EQUALITY of status and of opportunity; and to promote among them all
FRATERNITY assuring the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the Nation..."
```
- **Nature of Indian State:** Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic
- **Key Amendment:** 42nd Amendment Act (1976) added 3 new words: **Socialist, Secular, Integrity**.
- **Preamble is part of Constitution:** Kesavananda Bharati Case (1973) held that Preamble is an integral part of the Constitution and can be amended under Article 368 without altering the "Basic Structure".

### All 12 Schedules of the Indian Constitution
> **Mnemonic: TEARS OF OLD PM**

| Schedule | Letter | Subject Matter |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| **1st** | **T** (Territory) | Names of States and Union Territories and their territorial jurisdiction |
| **2nd** | **E** (Emoluments) | Salaries, allowances, privileges of President, Governors, Judges, CAG, etc. |
| **3rd** | **A** (Affirmation/Oaths) | Forms of Oaths or Affirmations for Union ministers, MPs, Judges, CAG |
| **4th** | **R** (Rajya Sabha) | Allocation of seats in Rajya Sabha to States and UTs |
| **5th** | **S** (Scheduled Areas) | Administration and control of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes |
| **6th** | **O** (Other Tribal Areas) | Administration of tribal areas in **AMTM** (Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram) |
| **7th** | **F** (Federal Lists) | Division of powers: Union List (100 items), State List (61 items), Concurrent List (52 items) |
| **8th** | **O** (Official Languages) | **22 Recognized Languages** (Originally 14; 21st Amend: Sindhi; 71st Amend: Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali; 92nd Amend: Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santhali) |
| **9th** | **L** (Land Reforms) | Added by 1st Amendment (1951); Acts and Regulations protected from judicial review (now subject to basic structure test) |
| **10th**| **D** (Defection) | Anti-Defection Law; added by **52nd Amendment Act (1985)** |
| **11th**| **P** (Panchayats) | Added by **73rd Amendment Act (1992)**; 29 functional matters |
| **12th**| **M** (Municipalities) | Added by **74th Amendment Act (1992)**; 18 functional matters |

## Fundamental Rights, DPSP & Fundamental Duties

### Fundamental Rights (Part III, Articles 12–35)
Borrowed from the US Bill of Rights. Termed the *Magna Carta of India*.
Originally 7 rights; Right to Property (Art 31) deleted by **44th Amendment Act, 1978** and made a legal right under **Article 300A**.

```
1. Right to Equality (Articles 14–18)
   ├── Art 14: Equality before law & equal protection of laws
   ├── Art 15: Prohibition of discrimination (religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth)
   ├── Art 16: Equality of opportunity in public employment
   ├── Art 17: Abolition of Untouchability
   └── Art 18: Abolition of Titles (except military and academic)

2. Right to Freedom (Articles 19–22)
   ├── Art 19: Six freedoms (speech, assembly, association, movement, residence, profession)
   ├── Art 20: Protection in respect of conviction for offences (no ex-post facto, no double jeopardy, no self-incrimination)
   ├── Art 21: Protection of life and personal liberty (Cannot be suspended even during Emergency!)
   ├── Art 21A: Right to free & compulsory elementary education (6–14 yrs) — Added by 86th Amend (2002)
   └── Art 22: Protection against arrest and detention in certain cases

3. Right against Exploitation (Articles 23–24)
   ├── Art 23: Prohibition of human trafficking and forced labour (begar)
   └── Art 24: Prohibition of employment of children in factories, mines, hazardous work (<14 yrs)

4. Right to Freedom of Religion (Articles 25–28)
   ├── Art 25: Freedom of conscience, free profession, practice and propagation of religion
   ├── Art 26: Freedom to manage religious affairs
   ├── Art 27: Freedom from payment of taxes for promotion of any particular religion
   └── Art 28: Freedom from attending religious instruction in certain educational institutions

5. Cultural and Educational Rights (Articles 29–30)
   ├── Art 29: Protection of language, script and culture of minorities
   └── Art 30: Right of minorities to establish and administer educational institutions

6. Right to Constitutional Remedies (Article 32)
   └── Called the "Heart and Soul of the Constitution" by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
```

#### The 5 Writs (Under Article 32 for SC, Article 226 for HC)
| Writ | Meaning | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Habeas Corpus** | "To have the body of" | Protects against unlawful detention; releases illegally detained person |
| **Mandamus** | "We command" | Commands a public official/body to perform an official statutory duty |
| **Prohibition** | "To forbid" | Issued by a higher court to a lower court/tribunal to prevent exceeding jurisdiction |
| **Certiorari** | "To be certified / informed" | Quashes order of an inferior court/tribunal acting without or in excess of jurisdiction |
| **Quo-Warranto** | "By what authority / warrant" | Inquires into legality of claim of a person to a public office |

### Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV, Articles 36–51)
- Borrowed from **Ireland**; Non-justiciable (cannot be enforced by courts).
- Aim: Establish a **Welfare State** and Socio-Economic Democracy.
- **Article 39A:** Equal justice and free legal aid
- **Article 40:** Organization of Village Panchayats
- **Article 44:** **Uniform Civil Code (UCC)** for citizens
- **Article 45:** Provision for early childhood care and education below 6 years
- **Article 48A:** Protection and improvement of environment, forests and wildlife
- **Article 50:** Separation of Judiciary from Executive
- **Article 51:** Promotion of international peace and security

### Fundamental Duties (Part IV-A, Article 51A)
- Recommended by **Swaran Singh Committee**; Borrowed from **USSR**.
- Added by **42nd Amendment Act (1976)**: Originally 10 duties.
- 11th Duty added by **86th Amendment Act (2002)**: Duty of parent/guardian to provide education to child aged 6 to 14 years.
- Non-justiciable in court.

## The Union Executive & Legislature

### President of India (Articles 52–62)
- **Head of State**, First Citizen of India, Supreme Commander of Armed Forces.
- **Qualifications:** Citizen of India, minimum **35 years** of age, qualified to be member of Lok Sabha, no office of profit.
- **Electoral College (Art 54):** Elected members of Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha + State Legislative Assemblies (including Delhi & Puducherry). *(Nominated members do not vote!)*
- **Term:** 5 years. Resignation addressed to Vice-President.
- **Impeachment (Article 61):** Ground: "Violation of the Constitution". Requires 2/3rd majority of total membership of each House.
- **Pardoning Power (Article 72):** Pardon, reprieve, respite, remit or commute punishment/sentence (including death penalty and court-martial).
- **Ordinance Making Power (Article 123):** Can promulgate ordinances when Parliament is not in session. Must be approved within 6 weeks of reassembly.

### Vice-President of India (Articles 63–71)
- Ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha (Art 64).
- Minimum age: **35 years**. Elected by members of both Houses of Parliament (both elected & nominated).

### Prime Minister & Council of Ministers (Articles 74, 75, 78)
- Real executive authority (De facto executive).
- Appointed by President; must be a member of either House (or become one within 6 months).
- Council of Ministers is **collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha** (Art 75(3)).
- Size of Council of Ministers cannot exceed **15%** of total Lok Sabha strength (91st Amendment, 2003).

### Parliament of India (Articles 79–122)
Comprises **President + Rajya Sabha + Lok Sabha**.

| Parameter | Rajya Sabha (Council of States) | Lok Sabha (House of the People) |
|-----------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Nature | Permanent House (Cannot be dissolved) | Dissolved every 5 years or earlier |
| Max Strength | 250 (238 states/UTs + 12 nominated by President) | 550 (originally 552; Anglo-Indian seats removed by 104th Amend) |
| Min Age | **30 years** | **25 years** |
| Member Tenure | 6 years (1/3rd retire every 2 years) | 5 years |
| Presiding Officer | Chairman (Vice-President of India) | Speaker (elected by members) |
| Money Bill | Can only delay for maximum **14 days** | Exclusive authority; introduced only here |

#### Important Parliamentary Terms:
- **Quorum:** Minimum 1/10th (10%) of total membership required to transact business.
- **Question Hour:** First hour of parliamentary sitting (Questions: Starred = oral answer; Unstarred = written answer; Short notice).
- **Zero Hour:** Starts immediately after Question Hour (Indian innovation since 1962).
- **Joint Sitting (Article 108):** Summoned by President to resolve deadlock; **Presided over by Speaker of Lok Sabha**.
- **Money Bill (Article 110):** Decided exclusively by Lok Sabha Speaker. President's prior recommendation needed.
- **Annual Financial Statement (Budget - Article 112):** Presented in Parliament; the term "Budget" is not mentioned in Constitution.

## State Government & Local Governance

### Governor (Articles 153–162)
- Constitutional Head of State, appointed by President (holds office during pleasure of President).
- Minimum age: **35 years**.
- Executive, legislative, financial, and judicial powers (Pardoning power under Art 161; cannot pardon death sentence or court-martial).
- Ordinance power under **Article 213**.

### State Legislature
- Most states have unicameral legislature (Vidhan Sabha / Legislative Assembly).
- 6 states have bicameral legislature (Legislative Council / Vidhan Parishad): **Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana**.

### Local Self-Government
```
73rd Amendment Act, 1992 ── Panchayati Raj (Part IX, Articles 243 to 243O, 11th Schedule)
├── 3-Tier System: Gram Panchayat (village) → Panchayat Samiti (block) → Zilla Parishad (district)
├── Minimum Age to contest: 21 years
├── Tenure: 5 years
├── 1/3rd seats reserved for women
└── First established: Nagaur district, Rajasthan on October 2, 1959 (Balwant Rai Mehta Committee)

74th Amendment Act, 1992 ── Municipalities (Part IX-A, Articles 243P to 243ZG, 12th Schedule)
├── Nagar Panchayat (transitional area) → Municipal Council (smaller urban) → Municipal Corporation (large urban)
└── Tenure: 5 years; 1/3rd reservation for women
```

## Judiciary

### Supreme Court of India (Articles 124–147)
- Established: January 28, 1950. Seat: New Delhi.
- Strength: Chief Justice of India + 33 Judges = **34 Judges**.
- Retirement Age: **65 years**.
- Appointment: By President via Collegium system.
- Removal: By President after an address by Parliament supported by special majority (Art 124(4)).
- Jurisdictions:
  - **Original Jurisdiction (Art 131):** Dispute between Centre and States or between States.
  - **Writ Jurisdiction (Art 32):** Protection of Fundamental Rights.
  - **Appellate Jurisdiction (Art 132–136):** Appeals from High Courts.
  - **Advisory Jurisdiction (Art 143):** President can consult SC on law or fact.
  - **Court of Record (Art 129):** Decisions have evidentiary value; power to punish for contempt.

### High Courts (Articles 214–231)
- Total High Courts in India: **25** (Latest: Andhra Pradesh HC at Amaravati).
- Oldest High Court: Calcutta High Court (1862).
- Retirement Age of HC Judge: **62 years**.
- Writ Jurisdiction under **Article 226** is wider than SC (can issue writs for Fundamental Rights and ordinary legal rights).

## Emergency Provisions (Part XVIII, Articles 352–360)

```
1. National Emergency (Article 352)
   ├── Grounds: War, External Aggression, or Armed Rebellion (replaced "internal disturbance" by 44th Amend)
   ├── Approved within 1 month by special majority; valid for 6 months at a time
   └── Articles 20 and 21 CANNOT be suspended during National Emergency!

2. President's Rule / State Emergency (Article 356 & 365)
   ├── Grounds: Failure of constitutional machinery in a State
   ├── Approved within 2 months by simple majority; maximum duration: 3 years
   └── First imposed in Punjab (1951)

3. Financial Emergency (Article 360)
   ├── Grounds: Financial stability or credit of India threatened
   └── Has NEVER been declared in India so far!
```

## Last 10 Years PYQs with Explanations

### PYQ 1 — SSC CHSL 2024
**Q.** Which amendment lowered the voting age from 21 years to 18 years in India?
- A) 42nd Amendment
- B) 44th Amendment
- C) 61st Amendment
- D) 73rd Amendment
- **Answer:** **C) 61st Amendment (1989)** ✅
- *Explanation:* The 61st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1988 amended Article 326 to reduce the voting age from 21 to 18 years.

### PYQ 2 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** Who presided over the first meeting of the Constituent Assembly on December 9, 1946?
- A) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
- B) Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha
- C) Dr. Rajendra Prasad
- D) Jawaharlal Nehru
- **Answer:** **B) Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha** ✅
- *Explanation:* Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha was appointed interim temporary President following the French practice of appointing the oldest member. Dr. Rajendra Prasad was elected permanent president on Dec 11.

### PYQ 3 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** Which Article of the Indian Constitution guarantees the Abolition of Untouchability?
- A) Article 14
- B) Article 17
- C) Article 19
- D) Article 21
- **Answer:** **B) Article 17** ✅
- *Explanation:* Article 17 abolishes untouchability and forbids its practice in any form.

### PYQ 4 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** The concept of "Directive Principles of State Policy" (DPSP) was borrowed from which country?
- A) USA
- B) Ireland
- C) USSR
- D) Australia
- **Answer:** **B) Ireland** ✅
- *Explanation:* Ireland borrowed it from Spain, and India adopted it from Ireland into Part IV of our Constitution.

### PYQ 5 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** Under which Article can the President of India declare a Financial Emergency?
- A) Article 352
- B) Article 356
- C) Article 360
- D) Article 365
- **Answer:** **C) Article 360** ✅
- *Explanation:* Article 352 is National Emergency, Article 356 is President's Rule, and Article 360 is Financial Emergency (never declared so far).

### PYQ 6 — SSC CHSL 2021
**Q.** Who acts as the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha?
- A) President
- B) Prime Minister
- C) Vice-President
- D) Chief Justice of India
- **Answer:** **C) Vice-President** ✅
- *Explanation:* Under Article 64, the Vice-President of India is the ex-officio Chairman of the Council of States (Rajya Sabha).

### PYQ 7 — SSC CHSL 2020
**Q.** Which Fundamental Right was removed by the 44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978?
- A) Right to Equality
- B) Right to Property
- C) Right to Freedom of Speech
- D) Right against Exploitation
- **Answer:** **B) Right to Property** ✅
- *Explanation:* Right to Property was deleted from Article 19(1)(f) and Article 31, and made a constitutional/legal right under Article 300A in Part XII.

### PYQ 8 — SSC CHSL 2019
**Q.** What is the minimum age required to become a member of the Lok Sabha?
- A) 18 years
- B) 21 years
- C) 25 years
- D) 30 years
- **Answer:** **C) 25 years** ✅
- *Explanation:* Lok Sabha: 25 years; Rajya Sabha: 30 years; President & VP & Governor: 35 years; Panchayat member: 21 years; Voter: 18 years.

### PYQ 9 — SSC CHSL 2018
**Q.** The joint sitting of both Houses of Parliament is presided over by:
- A) President of India
- B) Vice-President of India
- C) Speaker of Lok Sabha
- D) Prime Minister
- **Answer:** **C) Speaker of Lok Sabha** ✅
- *Explanation:* Under Article 118(4), the Speaker of Lok Sabha presides over a joint sitting of Parliament called by the President under Article 108.

### PYQ 10 — SSC CHSL 2017
**Q.** Which schedule of the Indian Constitution contains the Anti-Defection Law?
- A) 8th Schedule
- B) 9th Schedule
- C) 10th Schedule
- D) 11th Schedule
- **Answer:** **C) 10th Schedule** ✅
- *Explanation:* Added by the 52nd Amendment Act in 1985 to disqualify legislators on grounds of defection.
