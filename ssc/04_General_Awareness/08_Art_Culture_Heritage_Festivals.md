## Classical Dances of India & Renowned Exponents

The **Sangeet Natak Akademi** recognizes **8 Classical Dance Forms** of India:

| Dance Form | State of Origin | Key Features / Style | Renowned Exponents |
|------------|-----------------|----------------------|--------------------|
| **Bharatanatyam** | **Tamil Nadu** | Oldest classical dance; fire dance (*Ekaharya* solo performance); temple dance originally performed by Devadasis; Rukmini Devi Arundale revived it | **Rukmini Devi Arundale, Yamini Krishnamurthy, Padma Subrahmanyam, Sonal Mansingh, Mrinalini Sarabhai, Mallika Sarabhai** |
| **Kathak** | **Uttar Pradesh / North India** | Derived from *"Katha"* (storyteller); intricate footwork (*Tatkar*), spins (*Chakkars*); Gharanas: Lucknow, Jaipur, Banaras | **Pandit Birju Maharaj, Lachhu Maharaj, Sitara Devi, Shambhu Maharaj, Shovana Narayan** |
| **Kathakali** | **Kerala** | Elaborate facial makeup (*Paccha, Kathi, Thadi*), colorful headgear; enacts stories from Mahabharata/Ramayana; male-dominated | **Kalamandalam Gopi, Guru Kunchu Kurup, Kottakkal Sivaraman** |
| **Kuchipudi** | **Andhra Pradesh** | Named after Kuchelapuram village; includes *Tarangam* (dancing on brass plate edges with pot on head) | **Yamini Krishnamurthy, Raja and Radha Reddy, Vempati Chinna Satyam, Swapnasundari** |
| **Odissi** | **Odisha** | Sensuous, lyrical dance; *Tribhanga* posture (three-bend posture) and *Chowk*; themes of Lord Jagannath & Jayadeva's Gita Govinda | **Kelucharan Mohapatra, Sonal Mansingh, Sanjukta Panigrahi, Madhavi Mudgal** |
| **Manipuri** | **Manipur** | Soft, graceful movements; *Raas Leela* of Radha-Krishna; dancer wears barrel-shaped cylindrical skirt (*Kumil*) | **Guru Bipin Singh, Jhaveri Sisters (Nayna, Suverna, Ranjana, Darshana), Charu Mathur** |
| **Mohiniyattam** | **Kerala** | Dance of the Enchantress (*Mohini*); graceful swaying movements resembling coconut trees and waves; white/off-white saree with gold border (*Kasavu*) | **Kalyanikutty Amma ("Mother of Mohiniyattam"), Bharati Shivaji, Sunanda Nair, Kanak Rele** |
| **Sattriya** | **Assam** | Introduced in 15th century by Vaishnavite saint **Mahapurush Srimanta Sankardev**; preserved in *Sattras* (monasteries); recognized as classical dance in 2000 | **Bapuram Barbayan, Jatin Goswami, Ghanakanta Bora, Tankeswar Hazarika** |

> *Note:* Ministry of Culture also considers **Chhau** (semi-classical dance of Jharkhand, West Bengal, Odisha) as a classical dance.

## Classical Music, Musical Instruments & Maestros

### Two Traditions of Indian Classical Music
1. **Hindustani Classical (North India):** Influenced by Persian and Arab elements; Styles: Dhrupad (oldest), Khayal, Thumri, Tarana, Tappa.
2. **Carnatic Classical (South India):** Indigenous; Trinity of Carnatic Music: **Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri**.

## UNESCO World Heritage Sites in India

India has **42 UNESCO World Heritage Sites** (as of 2023–2024: 34 Cultural, 7 Natural, 1 Mixed):
- **Mixed Heritage Site:** **Khangchendzonga National Park** (Sikkim) — *Only mixed site in India*.
- **Recent Inscriptions:**
  - **41st Site (2023):** **Santiniketan** (West Bengal — established by Rabindranath Tagore).
  - **42nd Site (2023):** **Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas** (Karnataka — Belur, Halebidu, Somanathapura temples).
  - *Previous additions:* 39th: Kakatiya Rudreshwara / Ramappa Temple (Telangana); 40th: Dholavira (Harappan city, Gujarat).
- **First Sites Inscribed (1983):** Ajanta Caves, Ellora Caves, Agra Fort, Taj Mahal.

## Famous Historical Monuments & Temple Architecture

### Three Styles of Indian Temple Architecture
1. **Nagara Style (North India):** Shikhara (curvilinear tower), Garbhagriha (sanctum sanctorum), Amalaka & Kalasha on top. Examples: Khajuraho (Kandariya Mahadeva), Sun Temple (Konark), Jagannath Temple (Puri), Dilwara Temples (Mount Abu).
2. **Dravida Style (South India):** High boundary walls, massive entrance gateways (**Gopurams**), stepped pyramid tower (**Vimana**), temple tank. Examples: Brihadisvara Temple (Thanjavur, built by Rajaraja Chola I), Meenakshi Temple (Madurai), Shore Temple (Mahabalipuram).
3. **Vesara Style (Hybrid):** Blend of Nagara and Dravida styles; developed under Chalukyas and Hoysalas in the Deccan region. Examples: Badami, Aihole, Belur, Halebidu.

### Famous Temples Quick Factsheet
| Temple | Location | Built By / Era | Key Feature |
|--------|----------|----------------|-------------|
| **Sun Temple (Black Pagoda)** | Konark, Odisha | King Narasimhadeva I (Eastern Ganga Dynasty) | Designed as a massive chariot with 24 carved wheels pulled by 7 horses |
| **Brihadisvara Temple (Big Temple)** | Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu | Rajaraja Chola I | Granite temple; single stone cupola at top |
| **Kailash Temple (Cave 16)** | Ellora, Maharashtra | Krishna I (Rashtrakuta Dynasty) | Monolithic rock-cut temple carved from top to bottom |
| **Khajuraho Temples** | Chhatarpur, Madhya Pradesh | Chandela Dynasty | Famous for erotic sculptures and Nagara architecture |
| **Dilwara Temples** | Mount Abu, Rajasthan | Vimal Shah / Vastupala & Tejapala | Jain marble temples with intricate carvings |
| **Kamakhya Temple** | Guwahati, Assam | Mlechha/Koch Dynasty | Shakti Peeth on Nilachal Hill; Ambubachi Mela |
| **Shore Temple** | Mahabalipuram, Tamil Nadu | Narasimhavarman II (Pallava Dynasty) | Early rock-cut and structural temple on sea coast |

## Traditional Paintings, Handicrafts & Martial Arts

### Traditional Indian Paintings
| Painting Style | State | Description |
|----------------|-------|-------------|
| **Madhubani (Mithila)** | **Bihar** | Done with fingers, twigs, brushes, matchsticks using natural dyes; themes of nature, Hindu deities |
| **Pattachitra** | **Odisha & West Bengal** | Traditional cloth-based scroll painting; mythological themes of Lord Jagannath |
| **Warli Painting** | **Maharashtra** | Tribal art using rice paste on mud walls; rhythmic human geometric figures (circles, triangles) |
| **Kalamkari** | **Andhra Pradesh** | Hand-painted or block-printed cotton textile using tamarind pen (*kalam*) |
| **Phad Painting** | **Rajasthan** | Large scroll painting depicting folk deities Pabuji and Devnarayan |
| **Thangka** | **Sikkim, Ladakh, HP** | Tibetan Buddhist scroll painting on cotton/silk appliqué |

### Traditional Martial Arts
- **Kalaripayattu:** **Kerala** (considered mother of all martial arts).
- **Silambam:** **Tamil Nadu** (bamboo staff martial art).
- **Thang-Ta:** **Manipur** (sword and spear martial art).
- **Gatka:** **Punjab** (Sikh martial art with wooden sticks/shields).
- **Mallakhamb:** **Madhya Pradesh** (State Sport of MP; aerial acrobatics on a vertical wooden pole).

## Last 10 Years PYQs with Detailed Explanations

### PYQ 1 — SSC CHSL 2024
**Q.** In which state of India is the famous "Hornbill Festival" celebrated annually?
- A) Manipur
- B) Mizoram
- C) Nagaland
- D) Meghalaya
- **Answer:** **C) Nagaland** ✅
- *Explanation:* Known as the "Festival of Festivals", held every year from December 1 to 10 at Naga Heritage Village, Kisama near Kohima.

### PYQ 2 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** Pandit Birju Maharaj, who passed away in 2022, was a maestro of which classical dance form?
- A) Kathakali
- B) Kathak
- C) Odissi
- D) Bharatanatyam
- **Answer:** **B) Kathak** ✅
- *Explanation:* Pandit Birju Maharaj was a legendary exponent of the Lucknow Kalka-Bindadin gharana of Kathak dance.

### PYQ 3 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** The Cheraw dance is a popular traditional folk dance of which Indian state?
- A) Mizoram
- B) Assam
- C) Sikkim
- D) Tripura
- **Answer:** **A) Mizoram** ✅
- *Explanation:* Cheraw is the famous bamboo dance of Mizoram where dancers step in and out of rhythmic bamboo grids.

### PYQ 4 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** The Ambubachi Mela is an annual Hindu mela held at which temple?
- A) Kashi Vishwanath, Varanasi
- B) Kamakhya Temple, Guwahati
- C) Somnath Temple, Gujarat
- D) Jagannath Temple, Puri
- **Answer:** **B) Kamakhya Temple, Guwahati (Assam)** ✅
- *Explanation:* Celebrated during monsoon in June at Kamakhya Temple atop Nilachal Hill, symbolizing the annual menstrual cycle of Goddess Kamakhya.

### PYQ 5 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** Rukmini Devi Arundale was associated with the revival of which Indian classical dance form?
- A) Kathak
- B) Manipuri
- C) Bharatanatyam
- D) Sattriya
- **Answer:** **C) Bharatanatyam** ✅
- *Explanation:* Rukmini Devi Arundale established the Kalakshetra foundation in Chennai and revived Sadir/Devadasi dance into dignified modern Bharatanatyam.

### PYQ 6 — SSC CHSL 2021
**Q.** Which musical instrument was played by the legendary maestro Ustad Bismillah Khan?
- A) Sitar
- B) Sarod
- C) Shehnai
- D) Flute
- **Answer:** **C) Shehnai** ✅
- *Explanation:* Ustad Bismillah Khan elevated the Shehnai from folk ceremonies to classical concert stages; awarded Bharat Ratna in 2001.

### PYQ 7 — SSC CHSL 2020
**Q.** "Sattriya" was recognized as a Classical Dance form of India in 2000. It originated in:
- A) Manipur
- B) Assam
- C) Tripura
- D) West Bengal
- **Answer:** **B) Assam** ✅
- *Explanation:* Introduced in the 15th century by the great Vaishnavite reformer Srimanta Sankardev as an accompaniment to the Ankia Naat.

### PYQ 8 — SSC CHSL 2019
**Q.** The Sun Temple of Konark was built by which ruler?
- A) Rajaraja Chola I
- B) Narasimhadeva I
- C) Krishna I
- D) Pulakeshin II
- **Answer:** **B) Narasimhadeva I** ✅
- *Explanation:* Built in the 13th century by King Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga Dynasty; known as the "Black Pagoda".

### PYQ 9 — SSC CHSL 2018
**Q.** Madhubani painting style is native to which Indian state?
- A) West Bengal
- B) Rajasthan
- C) Bihar
- D) Madhya Pradesh
- **Answer:** **C) Bihar** ✅
- *Explanation:* Madhubani (Mithila art) originated in the Mithila region of Bihar; characterized by line drawings filled with bright natural colors.

### PYQ 10 — SSC CHSL 2017
**Q.** Which of the following is the only "Mixed" UNESCO World Heritage Site in India?
- A) Kaziranga National Park
- B) Sundarbans National Park
- C) Khangchendzonga National Park
- D) Manas Wildlife Sanctuary
- **Answer:** **C) Khangchendzonga National Park (Sikkim)** ✅
- *Explanation:* Inscribed in 2016 under both natural and cultural criteria (Tibetan Buddhist sacred landscape around Mount Kanchenjunga).
