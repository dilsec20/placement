## The Olympic Games & Major Multi-Sport Events

### The Olympic Games
- **Ancient Olympics:** Began in 776 BCE at Olympia, Greece.
- **Modern Olympics:** Revived by **Baron Pierre de Coubertin**; first held in **1896 in Athens, Greece**.
- **Headquarters of International Olympic Committee (IOC):** **Lausanne, Switzerland**.
- **Olympic Motto:** *"Citius, Altius, Fortius - Communiter"* (Faster, Higher, Stronger - Together).
- **Olympic Rings (5 Interlinked Rings representing 5 Continents):**
  - 🔵 **Blue:** Europe
  - 🟡 **Yellow:** Asia
  - ⚫ **Black:** Africa
  - 🟢 **Green:** Oceania / Australia
  - 🔴 **Red:** Americas

#### Milestones of India at the Olympics
| Year & Venue | Indian Athlete / Team | Sport | Significance |
|--------------|----------------------|-------|--------------|
| **1900 Paris** | Norman Pritchard | Athletics | 2 Silver medals (200m & 200m hurdles) — First Asian/Indian medalist |
| **1928 Amsterdam** | Indian Men's Hockey Team | Field Hockey | First Olympic Gold for independent/colonial India under Jaipal Singh Munda |
| **1928–1956** | Indian Hockey Team | Hockey | Won **6 consecutive Olympic Gold Medals** (Legend Major Dhyan Chand played in 1928, 1932, 1936) |
| **1952 Helsinki** | **K.D. Jadhav** | Wrestling | Bronze — **First individual medal for independent India** |
| **2000 Sydney** | **Karnam Malleswari** | Weightlifting | Bronze (69 kg) — **First Indian woman to win an Olympic medal** |
| **2008 Beijing** | **Abhinav Bindra** | Shooting | Gold (10m Air Rifle) — **First individual Olympic Gold for India** |
| **2008 Beijing** | Vijender Singh (Bronze - Boxing), Sushil Kumar (Bronze - Wrestling) | Multi | 3 total medals |
| **2012 London** | Sushil Kumar, Mary Kom, Saina Nehwal, Vijay Kumar, Gagan Narang, Yogeshwar Dutt | Multi | 6 medals (Sushil Kumar became first to win back-to-back individual medals: 2008 & 2012) |
| **2016 Rio** | P.V. Sindhu (Silver - Badminton), Sakshi Malik (Bronze - Wrestling) | Multi | Sakshi Malik became first Indian woman wrestler to win an Olympic medal |
| **2020 Tokyo** (held 2021)| **Neeraj Chopra** | Athletics (Javelin Throw) | **Gold (87.58 m)** — First track and field Olympic Gold for India; India's best tally: 7 medals (1 Gold, 2 Silver, 4 Bronze) |
| **2024 Paris** | Manu Bhaker (2 Bronze - Shooting), Neeraj Chopra (Silver), Aman Sehrawat (Bronze), Swapnil Kusale (Bronze), Hockey Team (Bronze) | Multi | Manu Bhaker became first Indian in independent India to win **two medals in a single Olympics** |

- **Upcoming Olympic Hosts:** 2028: Los Angeles, USA | 2032: Brisbane, Australia.

### Asian Games
- **First Asian Games:** Held in **New Delhi, India in 1951** (Motto: "Ever Onward"; inaugurated by President Rajendra Prasad).
- India hosted the Asian Games twice: **1951 and 1982** (both in New Delhi).
- **19th Asian Games (Hangzhou, China 2023):** India crossed the 100-medal milestone for the first time, winning **107 medals** (28 Gold, 38 Silver, 41 Bronze).
- **Next Asian Games:** 2026 in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan.

### Commonwealth Games
- **First Commonwealth Games (British Empire Games):** 1930 in **Hamilton, Canada**.
- India hosted the XIX Commonwealth Games in **2010 in New Delhi**.
- **2022 Birmingham CWG:** India finished 4th with 61 medals (22 Gold).

## Major Global Tournaments

### Cricket
- **First Test Match:** 1877 (Australia vs England, Melbourne).
- **First One Day International (ODI):** 1971 (Melbourne).
- **First Cricket World Cup:** 1975 in England (Won by West Indies; captain Clive Lloyd).
- **India's World Cup Triumphs:**
  - **1983 ODI World Cup:** India beat West Indies at Lord's under **Kapil Dev**.
  - **2007 T20 World Cup:** India beat Pakistan at Johannesburg under **M.S. Dhoni**.
  - **2011 ODI World Cup:** India beat Sri Lanka at Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai under **M.S. Dhoni**.
  - **2024 T20 World Cup:** India beat South Africa at Barbados under **Rohit Sharma**.
- **Pitch Dimensions:** Length = **22 yards (20.12 metres)**; Stumps height = 28 inches; Weight of ball = 155.9g to 163g.

### Football (Soccer)
- **Governing Body:** FIFA (Fédération Internationale de Football Association; HQ: **Zurich, Switzerland**).
- **First World Cup:** 1930 in **Uruguay** (Winner: Uruguay).
- **Most World Cup Wins:** **Brazil (5 titles: 1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002)**.
- **FIFA World Cup 2022 (Qatar):** Winner: **Argentina** (beat France on penalties); Golden Ball (Best Player): **Lionel Messi**; Golden Boot (Top Scorer): **Kylian Mbappé** (8 goals); Golden Glove (Best Goalkeeper): Emiliano Martínez.
- **2026 World Cup:** Jointly hosted by USA, Canada, and Mexico (48 teams).

### Tennis Grand Slam Tournaments (Chronological Order)
| Grand Slam | Surface | Time of Year | Inaugural Year |
|------------|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Australian Open** | Hard Court (Plexicushion) | Mid-January | 1905 |
| **French Open (Roland Garros)** | Clay Court (Red Clay) | Late May - Early June | 1891 |
| **Wimbledon** | Grass Court | Late June - Early July | **1877 (Oldest Grand Slam)** |
| **US Open** | Hard Court (DecoTurf) | Late August - September | 1881 |

- **Most Men's Grand Slam Singles Titles:** **Novak Djokovic (24)**, Rafael Nadal (22), Roger Federer (20).
- **Indian Grand Slam Icons:**
  - **Mahesh Bhupathi:** First Indian to win a Grand Slam (1997 French Open Mixed Doubles with Rika Hiraki).
  - **Leander Paes:** 18 Grand Slam doubles/mixed titles + 1996 Atlanta Olympic singles bronze.
  - **Sania Mirza:** 6 Grand Slam doubles/mixed titles (former World No. 1 in doubles).
  - **Rohan Bopanna:** Oldest first-time men's doubles Grand Slam champion and World No. 1 (2024 Australian Open at age 43).

### Badminton
- **Thomas Cup:** Men's World Team Championship (Founded 1949). **India won historic first title in 2022** beating Indonesia 3-0.
- **Uber Cup:** Women's World Team Championship (Founded 1956).
- **Sudirman Cup:** Mixed World Team Championship.

### Chess
- **Governing Body:** FIDE (HQ: Lausanne, Switzerland).
- **Board:** 64 squares (32 white, 32 black).
- **First Indian Grandmaster:** **Viswanathan Anand** (1988; 5-time World Chess Champion).
- **45th Chess Olympiad (Budapest 2024):** India won **historic double Gold medals** in both Open and Women's categories (Gukesh D, Arjun Erigaisi, Praggnanandhaa, Harika Dronavalli, Divya Deshmukh, Vantika Agrawal).

## Last 10 Years PYQs with Detailed Explanations

### PYQ 1 — SSC CHSL 2024
**Q.** On which date is National Sports Day celebrated in India every year?
- A) August 15
- B) August 29
- C) September 5
- D) July 26
- **Answer:** **B) August 29** ✅
- *Explanation:* Celebrated on August 29 to commemorate the birth anniversary of hockey wizard Major Dhyan Chand.

### PYQ 2 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** Which country won the FIFA Men's World Cup 2022 held in Qatar?
- A) France
- B) Argentina
- C) Brazil
- D) Croatia
- **Answer:** **B) Argentina** ✅
- *Explanation:* Argentina defeated France 4-2 on penalties after a 3-3 draw to claim their 3rd World Cup title; Lionel Messi won the Golden Ball.

### PYQ 3 — SSC CHSL 2023
**Q.** The "Thomas Cup" is an international trophy associated with which sport?
- A) Tennis
- B) Table Tennis
- C) Badminton
- D) Golf
- **Answer:** **C) Badminton** ✅
- *Explanation:* The Thomas Cup is the world men's team championship in badminton. Uber Cup is for women. India won the Thomas Cup for the first time in 2022.

### PYQ 4 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** How many players are there on each side in a game of Rugby Football?
- A) 11
- B) 12
- C) 14
- D) 15
- **Answer:** **D) 15** ✅
- *Explanation:* A standard Rugby Union team consists of 15 players on the field.

### PYQ 5 — SSC CHSL 2022
**Q.** World Environment Day is observed across the globe annually on:
- A) April 22
- B) June 5
- C) September 16
- D) October 24
- **Answer:** **B) June 5** ✅
- *Explanation:* Designated by the UN General Assembly in 1972 on the opening day of the Stockholm Conference on the Human Environment.

### PYQ 6 — SSC CHSL 2021
**Q.** The oldest football tournament in Asia is:
- A) Santosh Trophy
- B) Durand Cup
- C) Rovers Cup
- D) IFA Shield
- **Answer:** **B) Durand Cup** ✅
- *Explanation:* Founded in 1888 in Shimla by Sir Mortimer Durand; it is the oldest football tournament in Asia and the 3rd oldest in the world.

### PYQ 7 — SSC CHSL 2020
**Q.** What does the term "Chinaman" refer to in the game of cricket?
- A) A straight drive shot
- B) A left-arm unorthodox spin bowler
- C) A type of fielding position
- D) A batsman dismissed on zero
- **Answer:** **B) A left-arm unorthodox spin bowler** ✅
- *Explanation:* A left-arm wrist spin bowler (like Kuldeep Yadav) is traditionally called a "Chinaman".

### PYQ 8 — SSC CHSL 2019
**Q.** In which city were the first modern Olympic Games held in 1896?
- A) Paris
- B) London
- C) Athens
- D) Rome
- **Answer:** **C) Athens, Greece** ✅
- *Explanation:* The first modern Olympic Games were held in the Panathenaic Stadium in Athens, organized under the IOC founded by Pierre de Coubertin.

### PYQ 9 — SSC CHSL 2018
**Q.** Which Grand Slam tennis tournament is played on a natural grass court?
- A) French Open
- B) US Open
- C) Wimbledon
- D) Australian Open
- **Answer:** **C) Wimbledon** ✅
- *Explanation:* Wimbledon (oldest Grand Slam, est. 1877) is played on outdoor grass courts. French Open is on clay; Australian and US Opens are on hard courts.

### PYQ 10 — SSC CHSL 2017
**Q.** Pravasi Bharatiya Divas (Non-Resident Indian Day) is celebrated in India on January 9 to commemorate:
- A) The adoption of the Constitution
- B) Mahatma Gandhi's return to India from South Africa
- C) The launch of the Non-Cooperation Movement
- D) Swami Vivekananda's return from Chicago
- **Answer:** **B) Mahatma Gandhi's return to India from South Africa** ✅
- *Explanation:* Mahatma Gandhi returned to Mumbai from South Africa on January 9, 1915.
