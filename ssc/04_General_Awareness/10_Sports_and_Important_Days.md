## 1. The Olympic Games & Paralympics (Complete India Record)

### 1.1 Ancient & Modern Olympic Games: Core Facts

| Parameter | Ancient Olympic Games | Modern Olympic Games |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Inaugural Year & Venue** | **776 BCE**, Olympia, Greece | **1896 (April 6–15)**, Panathenaic Stadium, **Athens, Greece** |
| **Dedicated To / Founder** | Held in honour of Greek God **Zeus** | Revived by **Baron Pierre de Coubertin** (Father of Modern Olympics) |
| **Abolition / Governing Body** | Abolished in **393 CE** by Roman Emperor **Theodosius I** | **IOC (International Olympic Committee)** — founded **23 June 1894** (Olympic Day) |
| **Headquarters** | Olympia (Elis), Greece | **Lausanne, Switzerland** (Olympic Museum also in Lausanne) |
| **Olympic Motto** | — | ***Citius, Altius, Fortius – Communiter*** (Faster, Higher, Stronger – Together; *"Communiter"* added in **2021** at Tokyo) |
| **Olympic Flag & 5 Rings** | — | Designed by Coubertin in **1913**; first hoisted at **1920 Antwerp Olympics**; white background with **5 interlocking rings** representing 5 inhabited continents |
| **Ring Colours & Continents** | — | **Blue** = Europe, **Yellow** = Asia, **Black** = Africa, **Green** = Oceania (Australia), **Red** = Americas |
| **Olympic Torch Relay** | Sacred flame lit at Olympia | Modern torch relay introduced at **1936 Berlin Olympics** (lit by sun's rays at Olympia) |
| **Olympic Mascot Debut** | — | First official mascot: **Waldi** (dachshund) at **1972 Munich** (unofficial: *Schuss* at 1968 Winter Olympics) |
| **Indian Olympic Association (IOA)** | — | Founded in **1927**; 1st President: **Sir Dorabji Tata**; 1st Woman President: **P. T. Usha** (elected Dec 2022) |

> **High-Yield Exam Pointers:**
> - **1900 Paris:** First Olympics in which **women participated**; also India's debut (represented by **Norman Pritchard**).
> - **1920 Antwerp:** India sent its **first official team** (6 athletes) under British India.
> - **1948 London:** First Olympics in which **Independent India** participated under the Tiranga.
> - **1924 Paris (Chamonix):** First **Winter Olympics** held.
> - **Abhinav Bindra** (2008 Beijing, Shooting) is India's **first individual Olympic Gold** medalist; **Neeraj Chopra** (2020 Tokyo, Javelin) is India's **first Athletics Gold** medalist.

- **ICC (International Cricket Council):** Founded **1909** (as Imperial Cricket Conference), HQ: **Dubai, UAE** (moved from London in 2005).

- **Historic Firsts:**
  - **1st Test Match:** **1877** (Australia vs England at MCG, Melbourne — Australia won by 45 runs).

  - **1st T20I Match:** **2005** (Australia vs New Zealand at Auckland); **India's 1st T20I:** **2006** vs South Africa at Johannesburg (Capt: Virender Sehwag).

## 6. Last 10 Years SSC CHSL / CGL Sports & Days PYQs (35 Solved Questions)

1. **Who was the first Indian to win an individual Olympic medal after independence?**
   (a) Milkha Singh (b) K. D. Jadhav (c) Leander Paes (d) Karnam Malleswari
   **Ans: (b) K. D. Jadhav** — *Won Wrestling Bronze at the 1952 Helsinki Olympics.*

2. **Who was the first Indian woman to win an Olympic medal?**
   (a) P. T. Usha (b) Saina Nehwal (c) Karnam Malleswari (d) M. C. Mary Kom
   **Ans: (c) Karnam Malleswari** — *Won Weightlifting Bronze at the 2000 Sydney Olympics.*

3. **In which Olympics did Abhinav Bindra win India's first individual Gold medal?**
   (a) 2004 Athens (b) 2008 Beijing (c) 2012 London (d) 2016 Rio
   **Ans: (b) 2008 Beijing** — *Won Gold in Men's 10m Air Rifle Shooting.*

4. **How many total medals did India win at the Hangzhou Asian Games (held in 2023)?**
   (a) 70 (b) 85 (c) 101 (d) 107
   **Ans: (d) 107** — *28 Gold, 38 Silver, 41 Bronze (4th place overall).*

5. **At the Paris 2024 Olympics, who became the first Indian post-independence to win two medals in a single edition?**
   (a) Neeraj Chopra (b) P. V. Sindhu (c) Manu Bhaker (d) Swapnil Kusale
   **Ans: (c) Manu Bhaker** — *Won Bronze in Women's 10m Air Pistol and 10m Air Pistol Mixed Team (with Sarabjot Singh).*

6. **How many medals did India win at the Paris 2024 Paralympic Games?**
   (a) 19 (b) 24 (c) 29 (d) 32
   **Ans: (c) 29** — *7 Gold, 9 Silver, 13 Bronze (all-time record for India).*

7. **Who was India's first-ever Paralympic Gold medalist (1972 Heidelberg)?**
   (a) Devendra Jhajharia (b) Murlikant Petkar (c) Mariyappan Thangavelu (d) Joginder Singh Bedi
   **Ans: (b) Murlikant Petkar** — *Won 50m Freestyle Swimming Gold with a World Record of 37.33 seconds.*

8. **What do the five interlocking rings on the Olympic flag represent?**
   (a) Five founding countries (b) Five Olympic sports (c) Five inhabited continents (d) Five ideals of Greece
   **Ans: (c) Five inhabited continents** — *Blue (Europe), Yellow (Asia), Black (Africa), Green (Oceania), Red (Americas).*

9. **Which country will host the 2028 Summer Olympic Games?**
   (a) Australia (b) USA (c) France (d) Japan
   **Ans: (b) USA** — *Los Angeles (2028), followed by Brisbane, Australia (2032).*

10. **Who captained the Indian Hockey team to its 8th and last Olympic Gold medal at Moscow 1980?**
    (a) Ajit Pal Singh (b) Charanjit Singh (c) Vasudevan Baskaran (d) Balbir Singh Sr.
    **Ans: (c) Vasudevan Baskaran** — *India defeated Spain 4–3 in the 1980 final.*

11. **What is the standard length of a cricket pitch from stump to stump?**
    (a) 20 yards (b) 22 yards (c) 24 yards (d) 18 yards
    **Ans: (b) 22 yards** — *Equivalent to 66 feet or 20.12 metres.*

12. **What is the standard height of cricket stumps above the ground?**
    (a) 26 inches (b) 28 inches (c) 30 inches (d) 32 inches
    **Ans: (b) 28 inches** — *Width across the three stumps is 9 inches.*

13. **Which team did India defeat in the final to win the 2024 ICC Men's T20 World Cup in Barbados?**
    (a) Australia (b) England (c) South Africa (d) New Zealand
