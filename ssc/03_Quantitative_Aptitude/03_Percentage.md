## Basic Concepts

### Definition
```
Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100

"Per cent" = "per hundred" = "out of 100"

25% = 25/100 = 1/4 = 0.25
```

### Key Relationships
```
x% of y = y% of x
Example: 25% of 80 = 80% of 25 = 20

Percentage = (Value / Total) × 100
Value = (Percentage × Total) / 100
```

## Percentage Change

### Formula
```
Percentage Change = [(New - Old) / Old] × 100

Percentage Increase = [(New - Old) / Old] × 100
Percentage Decrease = [(Old - New) / Old] × 100
```

### Examples:
```
Q: Price increased from ₹200 to ₹250. Percentage increase?
= [(250-200)/200] × 100 = (50/200) × 100 = 25%

Q: Population decreased from 50000 to 45000. Percentage decrease?
= [(50000-45000)/50000] × 100 = (5000/50000) × 100 = 10%
```

### A Important Concept: "A is what % of B" vs "A is what % more than B"
```
"A is what % of B" = (A/B) × 100
"A is what % more than B" = [(A-B)/B] × 100

Example: A = 60, B = 50
A is what % of B = (60/50) × 100 = 120%
A is what % more than B = [(60-50)/50] × 100 = 20%
```

## Successive Percentage Change

### Formula
```
If two successive changes of a% and b% are applied:
Net change = a + b + (ab/100)

Example: Price increases by 20%, then decreases by 10%
Net = 20 + (-10) + (20×(-10))/100 = 20 - 10 - 2 = 8% increase

Example: Two successive discounts of 20% and 10%:
Net discount = -20 + (-10) + ((-20)×(-10))/100 = -30 + 2 = -28%
Equivalent single discount = 28%
```

### Quick Results for Common Successive Changes
```
+10%, +10% = +21%
+20%, +20% = +44%
+10%, -10% = -1% (net loss!)
+20%, -20% = -4% (net loss!)
+25%, -20% = 0% (no change!)
+50%, -33.33% = 0% (no change)
```

## Important Formulas

### Population/Value Growth
```
After n years at r% growth:
Final = Initial × (1 + r/100)ⁿ

After n years at r% decline:
Final = Initial × (1 - r/100)ⁿ

Example: Population 10000, grows 10% per year, after 2 years:
= 10000 × (1.1)² = 10000 × 1.21 = 12100
```

### If A is x% more than B, then B is less than A by:
```
B is less than A by: [x/(100+x)] × 100 %

Example: If A is 25% more than B:
B is less than A by: [25/125] × 100 = 20%

Quick pairs to memorize:
+10% more ↔ 9.09% less (1/11)
+20% more ↔ 16.67% less (1/6)
+25% more ↔ 20% less (1/5)
+33.33% more ↔ 25% less (1/4)
+50% more ↔ 33.33% less (1/3)
+100% more ↔ 50% less (1/2)
```

### If Price Increases by x%, Consumption Should Decrease by:
```
Decrease = [x/(100+x)] × 100 %

To keep expenditure constant.

Example: Price increases 25%. To keep expenditure same:
Consumption decreases by: [25/125] × 100 = 20%
```

## Tips and Tricks

### ⚡ Quick Calculations:
```
10% of anything: Move decimal one place left
   10% of 450 = 45

5% = half of 10%
   5% of 450 = 22.5

1% = move decimal two places
   1% of 450 = 4.5

25% = divide by 4
   25% of 480 = 120

33.33% = divide by 3
   33.33% of 360 = 120

50% = divide by 2
   50% of 380 = 190

To find x% of y: Calculate 10% first, then adjust
   37% of 200: 10%=20, 30%=60, 7%=14, total=74
```
