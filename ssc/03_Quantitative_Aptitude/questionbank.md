### Q001 · Percentage

Find 15% of 625.

A. 93.75
B. 98.75
C. 83.75
D. 108.75

**Answer:** A. 93.75
**Explanation:** 15% of 625 = 625 × 15/100 = 93.75.
**Shortcut / rule:** Use 10% = one tenth, 5% = half of 10%, and 1% = one hundredth to calculate mentally.

### Q002 · Profit and Loss

An article costs ₹640. If it is sold at a profit of 20%, find its selling price.

A. ₹533.33
B. ₹512
C. ₹768
D. ₹660

**Answer:** C. ₹768
**Explanation:** Selling price = cost price × (100 + profit%)/100 = 640 × 120/100 = ₹768.
**Shortcut / rule:** For profit, multiply cost price by (100 + profit%)/100; for loss use (100 − loss%)/100.

### Q003 · Ratio and Proportion

Two amounts are in the ratio 5:11. If their sum is 224, what is the smaller amount?

A. 154
B. 210
C. 14
D. 70

**Answer:** D. 70
**Explanation:** Total parts = 5+11=16. One part = 224/16=14; the smaller amount is 5×14=70.
**Shortcut / rule:** Add ratio parts first, divide the total by that sum, then multiply by the requested part.
