## 💡 Top Golden Rules for Cracking IBM On-Campus
1. **Never Hardcode Output:** HackerRank runs hidden test cases with large numbers ($N = 10^5$, weights $> 10^9$). Always use $O(N)$ or $O(N \log N)$ approaches and 64-bit integers (`long` in Java, `long long` in C++).
2. **Cognitive Games Reward Speed AND Accuracy:** In *Resemble* and *Shortcuts*, making an incorrect move or spamming clicks docks points heavily. Plan your trajectory before clicking.
3. **English AI Tests Enunciation, Not Accent:** Speak in a calm, steady corporate Indian English tone. Do not fake an accent; ensure your microphone is noise-free and keep pacing around 125–135 words per minute.
4. **Speak the Language of Hybrid Cloud & SDLC:** IBM emphasizes its hybrid cloud (Red Hat OpenShift, IBM Cloud) and enterprise AI solutions (watsonx). Mentioning containerization, CI/CD, and Agile in your interviews will instantly set you apart from other freshers.
