## 2. Situational Judgment Questions

**Choose the BEST response:**

---

**Q11.** A teammate is struggling with their assigned task and the deadline is tomorrow. You:
- (a) Do their task for them to save time
- (b) Report to the manager that they're falling behind
- (c) Offer to help them and work together to complete it
- (d) Continue with your own work, it's not your problem

> **Answer: (c)**
> Collaboration + not undermining teammate.

---

**Q12.** Your manager gives you feedback that your report was poorly structured. You:
- (a) Defend your work, explaining why you did it that way
- (b) Accept the feedback, ask for specific improvements, and revise
- (c) Feel demotivated and wonder if this job is right for you
- (d) Apologize and redo the report exactly as manager says without asking

> **Answer: (b)**
> Growth mindset + proactive learning.

---

**Q13.** You disagree with a decision made by your team lead. You:
- (a) Express your concerns privately to the lead and explain your reasoning
- (b) Complain to other teammates about the bad decision
- (c) Just follow the decision without saying anything
- (d) Escalate to the manager above

> **Answer: (a)**
> Professional and constructive disagreement.

---

**Q14.** You are given two urgent tasks with the same deadline. You:
- (a) Panic and ask to extend one deadline
- (b) Work on both simultaneously and try to finish both
- (c) Assess priority, communicate with relevant stakeholders, and plan accordingly
- (d) Complete the easier one first and leave the harder one

> **Answer: (c)**
> Prioritization + communication.

---

**Q15.** A client is unhappy with the deliverable you submitted. You:
- (a) Explain that the brief they gave was unclear
- (b) Apologize, understand their expectations, and propose a solution
- (c) Ask your manager to handle the client
- (d) Submit a revised version immediately without discussing

> **Answer: (b)**
> Client-first mindset + accountability.

---

**Q16.** You notice a colleague making an ethical mistake (e.g., faking data). You:
- (a) Ignore it — not your business
- (b) Confront them aggressively in front of the team
- (c) Talk to them privately first, then escalate if needed
- (d) Immediately report to HR without talking to them

> **Answer: (c)**
> Integrity + professional ethics.

---

**Q17.** You are new to a project and don't understand the requirements. You:
- (a) Wait until you figure it out on your own
- (b) Ask a senior colleague to explain it to you
- (c) Google for similar projects and guess
- (d) Start the project and correct mistakes later

> **Answer: (b)**
> Asking for help = growth mindset, not weakness.

---

**Q18.** Your team celebrates success without acknowledging your individual contribution. You:
- (a) Feel resentful and bring it up in the meeting
- (b) Feel satisfied that the team succeeded, even without individual credit
- (c) Ask the manager to specifically mention your name
- (d) Withdraw from future team efforts

> **Answer: (b)**
> Shows team-first mentality.

## 5. Tips & Strategies

### ✅ DO's
- Answer as a **professional at work**, not as a student
- Be **consistent** — your answers will be cross-checked algorithmically
- **First instinct** is usually correct — don't overthink
- Show **growth mindset** — embracing feedback, change, learning
- Express **collaborative** tendencies

### ❌ DON'Ts
- Don't **always pick extremes** (5 or 1) — looks fake
- Don't **contradict yourself** — the algorithm catches it
- Don't try to **game the system** — just be genuine
- Don't **rush** — you have ~22 seconds per question, use it

### ⏰ Time Strategy
- 54 questions in 20 minutes = ~22 seconds per question
- **Read, react, move on** — first instinct is best
- Don't go back and change answers unless you're very sure
