## 📝 Question Types

### Type 1: Read Aloud (Most Common)
A sentence appears on screen. Read it aloud clearly.

**Tips:**
- Read at moderate pace — not too fast, not too slow
- Don't skip words
- Stress key words naturally
- Don't re-read if you stumble — continue smoothly

**Example sentences to practice:**
```
"The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog."
"Technology is rapidly transforming the way we work and communicate."
"Effective leadership requires both vision and the ability to inspire others."
"The software development lifecycle includes planning, development, testing, and deployment."
"Cloud computing enables organizations to scale their infrastructure dynamically."
```

---

### Type 2: Repeat After Listening
You hear a sentence → Repeat it exactly.

**Tips:**
- Listen carefully to the FULL sentence before speaking
- Focus on key phrases, not individual words
- Maintain the same intonation pattern
- Don't add or remove words

---

### Type 3: Short Answer Questions
Answer a question in 1–2 sentences.

**Example Questions & Model Answers:**

**Q: What is your favorite technology and why?**
> "My favorite technology is cloud computing because it enables businesses to scale efficiently and reduces infrastructure costs significantly."

**Q: Describe a challenge you faced and how you overcame it.**
> "During my final year project, I faced difficulties integrating APIs. I broke the problem into smaller tasks, researched documentation thoroughly, and successfully delivered the project on time."

**Q: Why do you want to work at Accenture?**
> "I want to work at Accenture because it offers exposure to cutting-edge technologies, a global work environment, and excellent opportunities for professional growth."

---

### Type 4: Picture/Situation Description
A picture or scenario is shown. Describe it in 30–60 seconds.

**Framework for describing:**
```
1. What do you see? (Main subject/scene)
2. What is happening? (Actions/events)
3. Where is it? (Setting/location)
4. What might be the context? (Interpretation)
5. Your observation (Quick conclusion)
```

**Example:**
> "In this image, I can see a group of professionals collaborating in a modern office space. They appear to be having a team meeting with laptops and a whiteboard. The setting looks like a technology company, and everyone seems engaged in the discussion. This reflects the importance of teamwork and communication in the corporate environment."

---

### Type 5: Sentence Building
Given words/phrases → Form a grammatically correct sentence.

**Example:**
Given: technology / important / modern / is / in / world
> "Technology is important in the modern world."

## 📘 Common Grammar Rules to Remember

### Subject-Verb Agreement
```
✅ He goes to work every day.
❌ He go to work every day.

✅ The team is working on the project.
❌ The team are working on the project. (In American English)

✅ Each of the students has submitted the assignment.
❌ Each of the students have submitted the assignment.
```

### Tenses
```
Present Simple:  I work at Accenture.
Present Continuous: I am working on a project.
Past Simple: I completed the task yesterday.
Past Continuous: I was working when he called.
Present Perfect: I have finished the assignment.
Future Simple: I will attend the meeting tomorrow.
```

### Common Errors
```
❌ "I am having a car." → ✅ "I have a car."
❌ "He did not went." → ✅ "He did not go."
❌ "She is more taller." → ✅ "She is taller."
❌ "I am agree." → ✅ "I agree."
❌ "Discuss about this." → ✅ "Discuss this."
❌ "Return back." → ✅ "Return."
```

## 💡 Preparation Tips

### Daily Practice (10 mins/day)
1. **Read aloud** — newspaper articles, tech blogs
2. **Record yourself** — listen for unclear pronunciation
3. **Shadow speaking** — repeat after English podcasts/TED talks
4. **Tongue twisters** — improve fluency

### Tongue Twisters for Practice
```
"She sells seashells by the seashore."
"Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled peppers."
"How much wood would a woodchuck chuck."
"Red lorry, yellow lorry, red lorry, yellow lorry."
```

### During the Assessment
| Do ✅ | Don't ❌ |
|------|---------|
| Speak clearly and at moderate pace | Don't speak too fast |
| Maintain consistent volume | Don't mumble or whisper |
| Use complete sentences | Don't use slang or fillers (um, uh) |
| Pause naturally between sentences | Don't have long silences (> 3 sec) |
| Use proper grammar | Don't use Hindi/regional words |
| Enunciate consonants clearly | Don't eat or drink while speaking |

### Equipment Checklist
```
✅ Headset with microphone (wired preferred)
✅ Quiet room — no background noise
✅ Test microphone before exam
✅ Stable internet connection
✅ Browser permissions for microphone enabled
```
