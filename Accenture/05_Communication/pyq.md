## 2. Short Answer Questions

**Speak for 15–30 seconds per answer. Use complete sentences.**

---

**Q1.** Tell me about yourself.
> "My name is [Name]. I am a [Year] year [Branch] student at [College]. I am passionate about technology, particularly software development. I have experience with C++ and web development through my college projects. I am a quick learner and enjoy solving problems."

---

**Q2.** Why do you want to work at Accenture?
> "I want to work at Accenture because it offers a global platform to work on cutting-edge technologies. The company's commitment to innovation and diversity aligns with my career aspirations. I believe Accenture will provide excellent opportunities for professional growth and learning."

---

**Q3.** What is your favorite subject and why?
> "My favorite subject is Data Structures and Algorithms because it teaches problem-solving skills that are fundamental to software development. Understanding how to optimize code and choose the right data structure for a given problem is both challenging and rewarding."

---

**Q4.** Describe a team project you worked on.
> "During my final year, I worked with a team of four on a web-based project management tool. My role was backend development using Node.js. We followed agile methodology with weekly sprints. The project taught me the importance of clear communication and task delegation."

---

**Q5.** What are your strengths?
> "My key strengths are problem-solving ability, adaptability, and teamwork. I am able to break complex problems into manageable parts and find efficient solutions. I also adapt quickly to new technologies and work well in collaborative environments."

---

**Q6.** What do you know about cloud computing?
> "Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services such as storage, processing power, and databases over the internet. It enables organizations to scale their infrastructure based on demand without investing in physical hardware. Major providers include AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud."

---

**Q7.** How do you handle pressure?
> "I handle pressure by prioritizing tasks and focusing on one thing at a time. I create a structured plan with clear deadlines, which helps me stay organized. I also believe in asking for help when needed rather than trying to do everything alone."

---

**Q8.** What is the most important skill for a software developer?
> "I believe problem-solving is the most important skill for a software developer. While languages and frameworks change over time, the ability to analyze a problem, design an efficient solution, and debug issues effectively remains constant and valuable throughout one's career."

---

**Q9.** Describe a technology trend that excites you.
> "Generative AI excites me because of its potential to transform how we create content, write code, and interact with technology. Tools like large language models are already being used to assist developers, automate documentation, and provide intelligent customer support."

---

**Q10.** What do you do in your free time?
> "In my free time, I enjoy coding practice on platforms like LeetCode and contributing to open-source projects. I also read technology blogs to stay updated with industry trends. Additionally, I enjoy playing cricket, which helps me relax and maintain a work-life balance."

## 4. Picture Description Templates

**Use these frameworks when describing any picture/scenario:**

### Template 1: General Scene
```
"In this image, I can see [main subject/people].
They are [action/activity] in what appears to be [location/setting].
The [specific detail] suggests that [interpretation].
Overall, this image depicts [main theme/message]."
```

### Template 2: Office/Work Scene
```
"This image shows [number] professionals in a [type] work environment.
They appear to be [engaged in activity — meeting/coding/presenting].
The modern workspace includes [details — laptops, whiteboards, etc.].
This reflects the collaborative nature of today's corporate culture."
```

### Template 3: Technology/Innovation
```
"This picture illustrates [technology/concept].
We can see [specific elements — devices, screens, interfaces].
This technology is significant because [importance/impact].
It demonstrates how [broader connection to industry/society]."
```

"Therefore..."

## 6. Grammar Error Correction

**Identify and correct the errors:**

---

**Q19.** "He don't know the answer." 
> ✅ "He **doesn't** know the answer."

**Q20.** "I am working here since 2020."
> ✅ "I **have been** working here since 2020."

**Q21.** "She is more smarter than him."
> ✅ "She is **smarter** than him." (remove "more" — double comparative)

**Q22.** "Each student have their own laptop."
> ✅ "Each student **has** their own laptop."

**Q23.** "I am agree with you."
> ✅ "I **agree** with you." (agree is a verb, not adjective)

**Q24.** "He gave me an useful advice."
> ✅ "He gave me **useful advice.**" (advice is uncountable — no "an")

**Q25.** "The informations are incorrect."
> ✅ "The **information is** incorrect." (information is uncountable)

**Q26.** "Please revert back to me."
> ✅ "Please **revert** to me." (revert already means go back)

**Q27.** "I did not went to college yesterday."
> ✅ "I did not **go** to college yesterday."

**Q28.** "He is one of the best student in class."
> ✅ "He is one of the best **students** in class."
