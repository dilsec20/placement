# 🎙️ Part A: AI Voice Scoring Mechanics & Speaking Drills

### How AI Rates Your Speech:
1. **Pacing & Fluency:** Optimal speaking rate is **130–150 words per minute**.
   * Speaking too slowly ($< 100$ WPM) registers as hesitation/struggle.
   * Speaking too fast ($> 180$ WPM) drops pronunciation clarity.
2. **Pause Architecture:** Natural pauses should occur ONLY at commas, periods, or logical phrase boundaries (150–300ms). Unnatural pauses mid-phrase or filler sounds (*"uh"*, *"um"*, *"like"*) sharply penalize your fluency score.
3. **Acoustic Clarity:** Speak with consistent vocal energy directly into the headset microphone. Do not trail off at sentence ends.

### Section 1: Error Spotting (Qs 1–12)

#### Q1. Find the part containing the grammatical error:
*(A) Each of the candidates / (B) who have qualified for the final interview / (C) are required to bring / (D) their original degree certificates.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
* **Explanation:** The subject of the sentence is the singular pronoun *"Each"*, not *"candidates"*. Hence, the singular verb *"is required"* must be used instead of *"are required"*.

#### Q2. Find the error:
*(A) Neither the principal architect / (B) nor the lead developers / (C) was present at the incident review meeting / (D) held early this morning.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
* **Explanation:** When two subjects are joined by *"neither... nor"*, the verb agrees with the subject closest to it. Here, *"lead developers"* is plural, so it requires *"were present"*.

#### Q3. Find the error:
*(A) The team had scarcely / (B) deployed the microservices update / (C) than the monitoring alerts / (D) detected a spike in CPU usage.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
* **Explanation:** Correlative conjunction rule: *"Scarcely... when"* or *"Hardly... when"* are paired together. *"Than"* is paired with *"No sooner... than"*. Replace *"than"* with *"when"*.

#### Q4. Find the error:
*(A) The committee has discussed about / (B) the budget allocation for cloud services / (C) for over three consecutive hours / (D) without reaching a consensus.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
* **Explanation:** *"Discuss"* is a transitive verb that directly takes an object. Saying *"discussed about"* is a redundant error. It should simply be *"discussed the budget allocation"*.

#### Q5. Find the error:
*(A) She is one of the / (B) few software engineers who / (C) has mastered both kernel development / (D) and distributed systems.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
* **Explanation:** In the structure *"one of the [plural noun] who"*, the relative pronoun *"who"* refers to the plural antecedent (*"engineers"*). Therefore, the relative clause requires a plural verb: *"have mastered"*.

#### Q6. Find the error:
*(A) Unless the development team does not / (B) complete the unit test suite by Friday, / (C) the release manager will not / (D) approve the deployment.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
* **Explanation:** Double negative error. *"Unless"* already carries a negative conditional meaning (*"if not"*). Using *"does not"* creates a redundant double negative. It should be: *"Unless the development team completes..."*

#### Q7. Find the error:
*(A) The performance of our / (B) newly optimized caching layer / (C) is superior than / (D) the legacy relational database.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
* **Explanation:** Latin comparatives ending in *-ior* (such as *superior, inferior, prior, senior, junior*) take the preposition **"to"**, never *"than"*. It should be: *"is superior to..."*

#### Q8. Find the error:
*(A) Having worked in the company / (B) for five years, / (C) the management promoted him / (D) to the position of Senior Analyst.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
* **Explanation:** Dangling modifier. As written, *"Having worked in the company for five years"* modifies *"the management"*, implying management worked for five years. Correct form: *"Having worked in the company for five years, he was promoted by the management..."*

#### Q9. Find the error:
*(A) The number of active / (B) user sessions on the platform / (C) have increased dramatically / (D) since the marketing campaign began.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
* **Explanation:** *"The number of"* takes a **singular** verb (*"has increased"*), whereas *"A number of"* takes a plural verb.

#### Q10. Find the error:
*(A) He requested his supervisor / (B) to kindly grant him leave / (C) because he was suffering / (D) with high fever.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(D)**
* **Explanation:** The correct preposition with the verb *suffer* in this context is **"from"**, not *"with"*. Correct: *"suffering from high fever"*.

#### Q11. Find the error:
*(A) If I was the project lead, / (B) I would have allocated / (C) more time for regression testing / (D) prior to the launch.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
* **Explanation:** Subjunctive mood for hypothetical/contrary-to-fact conditions requires **"were"** regardless of the subject. Correct: *"If I were the project lead..."*

#### Q12. Find the error:
*(A) Between you and I, / (B) the technical architecture proposed / (C) by the consultant is / (D) unnecessarily complex.*
* **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
* **Explanation:** Prepositions take objective pronouns. *"Between"* is a preposition, so it requires the objective form **"me"**, not the subjective *"I"*. Correct: *"Between you and me..."*

### Section 2: Sentence Correction & Completion (Qs 13–24)

#### Q13. Choose the grammatically correct sentence:
* (A) He has been living in Bangalore since five years.
* (B) He has been living in Bangalore for five years.
* (C) He is living in Bangalore since five years.
* (D) He had lived in Bangalore since five years.
* **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
* **Explanation:** *"For"* denotes a duration/period of time (*"for five years"*), whereas *"since"* denotes a specific starting point in time (*"since 2021"*).

#### Q14. The CTO emphasized that every engineer must adhere _______ the company's open-source security guidelines.
* (A) with
* (B) to
* (C) on
* (D) for
* **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
* **Explanation:** The verb *adhere* strictly takes the preposition **"to"** (*adhere to standards/rules*).

#### Q15. Identify the correct replacement for the bracketed portion:  
*"The database query was [so slow that neither of the reports were] generated on time."*
* (A) so slow that neither of the reports was
* (B) so slowly that neither of the reports were
* (C) so slow that none of the reports were
* (D) very slow that neither report was
* **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
* **Explanation:** *"Neither"* of two items is singular and takes the singular verb *"was"*. *"Slow"* functions correctly as a predicate adjective following the linking verb *"was"*.

#### Q16. "I look forward to _______ with your development team on the upcoming AI integration."
* (A) collaborate
* (B) collaborating
* (C) have collaborated
* (D) collaboration
* **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
* **Explanation:** In the phrasal verb *"look forward to"*, *"to"* is a preposition, not part of an infinitive. Prepositions are followed by a gerund (*"-ing"* form) or a noun.

#### Q17. The server crashed _______ the unexpected load spike occurred during the flash sale.
* (A) despite
* (B) because of
* (C) because
* (D) due to
* **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
* **Explanation:** *"Because"* is a conjunction introducing a subordinate clause with a subject (*"the unexpected load spike"*) and verb (*"occurred"*). *"Because of"* and *"due to"* are prepositions that precede nouns/noun phrases without verbs.

#### Q18. Choose the option that correctly improves the underlined phrase:  
*"Not only the frontend developer **did finish his sprint tasks early, but he also assisted** the QA team."*
* (A) finished his sprint tasks early, but he also assisted
* (B) did the frontend developer finish his sprint tasks early, but he also assisted
* (C) had the frontend developer finished his sprint tasks early, but also assisted
* (D) finished not only his sprint tasks early, but assisted also
* **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
* **Explanation:** Inversion rule: When a negative or restrictive conjunction like *"Not only"* begins a sentence, inverted word order (Auxiliary verb + Subject + Main verb) must follow: *"Not only did the frontend developer finish..."*

#### Q19. The team had to call _______ the deployment due to an unhandled security vulnerability.
* (A) out
* (B) in
* (C) off
* (D) up
* **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
* **Explanation:** The phrasal verb *"call off"* means to cancel an event or procedure.

#### Q20. Had the automated test suite been configured properly, the regression bug _______ caught before reaching production.
* (A) would be
* (B) would have been
* (C) will have been
* (D) had been
* **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
* **Explanation:** Inverted third conditional (*"Had + Subject + V3, Subject + would have been + V3"*).

#### Q21. The new feature is designed to be accessible _______ users across all mobile and desktop devices.
* (A) with
* (B) for
* (C) to
* (D) by
* **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
* **Explanation:** *Accessible* is followed by the preposition **"to"** (*accessible to someone*).

#### Q22. Choose the correct active voice form of:  
*"The database migration was completed by the infrastructure team before midnight."*
* (A) The infrastructure team completed the database migration before midnight.
* (B) The infrastructure team had completed the database migration before midnight.
* (C) The infrastructure team was completing the database migration before midnight.
* (D) The infrastructure team has completed the database migration before midnight.
* **Correct Answer:** **(A)**
* **Explanation:** The original sentence is in simple past passive (*"was completed"*). Its active equivalent is simple past (*"completed"*).

#### Q23. Despite working under extreme pressure, her demeanor remained completely _______.
* (A) agitated
* (B) unruffled
* (C) turbulent
* (D) volatile
* **Correct Answer:** **(B)**
* **Explanation:** *"Despite"* indicates contrast. The context of high pressure requires a word meaning calm and composed. *"Unruffled"* means undisturbed and poised.

#### Q24. "Scarcely had the architect finished the presentation _______ the board members raised critical questions."
* (A) than
* (B) then
* (C) when
* (D) while
* **Correct Answer:** **(C)**
* **Explanation:** *"Scarcely had..."* is strictly followed by **"when"**.
