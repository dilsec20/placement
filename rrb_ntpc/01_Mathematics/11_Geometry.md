## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Lines and Angles

**Types of Angles:**
| Type | Range |
|------|-------|
| Acute | 0° < θ < 90° |
| Right | θ = 90° |
| Obtuse | 90° < θ < 180° |
| Straight | θ = 180° |
| Reflex | 180° < θ < 360° |
| Complete | θ = 360° |

**Angle Pairs:**
- **Complementary:** Sum = 90° (e.g., 30° + 60°)
- **Supplementary:** Sum = 180° (e.g., 110° + 70°)
- **Vertically Opposite Angles:** Equal when two lines intersect
- **Linear Pair:** Adjacent angles on a straight line = 180°

**Parallel Lines Cut by Transversal:**
- Alternate interior angles are **equal**
- Alternate exterior angles are **equal**
- Co-interior (same-side interior) angles are **supplementary** (sum = 180°)
- Corresponding angles are **equal**

### 2. Triangles

**Sum of angles = 180°**

**Types by Sides:**
| Type | Property |
|------|----------|
| Equilateral | All sides equal, all angles = 60° |
| Isosceles | Two sides equal, base angles equal |
| Scalene | All sides different |

**Types by Angles:**
| Type | Property |
|------|----------|
| Acute | All angles < 90° |
| Right | One angle = 90° |
| Obtuse | One angle > 90° |

**Key Triangle Properties:**
- Exterior angle = Sum of two non-adjacent interior angles
- Sum of any two sides > third side (Triangle Inequality)
- Difference of any two sides < third side
- Side opposite to larger angle is longer

### 3. Important Triangle Theorems

**Pythagoras Theorem (Right Triangle):**
Hypotenuse² = Base² + Perpendicular²

**Common Pythagorean Triplets:**
(3,4,5), (5,12,13), (7,24,25), (8,15,17), (9,40,41), (11,60,61), (12,35,37), (20,21,29)
And their multiples: (6,8,10), (9,12,15), (10,24,26), etc.

**Mid-Point Theorem:** Line joining midpoints of two sides is parallel to the third side and half its length.

**Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT / Thales):** A line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides proportionally.

**Angle Bisector Theorem:** The angle bisector divides the opposite side in the ratio of adjacent sides.
If AD bisects angle A in triangle ABC: BD/DC = AB/AC

### 4. Congruence of Triangles

Two triangles are congruent if they have the **same shape AND size**.

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| SSS | Three sides equal |
| SAS | Two sides and included angle equal |
| ASA | Two angles and included side equal |
| AAS | Two angles and a non-included side equal |
| RHS | Right angle, hypotenuse, and one side equal |

### 5. Similarity of Triangles

Two triangles are similar if they have the **same shape** (but may differ in size).

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| AA | Two angles equal |
| SSS | Sides in same ratio |
| SAS | Two sides in same ratio and included angle equal |

**If triangles are similar with ratio k:**
- Corresponding sides ratio = k
- Corresponding altitudes ratio = k
- Area ratio = k²
- Perimeter ratio = k

### 6. Special Centers of Triangle

| Center | Definition | Property |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Centroid (G)** | Intersection of medians | Divides each median in 2:1 from vertex |
| **Incenter (I)** | Intersection of angle bisectors | Equidistant from all sides |
| **Circumcenter (O)** | Intersection of perpendicular bisectors | Equidistant from all vertices |
| **Orthocenter (H)** | Intersection of altitudes | Angles depend on triangle type |

**Incircle radius (r):** r = Area/s (where s = semi-perimeter)
**Circumcircle radius (R):** R = abc/(4 × Area)

### 7. Circles

**Key Terms:**
- **Chord:** Line segment with both endpoints on the circle
- **Diameter:** Longest chord, passes through center = 2r
- **Secant:** Line that intersects circle at two points
- **Tangent:** Line that touches circle at exactly one point
- **Arc:** Part of the circumference
- **Sector:** Pie-slice (area between two radii and an arc)
- **Segment:** Area between a chord and the arc

**Circle Theorems:**
1. Angle in a semicircle = 90°
2. Angles in the same segment are equal
3. Angle at center = 2 × Angle at circumference (same arc)
4. Tangent ⊥ Radius at point of contact
5. Tangent lengths from external point are equal
6. If two chords intersect inside: PA × PB = PC × PD
7. If two secants from external point: PA × PB = PC × PD
8. Tangent-secant: PT² = PA × PB

**Chord Properties:**
- Equal chords are equidistant from center
- Perpendicular from center bisects the chord
- If d = distance from center to chord, r = radius, l = half-chord length: r² = d² + l²

### 8. Quadrilaterals

**Sum of angles = 360°**

| Shape | Properties | Area Formula |
|-------|-----------|------|
| Parallelogram | Opposite sides equal & parallel, opposite angles equal, diagonals bisect each other | base × height |
| Rectangle | All angles 90°, diagonals equal | l × b |
| Square | All sides equal, all angles 90°, diagonals equal & perpendicular | a² |
| Rhombus | All sides equal, diagonals perpendicular & bisect each other | (d₁ × d₂)/2 |
| Trapezium | One pair of parallel sides | ½(a+b) × h |
| Kite | Two pairs of adjacent equal sides | (d₁ × d₂)/2 |

| Formula | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| Triangle area | ½ × base × height |
| Triangle area (Heron's) | √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)], s=(a+b+c)/2 |
| Equilateral triangle area | (√3/4) × a² |
| Equilateral triangle height | (√3/2) × a |
| Pythagoras | h² = a² + b² |
| Circle area | πr² |
| Circle circumference | 2πr |
| Sector area | (θ/360°) × πr² |
| Arc length | (θ/360°) × 2πr |

## 💡 Tips, Tricks & Shortcuts

### Tip 1: Memorize Pythagorean Triplets
(3,4,5) and its multiples solve 50% of right triangle problems instantly.

### Tip 2: Equilateral Triangle Shortcuts
- Area = (√3/4)a² ≈ 0.433a²
- Height = (√3/2)a ≈ 0.866a
- Inradius = a/(2√3), Circumradius = a/√3

### Tip 3: Circle Angle Shortcuts
- Angle in semicircle = 90° (ALWAYS)
- Central angle = 2 × inscribed angle (same arc)
- Tangent ⊥ radius — use this for right triangle setup

### Tip 4: Similar Triangles → Area Ratio = (Side Ratio)²
If sides are in ratio 3:5, areas are in ratio 9:25.

## 📝 RRB NTPC Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

### PYQ 1 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**In a triangle, two angles are 45° and 65°. Find the third angle.**

(A) 60° (B) 70° (C) 80° (D) 75°

**Answer: (B) 70°**
*Solution:* 180° − 45° − 65° = 70°.

---

### PYQ 2 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**If the sides of a triangle are 6 cm, 8 cm, and 10 cm, what type of triangle is it?**

(A) Acute (B) Right (C) Obtuse (D) Equilateral

**Answer: (B) Right**
*Solution:* 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100 = 10². Pythagorean triplet → right triangle.

---

### PYQ 3 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**The length of a tangent from a point 13 cm from the center of a circle of radius 5 cm is:**

(A) 10 cm (B) 12 cm (C) 8 cm (D) 11 cm

**Answer: (B) 12 cm**
*Solution:* Tangent² = 13² − 5² = 169 − 25 = 144. Tangent = 12 cm.

---

### PYQ 4 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**The diagonals of a rhombus are 16 cm and 12 cm. Find its area.**

(A) 96 cm² (B) 192 cm² (C) 48 cm² (D) 144 cm²

**Answer: (A) 96 cm²**
*Solution:* Area = (d₁ × d₂)/2 = (16 × 12)/2 = 96 cm².

---

### PYQ 5 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Two sides of a triangle are 7 cm and 10 cm. Which cannot be the third side?**

(A) 5 cm (B) 8 cm (C) 2 cm (D) 12 cm

**Answer: (C) 2 cm**
*Solution:* Third side must be > |10−7| = 3 and < 10+7 = 17. 2 < 3, so 2 cm is not possible.

---

### PYQ 6 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**If the circumference of a circle is 44 cm, find the area.**

(A) 154 cm² (B) 144 cm² (C) 132 cm² (D) 166 cm²

**Answer: (A) 154 cm²**
*Solution:* 2πr = 44 → r = 44/(2 × 22/7) = 7 cm. Area = 22/7 × 49 = 154 cm².

---

### PYQ 7 (RRB NTPC 2020)
**An angle is 20° more than its complement. Find the angle.**

(A) 55° (B) 50° (C) 45° (D) 35°

**Answer: (A) 55°**
*Solution:* x = (90 − x) + 20 → 2x = 110 → x = 55°.

---

### PYQ 8 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**The perimeter of a triangle whose sides are in the ratio 3:4:5 is 60 cm. Find the area.**

(A) 150 cm² (B) 120 cm² (C) 100 cm² (D) 180 cm²

**Answer: (A) 150 cm²**
*Solution:* Sides: 15, 20, 25 cm (sum=60). This is a right triangle (3:4:5). Area = ½ × 15 × 20 = 150 cm².

---

### PYQ 9 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**A chord is 24 cm long. Distance from center is 5 cm. Find the radius.**

(A) 12 cm (B) 13 cm (C) 14 cm (D) 15 cm

**Answer: (B) 13 cm**
*Solution:* Half-chord = 12 cm. r² = 12² + 5² = 144 + 25 = 169. r = 13 cm.

---

### PYQ 10 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**The area of a sector with radius 7 cm and angle 60° is:**

(A) 25.67 cm² (B) 77/3 cm² (C) 77 cm² (D) 154/3 cm²

**Answer: (B) 77/3 cm²**
*Solution:* Area = (60/360) × 22/7 × 49 = (1/6) × 154 = 154/6 = 77/3 cm².

## 🎯 Exam Strategy & Revision Tips

1. **Memorize Pythagorean triplets** — most right triangle questions use them
2. **Circle theorems** are very frequently asked — especially tangent and chord problems
3. **Similar triangles** — remember area ratio = (side ratio)²
4. **Don't confuse congruence with similarity** — congruent = same size, similar = same shape
5. **Expected time:** 60–90 seconds per question
6. **Draw figures** even if not given — it helps visualize
