## 📖 Complete Theory

### 🔷 PART A — 2D Mensuration (Plane Figures)

#### 1. Triangle

A triangle is a closed figure with three sides and three angles.

**Types of Triangles:**
- **Equilateral Triangle**: All sides equal, all angles = 60°
- **Isosceles Triangle**: Two sides equal
- **Scalene Triangle**: All sides different
- **Right-angled Triangle**: One angle = 90°

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
        A
       / \
      /   \
   b /     \ c
    /       \
   /____h____\
  B     a     C
```
Where `a` = base, `h` = height, `b` and `c` are the other two sides.

**Area of Triangle:**
- General formula: Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × a × h
- When all three sides are known (Heron's formula):
  - s = (a + b + c) / 2 (semi-perimeter)
  - Area = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]
- Equilateral triangle: Area = (√3/4) × a²
- Isosceles triangle: Area = (b/4) × √(4a² − b²), where `a` = equal sides, `b` = base
- Right-angled triangle: Area = ½ × base × perpendicular

**Perimeter of Triangle:**
- P = a + b + c
- Equilateral: P = 3a

**Example 1:** Find the area of a triangle with sides 5 cm, 12 cm, and 13 cm.

**Solution:**
- s = (5 + 12 + 13)/2 = 30/2 = 15
- Area = √[15 × (15−5) × (15−12) × (15−13)]
- Area = √[15 × 10 × 3 × 2] = √900 = **30 cm²**
- ✅ Shortcut: Since 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169 = 13², it's a right triangle.
- Area = ½ × 5 × 12 = **30 cm²**

---

#### 2. Rectangle

A rectangle has four right angles with opposite sides equal.

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
  l (length)
  ___________
 |           |
 |           | b (breadth)
 |___________|
```

- **Area** = length × breadth = l × b
- **Perimeter** = 2(l + b)
- **Diagonal** = √(l² + b²)

**Example 2:** A rectangular garden is 60 m long and 40 m wide. Find its area, perimeter, and diagonal.

**Solution:**
- Area = 60 × 40 = **2400 m²**
- Perimeter = 2(60 + 40) = 2 × 100 = **200 m**
- Diagonal = √(60² + 40²) = √(3600 + 1600) = √5200 = **20√13 m ≈ 72.11 m**

---

#### 3. Square

A square is a special rectangle where all four sides are equal.

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
  a
  _____
 |     |
 |     | a
 |_____|
```

- **Area** = a² = (diagonal)²/2
- **Perimeter** = 4a
- **Diagonal** = a√2

**Example 3:** If the diagonal of a square is 10 cm, find its area.

**Solution:**
- Area = d²/2 = 10²/2 = 100/2 = **50 cm²**

---

#### 4. Circle

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
      ___
    /     \
   |   •   |  ← r (radius from center)
    \_____/
      ← d = 2r (diameter) →
```

- **Area** = πr²
- **Circumference (Perimeter)** = 2πr = πd
- **Area of semi-circle** = πr²/2
- **Perimeter of semi-circle** = πr + 2r = r(π + 2)
- **Area of quadrant** = πr²/4
- **Area of ring (annulus)** = π(R² − r²), where R = outer radius, r = inner radius

**Example 4:** Find the area enclosed between two concentric circles of radii 14 cm and 7 cm.

**Solution:**
- Area of ring = π(R² − r²) = (22/7)(14² − 7²)
- = (22/7)(196 − 49) = (22/7) × 147 = 22 × 21 = **462 cm²**

---

#### 5. Parallelogram

A quadrilateral with opposite sides parallel and equal.

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
       ________
      /       /
     /   h   /
    /___↕___/
       b
```

- **Area** = base × height = b × h
- **Perimeter** = 2(a + b) where a and b are adjacent sides

**Example 5:** A parallelogram has base 15 cm and height 8 cm. Find its area.

**Solution:**
- Area = 15 × 8 = **120 cm²**

---

#### 6. Trapezium (Trapezoid)

A quadrilateral with one pair of parallel sides.

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
     a (parallel side 1)
    ________
   /        \
  /    h      \
 /____________\
     b (parallel side 2)
```

- **Area** = ½ × (a + b) × h = ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × height
- **Perimeter** = sum of all four sides

**Example 6:** A trapezium has parallel sides of 12 cm and 8 cm, and the distance between them is 5 cm. Find the area.

**Solution:**
- Area = ½ × (12 + 8) × 5 = ½ × 20 × 5 = **50 cm²**

---

#### 7. Rhombus

A parallelogram with all four sides equal. Diagonals bisect each other at right angles.

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
       d₁
    /  |  \
   / __|__ \
  /   d₂   \
   \  |  /
    \ | /
```

- **Area** = ½ × d₁ × d₂ (where d₁ and d₂ are diagonals)
- **Perimeter** = 4a
- **Side** = ½ × √(d₁² + d₂²)

**Example 7:** The diagonals of a rhombus are 16 cm and 12 cm. Find its area and side.

**Solution:**
- Area = ½ × 16 × 12 = **96 cm²**
- Side = ½ × √(16² + 12²) = ½ × √(256 + 144) = ½ × √400 = ½ × 20 = **10 cm**

### 🔶 PART B — 3D Mensuration (Solid Figures)

#### 1. Cube

All edges are equal. Has 6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices.

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
     ________
    /       /|
   /       / |
  /_______ / |  ← edge = a
  |       |  /
  |       | /
  |_______|/
```

- **Volume** = a³
- **Total Surface Area (TSA)** = 6a²
- **Lateral Surface Area (LSA)** = 4a²
- **Diagonal (space)** = a√3
- **Diagonal (face)** = a√2

**Example 8:** Find the volume and TSA of a cube with edge 5 cm.

**Solution:**
- Volume = 5³ = **125 cm³**
- TSA = 6 × 5² = 6 × 25 = **150 cm²**

---

#### 2. Cuboid (Rectangular Prism)

Has 6 rectangular faces with length (l), breadth (b), and height (h).

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
     __________
    /          /|
   /          / |
  /__________/  | h
  |          |  /
  |          | / b
  |__________|/
       l
```

- **Volume** = l × b × h
- **TSA** = 2(lb + bh + lh)
- **LSA** = 2h(l + b)
- **Diagonal** = √(l² + b² + h²)

**Example 9:** A room is 10 m long, 8 m wide, and 4 m high. Find the cost of painting the four walls at ₹25/m².

**Solution:**
- LSA = 2h(l + b) = 2 × 4 × (10 + 8) = 8 × 18 = 144 m²
- Cost = 144 × 25 = **₹3,600**

---

#### 3. Cylinder

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
     ___
    /   \   ← circular top (radius r)
   |     |
   |     | h (height)
   |     |
    \___/   ← circular bottom
```

- **Volume** = πr²h
- **Curved Surface Area (CSA)** = 2πrh
- **TSA** = 2πr(r + h)

**Example 10:** A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Find its volume and TSA.

**Solution:**
- Volume = (22/7) × 7² × 10 = (22/7) × 49 × 10 = 22 × 70 = **1540 cm³**
- TSA = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × (7 + 10) = 2 × 22 × 17 = **748 cm²**

---

#### 4. Cone

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
       /\
      /  \
     /    \ l (slant height)
    /   h  \
   /   |    \
  /____|____\
       r
```

Where: l = √(r² + h²)

- **Volume** = ⅓πr²h
- **CSA** = πrl
- **TSA** = πr(r + l)

**Example 11:** A cone has radius 3 cm and height 4 cm. Find its slant height, volume, and CSA.

**Solution:**
- l = √(3² + 4²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = **5 cm**
- Volume = ⅓ × (22/7) × 9 × 4 = (22/7) × 12 = 264/7 = **37.71 cm³**
- CSA = (22/7) × 3 × 5 = 330/7 = **47.14 cm²**

---

#### 5. Sphere

**Diagram (Text Description):**
```
      ___
    /     \
   |   • r |
    \_____/
```

- **Volume** = (4/3)πr³
- **Surface Area** = 4πr²

**Example 12:** Find the volume and surface area of a sphere with radius 7 cm.

**Solution:**
- Volume = (4/3) × (22/7) × 7³ = (4/3) × (22/7) × 343 = (4/3) × 22 × 49 = 4312/3 = **1437.33 cm³**
- SA = 4 × (22/7) × 49 = 4 × 22 × 7 = **616 cm²**

---

#### 6. Hemisphere

Half of a sphere cut along a diameter.

- **Volume** = (2/3)πr³
- **CSA** = 2πr²
- **TSA** = 3πr² (curved + flat circular base)

**Example 13:** Find the TSA of a hemisphere with radius 14 cm.

**Solution:**
- TSA = 3 × (22/7) × 14² = 3 × (22/7) × 196 = 3 × 22 × 28 = **1848 cm²**

---

### 🔶 Additional Important Concepts

#### Frustum of a Cone (Truncated Cone)
When a cone is cut by a plane parallel to the base:
- **Volume** = (πh/3)(R² + r² + Rr)
- **CSA** = π(R + r)l, where l = √[h² + (R − r)²]
- **TSA** = π(R + r)l + πR² + πr²

#### Prism
- **Volume** = Area of base × height
- **LSA** = Perimeter of base × height

#### Pyramid
- **Volume** = ⅓ × Area of base × height

## 💡 Tips, Tricks & Shortcuts

### 🔹 Shortcut 1: Pythagoras Triplets for Right Triangles
Memorize these common triplets for quick area calculation:
- (3, 4, 5), (5, 12, 13), (8, 15, 17), (7, 24, 25)
- (6, 8, 10), (9, 12, 15), (12, 16, 20) — multiples of (3,4,5)

### 🔹 Shortcut 2: When diagonal of square is given
- Area = d²/2 → No need to find side first!

### 🔹 Shortcut 3: Area ratios
- If sides become k times, Area becomes k² times
- If sides become k times, Volume becomes k³ times
- If sides become k times, Perimeter becomes k times

### 🔹 Shortcut 4: Circle inscribed in/circumscribed around a square
- **Circle inscribed in a square**: r = a/2, Area of circle = πa²/4
- **Square inscribed in a circle**: a = r√2, Area of square = 2r²
- **Ratio of areas** (circle inscribed in square) = π/4

### 🔹 Shortcut 5: Hollow shapes
- Volume of hollow cylinder = πh(R² − r²)
- Volume of hollow sphere = (4π/3)(R³ − r³)

### 🔹 Shortcut 6: Wire bending problems
When a wire is bent from one shape to another, the **perimeter remains the same**.
- Example: If a square wire (side a) is bent into a circle → 4a = 2πr → r = 2a/π

### 🔹 Shortcut 7: Value of π
- Use π = 22/7 when radius/diameter is a multiple of 7
- Use π = 3.14 for other cases (or when specified)

### 🔹 Shortcut 8: Equilateral triangle shortcuts
- Height = (√3/2) × a
- Area = (√3/4) × a²
- Inradius = a/(2√3) = a√3/6
- Circumradius = a/√3 = a√3/3

### 🔹 Shortcut 9: Cube/Cuboid diagonal relationships
- If diagonal of a cube = d, then side = d/√3
- Volume = d³/(3√3)

### 🔹 Shortcut 10: Quick mental math
- For cylinder volume: think of it as "area of circle × height"
- For cone volume: it's always ⅓ of the corresponding cylinder

## 📝 RRB NTPC Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

### Question 1
**The length and breadth of a rectangle are in the ratio 3:2. If the perimeter is 40 cm, find its area.**

(A) 84 cm²  
(B) 96 cm²  
(C) 108 cm²  
(D) 120 cm²  

**✅ Correct Answer: (B) 96 cm²**

**Solution:**
- Let length = 3x, breadth = 2x
- Perimeter = 2(3x + 2x) = 10x = 40 → x = 4
- Length = 12 cm, Breadth = 8 cm
- Area = 12 × 8 = **96 cm²**

---

### Question 2
**The area of a circle is 616 cm². Find its circumference.**

(A) 44 cm  
(B) 66 cm  
(C) 88 cm  
(D) 110 cm  

**✅ Correct Answer: (C) 88 cm**

**Solution:**
- πr² = 616 → (22/7) × r² = 616
- r² = 616 × 7/22 = 196 → r = 14 cm
- Circumference = 2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 14 = **88 cm**

---

### Question 3
**A cube has a total surface area of 294 cm². Find its volume.**

(A) 216 cm³  
(B) 343 cm³  
(C) 512 cm³  
(D) 125 cm³  

**✅ Correct Answer: (B) 343 cm³**

**Solution:**
- 6a² = 294 → a² = 49 → a = 7 cm
- Volume = 7³ = **343 cm³**

---

### Question 4
**The base radius and height of a cylinder are 7 cm and 10 cm respectively. Find its curved surface area.**

(A) 220 cm²  
(B) 440 cm²  
(C) 660 cm²  
(D) 880 cm²  

**✅ Correct Answer: (B) 440 cm²**

**Solution:**
- CSA = 2πrh = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 10
- = 2 × 22 × 10 = **440 cm²**

---

### Question 5
**The diagonals of a rhombus are 10 cm and 24 cm. Find its perimeter.**

(A) 48 cm  
(B) 52 cm  
(C) 56 cm  
(D) 60 cm  

**✅ Correct Answer: (B) 52 cm**

**Solution:**
- Side = ½ × √(d₁² + d₂²) = ½ × √(100 + 576) = ½ × √676 = ½ × 26 = 13 cm
- Perimeter = 4 × 13 = **52 cm**

---

### Question 6
**A cone has a radius of 6 cm and slant height of 10 cm. Find its total surface area.**

(A) 96π cm²  
(B) 108π cm²  
(C) 120π cm²  
(D) 132π cm²  

**✅ Correct Answer: (A) 96π cm²**

**Solution:**
- TSA = πr(r + l) = π × 6 × (6 + 10) = π × 6 × 16 = **96π cm²**

---

### Question 7
**If the side of a square is increased by 50%, by what percentage does its area increase?**

(A) 50%  
(B) 100%  
(C) 125%  
(D) 150%  

**✅ Correct Answer: (C) 125%**

**Solution:**
- New side = 1.5a → New area = (1.5a)² = 2.25a²
- Increase = 2.25a² − a² = 1.25a²
- % increase = (1.25/1) × 100 = **125%**
- 📌 Shortcut: For increase of x%, area increase = (2x + x²/100)% = (100 + 25)% = 125%

---

### Question 8
**A wire in the form of a circle of radius 14 cm is bent into a square. Find the side of the square.**

(A) 18 cm  
(B) 20 cm  
(C) 22 cm  
(D) 24 cm  

**✅ Correct Answer: (C) 22 cm**

**Solution:**
- Length of wire = circumference of circle = 2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 14 = 88 cm
- Perimeter of square = 88 → 4a = 88 → a = **22 cm**

### Question 9
**The volume of a sphere is 4851 cm³. Find its surface area. (π = 22/7)**

(A) 1386 cm²  
(B) 1232 cm²  
(C) 1452 cm²  
(D) 1540 cm²  

**✅ Correct Answer: (A) 1386 cm²**

**Solution:**
- (4/3) × (22/7) × r³ = 4851
- r³ = 4851 × 3 × 7 / (4 × 22) = 4851 × 21/88 = 101871/88 = 1157.625
- Wait, let's recalculate: r³ = (4851 × 3 × 7)/(4 × 22) = (4851 × 21)/88 = 101871/88 = 1157.625
- Hmm, let's try: r³ = 4851 × 7 × 3 / (4 × 22) = 4851 × 21/88
- 4851 ÷ 11 = 441, so 4851/88 = 4851/88
- 4851 × 21 = 101871, 101871/88 = 1157.625... Let me try r = 10.5 = 21/2
- r³ = (21/2)³ = 9261/8 = 1157.625 ✓ → r = 10.5 cm
- SA = 4 × (22/7) × (10.5)² = 4 × (22/7) × 110.25 = 4 × 22 × 15.75 = **1386 cm²**

### Question 10
**A rectangular tank is 8 m long, 6 m wide, and 2.5 m deep. How many litres of water can it hold?**

(A) 1,00,000 litres  
(B) 1,20,000 litres  
(C) 1,50,000 litres  
(D) 2,00,000 litres  

**✅ Correct Answer: (B) 1,20,000 litres**

**Solution:**
- Volume = 8 × 6 × 2.5 = 120 m³
- 1 m³ = 1000 litres
- Capacity = 120 × 1000 = **1,20,000 litres**

### Question 11
**The area of a trapezium is 165 cm². If its parallel sides are 15 cm and 18 cm, find the height.**

(A) 8 cm  
(B) 10 cm  
(C) 12 cm  
(D) 14 cm  

**✅ Correct Answer: (B) 10 cm**

**Solution:**
- Area = ½(a + b) × h
- 165 = ½ × (15 + 18) × h = ½ × 33 × h
- h = (165 × 2)/33 = 330/33 = **10 cm**

### Question 12
**A hemisphere has a diameter of 42 cm. Find its volume. (π = 22/7)**

(A) 19404 cm³  
(B) 38808 cm³  
(C) 9702 cm³  
(D) 15246 cm³  

**✅ Correct Answer: (A) 19404 cm³**

**Solution:**
- r = 42/2 = 21 cm
- Volume = (2/3)πr³ = (2/3) × (22/7) × 21³
- = (2/3) × (22/7) × 9261 = (2/3) × 22 × 1323 = (2/3) × 29106 = **19404 cm³**

### Question 13
**The length of the longest rod that can be placed in a room 12 m long, 9 m broad, and 8 m high is:**

(A) 15 m  
(B) 17 m  
(C) 19 m  
(D) 21 m  

**✅ Correct Answer: (B) 17 m**

**Solution:**
- Longest rod = space diagonal of cuboid = √(l² + b² + h²)
- = √(144 + 81 + 64) = √289 = **17 m**

## 🎯 Exam Strategy & Revision Tips

### ✅ Do's:
1. **Memorize all formulas** — Create a formula sheet and revise daily
2. **Practice unit conversions** — Many questions trick you with mixed units (m & cm)
3. **Identify the shape first** — Read the question carefully, draw a rough figure
4. **Use π = 22/7** when radius or diameter is a multiple of 7
5. **Remember scaling rules** — If dimension × k, then Area × k², Volume × k³
6. **Practice wire-bending problems** — Perimeter remains constant

### ❌ Don'ts:
1. Don't confuse **CSA** and **TSA** — Read what's asked
2. Don't forget to convert units if they're different
3. Don't waste time deriving formulas — Just apply them directly
4. Don't guess blindly — There's negative marking (−1/3)

### 📅 Revision Schedule:
| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| Day 1 | Learn all 2D formulas + practice 20 questions |
| Day 2 | Learn all 3D formulas + practice 20 questions |
| Day 3 | Mixed practice (2D + 3D) — 30 questions |
| Day 4 | Previous year questions + formula revision |
| Day 5 | Quick formula test + weak areas revision |

### 🏆 Time Management:
- **Easy questions** (direct formula): 30–45 seconds
- **Medium questions** (2 steps): 60–90 seconds
- **Complex questions** (multi-step): Skip if > 2 minutes, come back later

### 📌 Most Important Formulas to Memorize First:
1. Area & Perimeter of Circle, Rectangle, Triangle
2. Volume & TSA of Cube, Cuboid, Cylinder
3. Heron's formula
4. Cone and Sphere volume formulas
5. Rhombus area = ½ × d₁ × d₂
