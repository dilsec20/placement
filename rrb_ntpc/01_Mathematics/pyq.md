### PYQ-M1. LCM and HCF

Two integers have HCF 7 and LCM 70. If one integer is 35, what is the other?

A. 14  
B. 25  
C. 40  
D. 49

**Answer:** A. 14

**Explanation:** Product of the integers = HCF × LCM = 7 × 70 = 490. The missing integer is 490 ÷ 35 = 14.

**Related fact / rule:** For two positive integers, their product equals their HCF multiplied by their LCM.

### PYQ-M2. Time and Work

A completes a task in the same time as B and C working together. A and B together take 10 days, while C alone takes 60 days. How long would B alone take?

A. 23 days  
B. 22 days  
C. 21 days  
D. 24 days

**Answer:** D. 24 days

**Explanation:** Let daily rates be a, b, c. Given a=b+c and a+b=1/10, while c=1/60. Thus 2b+c=1/10, so b=1/24.

**Related fact / rule:** Convert each worker’s completion time to a one-day work rate before combining workers.

**Source:** [RRB NTPC 2021 CBT-1, 18 Jan Shift 2 paper (Prepp PDF)](https://cdn-images.prepp.in/public/image/RRBNTPC2021CBT1PreviousYearPaper18Jan2021Shift2_3b2114a823f008b22f49e2c2dbf09baf.pdf)
