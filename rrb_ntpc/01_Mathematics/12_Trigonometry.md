## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Trigonometric Ratios

In a right triangle with angle θ:

| Ratio | Formula | Reciprocal |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| sin θ | Opposite / Hypotenuse | cosec θ = 1/sin θ |
| cos θ | Adjacent / Hypotenuse | sec θ = 1/cos θ |
| tan θ | Opposite / Adjacent | cot θ = 1/tan θ |

**Also:** tan θ = sin θ / cos θ, cot θ = cos θ / sin θ

### 2. Standard Angle Values (MUST MEMORIZE!)

| Angle | sin | cos | tan | cosec | sec | cot |
|-------|-----|-----|-----|-------|-----|-----|
| 0° | 0 | 1 | 0 | ∞ | 1 | ∞ |
| 30° | 1/2 | √3/2 | 1/√3 | 2 | 2/√3 | √3 |
| 45° | 1/√2 | 1/√2 | 1 | √2 | √2 | 1 |
| 60° | √3/2 | 1/2 | √3 | 2/√3 | 2 | 1/√3 |
| 90° | 1 | 0 | ∞ | 1 | ∞ | 0 |

**Memory Trick:** For sin values: write 0,1,2,3,4 → divide by 4 → take square root
- sin 0° = √(0/4) = 0
- sin 30° = √(1/4) = 1/2
- sin 45° = √(2/4) = 1/√2
- sin 60° = √(3/4) = √3/2
- sin 90° = √(4/4) = 1

For cos: **Reverse the sin values** → cos 0° = 1, cos 30° = √3/2, etc.

### 3. Trigonometric Identities (CRITICAL!)

**Pythagorean Identities:**
1. **sin²θ + cos²θ = 1**
2. **1 + tan²θ = sec²θ**
3. **1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ**

**Derived forms:**
- sin²θ = 1 − cos²θ
- tan²θ = sec²θ − 1
- cot²θ = cosec²θ − 1

### 4. Complementary Angles

sin(90° − θ) = cos θ, cos(90° − θ) = sin θ

tan(90° − θ) = cot θ, cot(90° − θ) = tan θ

sec(90° − θ) = cosec θ, cosec(90° − θ) = sec θ

**Example:** sin 53° = cos 37° (since 53 + 37 = 90)

### 5. Height and Distance

**Angle of Elevation:** Looking UP from horizontal
**Angle of Depression:** Looking DOWN from horizontal

**Key:** Angle of elevation from A to B = Angle of depression from B to A

**Common scenarios:**
- **Finding height of tower/building:**
  - tan θ = Height / Distance from base
  - Height = Distance × tan θ

- **Shadow problems:**
  - tan θ = Height of object / Length of shadow

- **Two angles from same point:**
  If angles of elevation of top and bottom of a flagstaff on a building are α and β:
  - Height of building = d × tan β
  - Height of flagstaff = d × (tan α − tan β)

### 6. Maximum and Minimum Values

| Expression | Min | Max |
|-----------|-----|-----|
| sin θ, cos θ | −1 | 1 |
| sin²θ, cos²θ | 0 | 1 |
| a sin θ + b cos θ | −√(a²+b²) | √(a²+b²) |
| a sin²θ + b cos²θ | min(a,b) | max(a,b) |

## 💡 Tips, Tricks & Shortcuts

### Tip 1: Memorize the Standard Table
You CANNOT solve trig questions without knowing sin/cos/tan values for 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°.

### Tip 2: Complementary Angle Trick
If you see sin 37°, think cos 53°. If tan 28°, think cot 62°. This simplifies many expressions.

### Tip 3: Identity Simplification Pattern
Most RRB NTPC trig questions can be solved by:
1. Convert everything to sin and cos
2. Apply sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
3. Simplify

### Tip 4: Height & Distance — Draw the Figure!
Always draw a right triangle. Label the angle, height, and distance. Apply tan = opposite/adjacent.

### Tip 5: Common Values to Remember
- tan 45° = 1 (height = distance when angle = 45°)
- tan 30° = 1/√3 ≈ 0.577
- tan 60° = √3 ≈ 1.732
- √3 ≈ 1.732, 1/√3 ≈ 0.577, √2 ≈ 1.414

## 📝 RRB NTPC Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

### PYQ 1 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Find the value of sin²30° + cos²60°.**

(A) 0 (B) 1/2 (C) 1 (D) 3/4

**Answer: (B) 1/2**
*Solution:* sin²30° + cos²60° = (1/2)² + (1/2)² = 1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2.

---

### PYQ 2 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**If tan θ = 3/4, find sin θ.**

(A) 3/5 (B) 4/5 (C) 3/4 (D) 5/3

**Answer: (A) 3/5**
*Solution:* tan θ = 3/4 → opposite = 3, adjacent = 4, hypotenuse = 5. sin θ = 3/5.

---

### PYQ 3 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**What is the value of sin 60° × cos 30° + sin 30° × cos 60°?**

(A) 0 (B) 1 (C) 1/2 (D) √3/2

**Answer: (B) 1**
*Solution:* This is sin(60° + 30°) = sin 90° = 1. Or: (√3/2)(√3/2) + (1/2)(1/2) = 3/4 + 1/4 = 1.

---

### PYQ 4 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**A ladder 10 m long leans against a wall making an angle of 60° with the ground. How high does it reach?**

(A) 5 m (B) 5√3 m (C) 10√3 m (D) 5/√3 m

**Answer: (B) 5√3 m**
*Solution:* sin 60° = height/10 → height = 10 × √3/2 = 5√3 m.

---

### PYQ 5 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Find the value of: (sin²45° + cos²45°) / (tan²60° − tan²45°)**

(A) 1/2 (B) 1 (C) 2 (D) 1/3

**Answer: (A) 1/2**
*Solution:* Numerator = 1. Denominator = 3 − 1 = 2. Answer = 1/2.

---

### PYQ 6 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from a point 100 m away is 45°. Find the height.**

(A) 50 m (B) 100 m (C) 100√3 m (D) 200 m

**Answer: (B) 100 m**
*Solution:* tan 45° = h/100 → 1 = h/100 → h = 100 m.

---

### PYQ 7 (RRB NTPC 2020)
**If cos θ = 5/13, find tan θ.**

(A) 12/5 (B) 5/12 (C) 12/13 (D) 13/12

**Answer: (A) 12/5**
*Solution:* cos θ = 5/13 → adj=5, hyp=13, opp=12 (by Pythagoras). tan θ = 12/5.

---

### PYQ 8 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Find: sec²60° − tan²60°**

(A) 0 (B) 1 (C) 2 (D) 3

**Answer: (B) 1**
*Solution:* By identity: sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 (always!).

---

### PYQ 9 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**A tree casts a shadow of 15 m when the angle of elevation of the sun is 60°. Find the height of the tree.**

(A) 15√3 m (B) 15/√3 m (C) 30 m (D) 10√3 m

**Answer: (A) 15√3 m**
*Solution:* tan 60° = h/15 → √3 = h/15 → h = 15√3 m.

---

### PYQ 10 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**If sin(A + B) = 1 and cos(A − B) = √3/2, find A and B.**

(A) A=60°, B=30° (B) A=45°, B=45° (C) A=50°, B=40° (D) A=75°, B=15°

**Answer: (A) A=60°, B=30°**
*Solution:* sin(A+B) = 1 → A+B = 90°. cos(A−B) = √3/2 → A−B = 30°. Solving: A = 60°, B = 30°.
