## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Basic Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Cost Price (CP)** | The price at which an article is purchased |
| **Selling Price (SP)** | The price at which an article is sold |
| **Profit (Gain)** | When SP > CP → Profit = SP − CP |
| **Loss** | When CP > SP → Loss = CP − SP |
| **Marked Price (MP)** | The listed/label price of an article (also called List Price) |
| **Discount** | Reduction given on Marked Price → Discount = MP − SP |

### 2. Profit and Loss Percentage

**Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100**

**Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100**

> ⚠️ **Key Point:** Profit% and Loss% are ALWAYS calculated on **Cost Price (CP)**, unless stated otherwise.

### 3. Relationship Formulas

**When there is Profit:**
- SP = CP × (100 + P%) / 100
- CP = SP × 100 / (100 + P%)

**When there is Loss:**
- SP = CP × (100 − L%) / 100
- CP = SP × 100 / (100 − L%)

### 4. Discount and Marked Price

- **Discount = MP − SP**
- **Discount % = (Discount / MP) × 100**
- **SP = MP × (100 − D%) / 100**

**Relationship between MP, CP, and Profit:**
If markup is m% and discount is d%, then:
**Profit% = m − d − (m × d)/100**

### 5. Successive Discounts

If two successive discounts of d₁% and d₂% are given:
**Equivalent single discount = d₁ + d₂ − (d₁ × d₂)/100**

**Example:** Successive discounts of 20% and 10%:
- Equivalent = 20 + 10 − (20 × 10)/100 = 30 − 2 = **28%**
- This is NOT 30%!

### 6. Dishonest Dealer

A dealer uses a false weight and claims to sell at cost price:

**Profit % = [(True Weight − False Weight) / False Weight] × 100**

**Example:** A shopkeeper uses 900g weight instead of 1000g:
- Profit% = (1000 − 900)/900 × 100 = 100/900 × 100 = **11.11%**

### 7. When Two Articles Are Sold at Same SP

**Case 1: One at x% profit and other at x% loss**
- There is ALWAYS a **loss**
- **Loss% = x²/100 %**
- **Loss = (x² × SP) / (100² − x²) × 2**

**Example:** Two articles sold at ₹990 each, one at 10% profit and other at 10% loss:
- Loss% = (10)²/100 = 1%

### 8. When CP of Two Articles is Same

If one is sold at x% profit and other at x% loss:
- **Net effect = No profit, No loss** (they cancel out!)

### 9. Cost Price of x Articles = Selling Price of y Articles

**Profit% = [(x − y) / y] × 100** (when x > y → profit)
**Loss% = [(y − x) / x] × 100** (when y > x — use y as base... actually: Loss% = [(y − x) / y] × 100... )

Correct formula: **Profit or Loss % = [(x − y) / y] × 100**
- If positive → Profit
- If negative → Loss

**Example:** CP of 20 articles = SP of 16 articles
- Profit% = (20 − 16)/16 × 100 = 4/16 × 100 = **25% profit**

### 10. Selling at Different Prices

If by selling at price P₁ there's a loss of x%, and selling at price P₂ gives a profit of y%:

**CP = (P₁ × (100 + y) − P₂ × (100 − x)) is not right...**

Better approach: 
- From P₁: CP = P₁ × 100/(100 − x)
- Set up equation using the second condition to find unknowns.

**Shortcut:** If selling at SP₁ gives x% loss and selling at SP₂ gives y% profit:
- **CP = (SP₁ × 100) / (100 − x) = (SP₂ × 100) / (100 + y)**

## 📐 All Formulas — Quick Reference

| Formula | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| Profit | SP − CP |
| Loss | CP − SP |
| Profit % | (Profit/CP) × 100 |
| Loss % | (Loss/CP) × 100 |
| SP (profit) | CP × (100 + P%)/100 |
| SP (loss) | CP × (100 − L%)/100 |
| CP from SP (profit) | SP × 100/(100 + P%) |
| CP from SP (loss) | SP × 100/(100 − L%) |
| Discount | MP − SP |
| Discount % | (Discount/MP) × 100 |
| SP from MP | MP × (100 − D%)/100 |
| Markup + Discount → Profit% | m − d − md/100 |
| Successive discounts | d₁ + d₂ − d₁d₂/100 |
| Dishonest dealer | (True − False)/False × 100 |
| Same SP, x% P & x% L | Loss% = x²/100 |
| CP of x = SP of y | P% = (x−y)/y × 100 |

## 💡 Tips, Tricks & Shortcuts

### Trick 1: Use Multiplying Factors
- 20% profit → SP = CP × 1.2
- 15% loss → SP = CP × 0.85
- 25% discount → SP = MP × 0.75

### Trick 2: "CP of m = SP of n" Shortcut
Just remember: **Profit% = (m−n)/n × 100**

### Trick 3: Same SP with Profit & Loss
When selling two items at same price with same %, there's always a **NET LOSS = x²/100 %**

### Trick 4: Quick MP Calculation
If profit is 20% and discount is 10%:
- Profit% = 20 − 10 − (200/100) = 8%
- So actual profit is 8%, not 10%!

### Trick 5: Dishonest Dealer with Markup
If dealer marks up by m% AND uses false weight:
- **Total Profit% = [(100 + m%) × True Weight / (100 × False Weight) − 1] × 100**

### Common Traps:
- ❌ Successive discounts of 10% and 20% ≠ 30% discount
- ❌ Profit% is on CP, Discount% is on MP — don't mix bases!
- ❌ Same SP with same P% and L% always gives LOSS, never "no change"

## 📝 RRB NTPC Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

### PYQ 1 (RRB NTPC 2021 — Phase 1)
**A shopkeeper bought an article for ₹800 and sold it at a profit of 25%. What is the selling price?**

(A) ₹900 (B) ₹950 (C) ₹1000 (D) ₹1050

**Answer: (C) ₹1000**

*Solution:* SP = 800 × (100 + 25)/100 = 800 × 1.25 = ₹1000.

---

### PYQ 2 (RRB NTPC 2021 — Phase 2)
**By selling an article for ₹450, a person loses 10%. At what price should he sell to gain 10%?**

(A) ₹500 (B) ₹540 (C) ₹550 (D) ₹495

**Answer: (C) ₹550**

*Solution:* CP = 450 × 100/90 = ₹500. SP for 10% profit = 500 × 1.1 = ₹550.

---

### PYQ 3 (RRB NTPC 2021 — Phase 3)
**The cost price of 25 articles is equal to the selling price of 20 articles. Find the profit percentage.**

(A) 20% (B) 25% (C) 30% (D) 15%

**Answer: (B) 25%**

*Solution:* P% = (25 − 20)/20 × 100 = 5/20 × 100 = 25%.

---

### PYQ 4 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**A dealer marks his goods 40% above cost price and allows a discount of 10%. Find his gain percent.**

(A) 30% (B) 26% (C) 28% (D) 25%

**Answer: (B) 26%**

*Solution:* Gain% = 40 − 10 − (40 × 10)/100 = 30 − 4 = 26%.

### PYQ 5 (RRB NTPC 2021 — Phase 4)
**A person sold two articles at ₹1200 each. On one he gained 20% and on the other he lost 20%. What is the overall profit or loss?**

(A) 4% loss (B) No profit no loss (C) 4% profit (D) 2% loss

**Answer: (A) 4% loss**

*Solution:* Same SP with same %: Loss% = x²/100 = 400/100 = 4% loss.

### PYQ 6 (RRB NTPC 2021 — Phase 5)
**A shopkeeper gives two successive discounts of 10% and 20% on an article. What is the equivalent single discount?**

(A) 30% (B) 28% (C) 25% (D) 32%

**Answer: (B) 28%**

*Solution:* Equivalent = 10 + 20 − (10 × 20)/100 = 30 − 2 = 28%.

### PYQ 9 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**An article is sold at 20% profit. If both the cost price and selling price are ₹100 less, the profit would be 4% more. Find the cost price.**

(A) ₹500 (B) ₹600 (C) ₹400 (D) ₹550

**Answer: (A) ₹500**

*Solution:* Let CP = x. SP = 1.2x. New CP = x − 100, New SP = 1.2x − 100.
New profit% = 24%. So: 1.2x − 100 = (x − 100) × 1.24 = 1.24x − 124
→ 1.2x − 100 = 1.24x − 124 → 24 = 0.04x → x = 600. 
Checking: SP = 720. New: CP = 500, SP = 620. Profit = 120/500 × 100 = 24%. ✓ **Answer: (B) ₹600**

### PYQ 10 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**A man purchases 11 oranges for ₹10 and sells 10 oranges for ₹11. What is his profit or loss percentage?**

(A) 21% profit (B) 11% profit (C) 10% profit (D) 20% profit

**Answer: (A) 21% profit**

*Solution:* CP of 1 orange = 10/11. SP of 1 orange = 11/10. Profit = 11/10 − 10/11 = (121 − 100)/110 = 21/110. P% = (21/110)/(10/11) × 100 = (21/110) × (11/10) × 100 = 21%.

### PYQ 11 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**A trader mixes 26 kg of rice at ₹20/kg with 30 kg of rice at ₹36/kg and sells the mixture at ₹30/kg. His profit percentage is:**

(A) 5% (B) 8% (C) 10% (D) 12%

**Answer: (A) 5%**

*Solution:* Total CP = 26 × 20 + 30 × 36 = 520 + 1080 = 1600. Total SP = 56 × 30 = 1680. P% = 80/1600 × 100 = 5%.

### PYQ 12 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**A sells a bicycle to B at 20% profit, B sells to C at 10% loss. If C pays ₹1080, what did A pay for the bicycle?**

(A) ₹1000 (B) ₹900 (C) ₹950 (D) ₹850

**Answer: (A) ₹1000**

*Solution:* Let A's CP = x. B's CP = 1.2x. C's CP = 1.2x × 0.9 = 1.08x = 1080. So x = 1000.

## 🎯 Exam Strategy & Revision Tips

1. **Memorize base formulas** — SP = CP(100±P%)/100 and the discount relation
2. **Practice "CP of m = SP of n" type** — Very common in RRB NTPC
3. **Dishonest dealer** questions are frequent — Learn the formula
4. **Same SP with P% and L%** — Remember: always NET LOSS = x²/100
5. **Successive discounts** ≠ sum of discounts — Use the formula
6. **Convert everything to multiplying factors** for speed
7. **Expected time per question:** 60–90 seconds
8. **Don't confuse base:** Profit% → on CP | Discount% → on MP
