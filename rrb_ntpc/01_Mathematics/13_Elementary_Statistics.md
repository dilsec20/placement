## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Mean (Average)

**Arithmetic Mean = Sum of all observations / Number of observations**

**Mean = Σxᵢ / n**

**Example:** Data: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 → Mean = 75/5 = 15

#### Weighted Mean
**Weighted Mean = Σ(wᵢ × xᵢ) / Σwᵢ**

#### Combined Mean
If Group 1 has n₁ items with mean x̄₁ and Group 2 has n₂ items with mean x̄₂:
**Combined Mean = (n₁x̄₁ + n₂x̄₂) / (n₁ + n₂)**

#### Mean of Grouped Data (Frequency Distribution)

| Method | Formula |
|--------|---------|
| Direct | Σ(fᵢxᵢ) / Σfᵢ |
| Assumed Mean | A + Σ(fᵢdᵢ)/Σfᵢ, where dᵢ = xᵢ − A |
| Step Deviation | A + [Σ(fᵢuᵢ)/Σfᵢ] × h, where uᵢ = dᵢ/h |

#### Properties of Mean
- If each value is increased/decreased by k, mean increases/decreases by k
- If each value is multiplied/divided by k, mean is multiplied/divided by k
- Sum of deviations from mean = 0

### 2. Median

**Median** = Middle value when data is arranged in ascending/descending order.

**For ungrouped data:**
- If n is odd: Median = ((n+1)/2)th term
- If n is even: Median = Average of (n/2)th and (n/2 + 1)th terms

**Example (odd):** 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 → n=5, Median = 3rd term = 7
**Example (even):** 2, 4, 6, 8 → n=4, Median = (4+6)/2 = 5

**For grouped data:**
Median = l + [(n/2 − cf) / f] × h

Where: l = lower limit of median class, n = total frequency, cf = cumulative frequency before median class, f = frequency of median class, h = class width

### 3. Mode

**Mode** = Most frequently occurring value.

**For ungrouped data:** The value that appears most often.
- Data: 2, 3, 3, 4, 5, 3, 6 → Mode = 3 (appears 3 times)

**Types:**
- Unimodal: One mode
- Bimodal: Two modes
- Multimodal: More than two modes
- No mode: All values appear equally

**For grouped data:**
Mode = l + [(f₁ − f₀) / (2f₁ − f₀ − f₂)] × h

Where: l = lower limit of modal class, f₁ = frequency of modal class, f₀ = frequency of class before, f₂ = frequency of class after, h = class width

**Empirical Relationship:**
**Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean**

### 4. Range

**Range = Maximum value − Minimum value**

Simple measure of spread/dispersion.

### 5. Standard Deviation & Variance

**Variance (σ²) = Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / n**

**Standard Deviation (σ) = √Variance**

**Shortcut formula:** σ² = (Σxᵢ²/n) − (Σxᵢ/n)²

**Coefficient of Variation = (σ/x̄) × 100%**

### 6. Data Interpretation Basics

**Types of data representation:**
- Bar Graph
- Pie Chart (angles proportional, total = 360°)
- Line Graph
- Histogram (for continuous data)
- Frequency Polygon

**Pie Chart calculations:**
- Angle for a component = (Value / Total) × 360°
- Value = (Angle / 360°) × Total

## 💡 Tips, Tricks & Shortcuts

### Tip 1: Mean Shortcut — Assumed Mean Method
Choose a convenient number as assumed mean (A), find deviations, then Mean = A + (sum of deviations)/n.

### Tip 2: When One Value Changes
If one value changes from a to b in a dataset of n items:
New Mean = Old Mean + (b − a)/n

### Tip 3: Finding Missing Value
If mean is given and one value is missing: Missing value = n × Mean − (sum of known values)

### Tip 4: Median for Even Count
Quickly sort, find the two middle values, average them.

### Tip 5: Mode = 3Median − 2Mean
Use this when you know two of the three and need the third.

## 📝 RRB NTPC Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

### PYQ 1 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Find the mean of first 10 natural numbers.**

(A) 5 (B) 5.5 (C) 6 (D) 10

**Answer: (B) 5.5**
*Solution:* Sum = 10×11/2 = 55. Mean = 55/10 = 5.5.

---

### PYQ 2 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**The mean of 5 numbers is 20. If one number is excluded, the mean becomes 18. Find the excluded number.**

(A) 22 (B) 24 (C) 28 (D) 30

**Answer: (C) 28**
*Solution:* Total = 5 × 20 = 100. New total = 4 × 18 = 72. Excluded = 100 − 72 = 28.

---

### PYQ 3 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Find the median of: 3, 8, 5, 2, 9, 4, 7**

(A) 4 (B) 5 (C) 6 (D) 7

**Answer: (B) 5**
*Solution:* Sorted: 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9. n=7 (odd). Median = 4th term = 5.

---

### PYQ 4 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**Find the mode of: 2, 3, 4, 3, 5, 3, 6, 3, 7**

(A) 2 (B) 3 (C) 4 (D) 5

**Answer: (B) 3**
*Solution:* 3 appears 4 times (most frequent). Mode = 3.

---

### PYQ 5 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**The mean of 10 observations is 15. If each observation is increased by 5, the new mean is:**

(A) 15 (B) 20 (C) 25 (D) 10

**Answer: (B) 20**
*Solution:* New mean = 15 + 5 = 20.

---

### PYQ 6 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**If the mean of a, b, c, d, e is 28, find the mean of a+10, b+10, c+10, d+10, e+10.**

(A) 28 (B) 38 (C) 33 (D) 30

**Answer: (B) 38**
*Solution:* Each value increased by 10, so mean increases by 10: 28 + 10 = 38.

---

### PYQ 7 (RRB NTPC 2020)
**The range of data 15, 8, 23, 41, 6, 37 is:**

(A) 33 (B) 35 (C) 37 (D) 41

**Answer: (B) 35**
*Solution:* Range = 41 − 6 = 35.

---

### PYQ 8 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**If the median of x/5, x/4, x/2, x, x/3 (where x > 0) is 8, find x.**

(A) 20 (B) 24 (C) 32 (D) 40

**Answer: (B) 24**
*Solution:* Arrange: x/5, x/4, x/3, x/2, x. Median = x/3 = 8 → x = 24.

---

### PYQ 9 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**In a pie chart, the angle for a sector representing 25% is:**

(A) 25° (B) 60° (C) 90° (D) 120°

**Answer: (C) 90°**
*Solution:* Angle = 25% × 360° = 0.25 × 360 = 90°.

---

### PYQ 10 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**The mean and mode of a data set are 24 and 12 respectively. Find the median.**

(A) 18 (B) 20 (C) 22 (D) 16

**Answer: (B) 20**
*Solution:* Mode = 3Median − 2Mean → 12 = 3M − 48 → 3M = 60 → M = 20.
