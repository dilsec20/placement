## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Basic Formula

**Speed = Distance / Time**

**Distance = Speed × Time**

**Time = Distance / Speed**

### 2. Unit Conversions (CRITICAL!)

| Conversion | Formula |
|-----------|---------|
| km/h to m/s | Multiply by **5/18** |
| m/s to km/h | Multiply by **18/5** |
| km/h to m/min | Multiply by **1000/60** |

**Example:** 72 km/h = 72 × 5/18 = **20 m/s**

**Quick trick:** For km/h to m/s, just multiply the number by 5 and divide by 18.
- 36 km/h = 10 m/s
- 54 km/h = 15 m/s
- 72 km/h = 20 m/s
- 90 km/h = 25 m/s

### 3. Average Speed

**Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time**

> ⚠️ Average speed ≠ Average of speeds!

**Case 1: Same distance at speeds a and b:**
**Average Speed = 2ab / (a + b)**

**Case 2: Same time at speeds a and b:**
**Average Speed = (a + b) / 2**

**Example:** A man goes to office at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h.
- Average speed = 2 × 40 × 60 / (40 + 60) = 4800/100 = **48 km/h** (not 50!)

### 4. Relative Speed

**Same direction:** Relative speed = |S₁ − S₂|

**Opposite direction:** Relative speed = S₁ + S₂

### 5. Train Problems (🔥 Very Important for RRB!)

**Train passing a stationary object (pole, person, signal):**
- Distance = Length of train
- Time to pass = Length of train / Speed of train

**Train passing a platform/bridge:**
- Distance = Length of train + Length of platform
- Time = (L_train + L_platform) / Speed

**Two trains crossing each other (opposite directions):**
- Distance = L₁ + L₂
- Relative speed = S₁ + S₂
- Time = (L₁ + L₂) / (S₁ + S₂)

**Two trains crossing each other (same direction):**
- Distance = L₁ + L₂
- Relative speed = |S₁ − S₂|
- Time = (L₁ + L₂) / |S₁ − S₂|

**Train passing a person on another train:**
- Distance = Length of the train that passes (only one train's length)
- Use relative speed

### 6. Boats and Streams

- **Downstream speed** = Speed of boat + Speed of stream = u + v
- **Upstream speed** = Speed of boat − Speed of stream = u − v
- **Speed of boat in still water** = (Downstream + Upstream) / 2 = (a + b) / 2
- **Speed of stream** = (Downstream − Upstream) / 2 = (a − b) / 2

Where a = downstream speed, b = upstream speed

### 7. Races

**In a race of d meters, A beats B by x meters:**
- When A finishes d meters, B has covered (d − x) meters
- Ratio of speeds: A/B = d/(d − x)

**A beats B by t seconds:**
- A finishes the race t seconds before B

**A gives B a head start of x meters:**
- B starts x meters ahead of A

### 8. Problems with Changing Speed

If a person covers part of the journey at one speed and the rest at another:
- Use: Total Time = d₁/s₁ + d₂/s₂
- Or use the concept of "would have reached x minutes earlier/later"

### 9. Meeting Problems (Two people starting from opposite ends)

Two people A and B start from points P and Q at speeds a and b:
- **Time to meet = Distance(PQ) / (a + b)**
- **Meeting point from P = a × Time**

### 10. Circular Track Problems

Two people running in a circle of circumference C:
- **Same direction: Time to meet again = C / |S₁ − S₂|**
- **Opposite direction: Time to meet again = C / (S₁ + S₂)**

## 💡 Tips, Tricks & Shortcuts

### Trick 1: Memorize Common km/h ↔ m/s Conversions
| km/h | m/s |
|------|-----|
| 18 | 5 |
| 36 | 10 |
| 54 | 15 |
| 72 | 20 |
| 90 | 25 |
| 108 | 30 |

### Trick 2: Train Problems — Always Add Lengths
When two objects are crossing, distance = sum of both lengths. When passing a point, distance = train length only.

### Trick 3: "Late & Early" Problems
If a person walks at speed S₁, he's late by t₁ min. At speed S₂, he's early by t₂ min.
- **Distance = S₁ × S₂ × (t₁ + t₂) / (S₂ − S₁)**
- (Convert minutes to hours if speeds are in km/h)

### Trick 4: Proportionality
- Distance ∝ Speed (time constant)
- Distance ∝ Time (speed constant)
- Speed ∝ 1/Time (distance constant)

### Trick 5: Boats — Quick Speed Calculation
If downstream time = T₁ and upstream time = T₂ for same distance:
- Speed of boat / Speed of stream = (T₂ + T₁) / (T₂ − T₁)

## 📝 RRB NTPC Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

### PYQ 1 (RRB NTPC 2021 — Phase 1)
**A train 150m long passes a pole in 10 seconds. Find the speed of the train in km/h.**

(A) 45 (B) 54 (C) 60 (D) 50

**Answer: (B) 54 km/h**

*Solution:* Speed = 150/10 = 15 m/s = 15 × 18/5 = 54 km/h.

---

### PYQ 2 (RRB NTPC 2021 — Phase 2)
**A train 200m long crosses a platform of 300m in 25 seconds. Find the speed of the train.**

(A) 72 km/h (B) 60 km/h (C) 80 km/h (D) 54 km/h

**Answer: (A) 72 km/h**

*Solution:* Speed = (200+300)/25 = 500/25 = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h.

---

### PYQ 3 (RRB NTPC 2021 — Phase 3)
**Two trains of lengths 120m and 180m running in opposite directions cross each other in 12 seconds. If the speed of the first train is 80 km/h, find the speed of the second train.**

(A) 70 km/h (B) 60 km/h (C) 100 km/h (D) 10 km/h

**Answer: (C) 100 km/h**

*Solution:* Total distance = 120 + 180 = 300m. Relative speed = 300/12 = 25 m/s = 90 km/h. Speed₁ = 80 km/h. But 80 + Speed₂ = 90? That gives 10 km/h... Let me recheck: 25 m/s = 25 × 18/5 = 90 km/h. So S₁ + S₂ = 90. S₂ = 90 − 80 = 10 km/h. **Answer: (D) 10 km/h**

---

### PYQ 4 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**A boat goes 24 km upstream and 28 km downstream in 6 hours. It also goes 30 km upstream and 21 km downstream in 6.5 hours. Find the speed of the boat in still water.**

(A) 10 km/h (B) 12 km/h (C) 8 km/h (D) 14 km/h

**Answer: (A) 10 km/h**

*Solution:* Let upstream speed = u, downstream speed = d. 24/u + 28/d = 6 ... (1). 30/u + 21/d = 6.5 ... (2). Let 1/u = x, 1/d = y. 24x + 28y = 6, 30x + 21y = 6.5. Solving: multiply (1) by 3 and (2) by 4: 72x + 84y = 18, 120x + 84y = 26. Subtract: 48x = 8 → x = 1/6 → u = 6. From (1): 24/6 + 28y = 6 → 28y = 2 → y = 1/14 → d = 14. Still water speed = (6+14)/2 = 10 km/h.

### PYQ 5 (RRB NTPC 2021 — Phase 4)
**A man covers a distance of 600m in 5 minutes. Find his speed in km/h.**

(A) 7.2 (B) 8.4 (C) 6.4 (D) 7.6

**Answer: (A) 7.2 km/h**

*Solution:* Speed = 600/(5×60) = 2 m/s = 2 × 18/5 = 7.2 km/h.

---

### PYQ 6 (RRB NTPC 2021 — Phase 5)
**A person travels from A to B at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h. Find the average speed for the whole journey.**

(A) 50 km/h (B) 48 km/h (C) 45 km/h (D) 52 km/h

**Answer: (B) 48 km/h**

*Solution:* Same distance: Avg speed = 2 × 40 × 60/(40 + 60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/h.

---

### PYQ 7 (RRB NTPC 2020)
**Two trains running in the same direction have speeds of 90 km/h and 70 km/h. The faster train passes a man sitting in the slower train in 36 seconds. Find the length of the faster train.**

(A) 200m (B) 180m (C) 150m (D) 250m

**Answer: (A) 200m**

*Solution:* Relative speed = 90 − 70 = 20 km/h = 20 × 5/18 = 50/9 m/s. Length = 50/9 × 36 = 200m.

---

### PYQ 8 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**The speed of a boat in still water is 15 km/h and the speed of the stream is 5 km/h. How long will it take to go 40 km upstream?**

(A) 2 hrs (B) 3 hrs (C) 4 hrs (D) 2.5 hrs

**Answer: (C) 4 hrs**

*Solution:* Upstream speed = 15 − 5 = 10 km/h. Time = 40/10 = 4 hours.

---

### PYQ 9 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**In a 1000m race, A beats B by 50 meters. In a 500m race, by how many meters will A beat B?**

(A) 25m (B) 20m (C) 30m (D) 50m

**Answer: (A) 25m**

*Solution:* Speed ratio A:B = 1000:950 = 20:19. In 500m race: When A finishes 500m, B covers 500 × 19/20 = 475m. A beats B by 25m.

---

### PYQ 10 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**A train takes 10 seconds to cross a pole and 20 seconds to cross a 200m long platform. Find the length of the train.**

(A) 100m (B) 150m (C) 200m (D) 250m

**Answer: (C) 200m**

*Solution:* Let train length = L. Speed = L/10. Also speed = (L+200)/20. So L/10 = (L+200)/20 → 2L = L + 200 → L = 200m.

---

### PYQ 11 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**A man walks at 5 km/h and reaches office 7 min late. If he walks at 6 km/h, he reaches 5 min early. What is the distance to his office?**

(A) 4 km (B) 6 km (C) 5 km (D) 3 km

**Answer: (B) 6 km**

*Solution:* Time difference = 7 + 5 = 12 min = 1/5 hr. D = S₁S₂(t₁+t₂)/(S₂−S₁) = 5 × 6 × (1/5) / (6−5) = 6/1 = 6 km.

## 🎯 Exam Strategy & Revision Tips

1. **Memorize km/h ↔ m/s conversions** — Used in almost every question
2. **Train problems are GUARANTEED** in RRB NTPC — Practice extensively
3. **Average speed ≠ average of speeds** — Common trap question
4. **Boats & Streams** = Just two extra formulas on top of basic speed
5. **"Late & Early" formula** is a direct solver — Don't waste time with algebra
6. **Expected time per question:** 60–120 seconds
7. **Draw diagrams** for complex meeting/race problems
