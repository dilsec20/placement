## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Dictionary Order (Alphabetical Arrangement)

Arrange words as they would appear in a **dictionary** — compare letter by letter from the first.

**Example:** Arrange: BREAD, BRAIN, BRICK, BROWN, BLACK
1. B-L-A-C-K → BLA...
2. B-R-A-I-N → BRA...
3. B-R-E-A-D → BRE...
4. B-R-I-C-K → BRI...
5. B-R-O-W-N → BRO...

**Order:** BLACK, BRAIN, BREAD, BRICK, BROWN

**Method:**
1. Compare first letters — group by first letter
2. Within same first letter, compare second letter
3. Continue letter by letter until order is determined
4. A < B < C < ... < Z

### 2. Logical Sequence of Events

Arrange events in their **natural/chronological** order.

**Example:** (1) Seed (2) Flower (3) Plant (4) Fruit (5) Root
**Order:** 1 → 5 → 3 → 2 → 4 (Seed → Root → Plant → Flower → Fruit)

### 3. Sentence Rearrangement

Rearrange jumbled parts of a sentence to form a meaningful sentence.

**Approach:**
1. Find the opening statement (usually introduces the topic)
2. Look for logical flow — cause → effect, general → specific
3. Pronoun references (he, she, it, they) must follow their antecedent
4. Connecting words (however, therefore, moreover) link sentences

### 4. Word Formation from a Given Word

**Example:** How many meaningful words can be formed from the 2nd, 4th, 6th, and 8th letters of COMPUTER?

COMPUTER → C(1) O(2) M(3) P(4) U(5) T(6) E(7) R(8)
Letters: O, P, T, R → Possible words: PORT, TROP... **PORT** is meaningful.

### 5. Reverse/Rearranged Alphabets

**Position of letters:**
A=1, B=2, C=3... Z=26

**Reverse positions:**
Z=1, Y=2, X=3... A=26

**Trick for reverse position:** Reverse position = 27 − Normal position

### 6. Alphabetical Position-Based Problems

**Example:** Which letter is 5th to the right of 12th letter from the left?
12th from left = L. 5th to right of L = Q. **Answer: Q**

**Example:** Which letter is exactly in the middle of J and R?
J=10, R=18. Middle = (10+18)/2 = 14 = N. **Answer: N**

## 📐 Key Concepts

| Concept | Method |
|---------|--------|
| Dictionary order | Compare letter by letter, left to right |
| Event sequence | Think of natural chronological order |
| Reverse alphabet position | 27 − Position |
| Middle letter | Average of two positions |
| nth from right | Position = 27 − n |

## 💡 Tips & Shortcuts

1. **Dictionary order:** Focus on the FIRST point of difference between words
2. **Event sequence:** Think of the real-world process/lifecycle
3. **Reverse alphabet:** EJOTY = 5,10,15,20,25 — memorize these positions
4. **Word formation:** First list all letters, then try common short words

## 📝 RRB NTPC Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

### PYQ 1 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Arrange in dictionary order: 1.Range 2.Random 3.Rank 4.Rapid 5.Rally**
(A) 5,2,3,1,4 (B) 5,2,1,3,4 (C) 2,5,1,3,4 (D) 5,1,2,3,4
**Answer: (B) 5,2,1,3,4** → Rally, Random, Range, Rank, Rapid

### PYQ 2 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Arrange in logical order: 1.Diagnosis 2.Illness 3.Treatment 4.Recovery 5.Doctor**
(A) 2,5,1,3,4 (B) 5,2,1,3,4 (C) 2,1,5,3,4 (D) 1,2,3,4,5
**Answer: (A) 2,5,1,3,4** → Illness → Doctor → Diagnosis → Treatment → Recovery

### PYQ 3 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**If TROUBLE is written as ELBUORT, how is JOURNEY written?**
(A) YENRUOJ (B) YRNUOEJ (C) YERNUOJ (D) YENUORJ
**Answer: (A) YENRUOJ** → Word reversed: JOURNEY → YENRUOJ

### PYQ 4 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**Which letter is 7th to the left of 16th letter from right in the English alphabet?**
16th from right = K (26−16+1=11th from left = K). 7th to left of K = D.
**Answer: D**

### PYQ 5 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Arrange in dictionary order: 1.Pearl 2.Peach 3.Peace 4.Peak 5.Pear**
(A) 2,3,4,5,1 (B) 2,3,4,1,5 (C) 3,2,4,5,1 (D) 2,4,3,5,1
**Answer: (A) 2,3,4,5,1** → Peach, Peace, Peak, Pear, Pearl

### PYQ 6 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Arrange: 1.Country 2.Furniture 3.Chair 4.Wood 5.Forest**
(A) 1,5,4,2,3 (B) 1,5,4,3,2 (C) 5,1,4,3,2 (D) 1,5,3,4,2
**Answer: (A) 1,5,4,2,3** → Country → Forest → Wood → Furniture → Chair

### PYQ 7 (RRB NTPC 2020)
**How many meaningful English words can be formed from the letters A, E, R, T using each letter once?**
(A) 1 (B) 2 (C) 3 (D) 4
**Answer: (C) 3** → RATE, TEAR, TARE (also ARE, EAR, ERA with 3 letters, but question says all 4 letters)

### PYQ 8 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Arrange: 1.Paragraph 2.Word 3.Sentence 4.Letter 5.Chapter 6.Book**
(A) 4,2,3,1,5,6 (B) 4,2,1,3,5,6 (C) 2,4,3,1,5,6 (D) 4,3,2,1,5,6
**Answer: (A) 4,2,3,1,5,6** → Letter → Word → Sentence → Paragraph → Chapter → Book

### PYQ 9 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**Which letter is exactly midway between the 8th and 18th letters from the left?**
8th=H, 18th=R. Midway = (8+18)/2 = 13th = M.
**Answer: M**

### PYQ 10 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Arrange in dictionary order: 1.Camel 2.Castle 3.Candy 4.Candle 5.Camera**
(A) 1,5,4,3,2 (B) 1,5,3,4,2 (C) 5,1,3,4,2 (D) 5,1,4,3,2
**Answer: (B) 1,5,3,4,2** → Camel, Camera, Candy, Candle, Castle
