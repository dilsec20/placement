# 🔍 Similarities and Differences (Classification/Odd One Out) — RRB NTPC

---

| Aspect | Details |

| **Topic** | Similarities, Differences & Classification |

In these questions, you identify the **odd one out** from a group, or classify items based on a common property.

---

## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Types of Classification Questions

#### A. Word-Based (Odd One Out)
Find the word that doesn't belong to the group.

**Example:** (A) Apple (B) Mango (C) Potato (D) Banana
**Answer: (C) Potato** — It's a vegetable; rest are fruits.

**Common categories tested:**
- Fruits vs Vegetables
- Rivers vs Mountains vs Cities
- Mammals vs Reptiles vs Birds
- Planets vs Stars
- Musical instruments (string vs percussion vs wind)
- Sports (indoor vs outdoor, team vs individual)
- Vitamins (water-soluble vs fat-soluble)
- Metals vs Non-metals
- Countries vs Capitals vs Continents

#### B. Number-Based
Find the number that doesn't fit the pattern.

**Example:** (A) 2 (B) 3 (C) 5 (D) 9
**Answer: (D) 9** — Rest are prime numbers; 9 is composite.

**Common number patterns:**
| Category | Examples |
|----------|---------|
| Prime numbers | 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13... |
| Perfect squares | 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36... |
| Perfect cubes | 1, 8, 27, 64, 125... |
| Even/Odd | 2,4,6,8 or 1,3,5,7 |
| Divisibility | Divisible by 3, by 7, etc. |
| Sum of digits | Common sum pattern |

#### C. Letter-Based
Find the letter group that doesn't match.

**Example:** (A) BD (B) FH (C) JL (D) MO
**Answer: (D) MO** — All others skip one letter; M to O skips N (correct pattern), but actually checking: B→D skip C, F→H skip G, J→L skip K, M→O skip N — all follow the same pattern. So the odd one would need a different setup.

**Common patterns:** Vowels vs consonants, position-based (sum of positions), gap between letters.

#### D. Figure-Based
Find the figure that doesn't belong. Look for:
- Number of sides
- Rotation/orientation
- Open vs closed figures
- Number of elements inside

### 2. Key Knowledge Areas for Word Classification

**Vitamins:**
- Fat-soluble: A, D, E, K
- Water-soluble: B, C

**Bones:**
- Femur, Tibia, Fibula (leg), Humerus, Radius, Ulna (arm), Skull, Spine

**Planets (order from Sun):**
Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune

**Indian States & Capitals** — Know which doesn't match

**Chemical Elements:** Metals vs Non-metals, Noble gases, Halogens

## 📐 Key Concepts & Methods

### Solving Framework:

1. **Read all four options first**
2. **Find the common link** among three items
3. **Identify the one that breaks the pattern**
4. **If stuck, try different categories** (type, property, use, origin, etc.)

### Common Classification Bases:

| Basis | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Category | Fruits vs Vegetables |
| Geography | Rivers vs Lakes |
| Gender | Male vs Female names |
| Even/Odd | 2,4,6 vs 9 |
| Prime/Composite | 2,3,5 vs 4 |
| Scientific class | Mammals vs Reptiles |
| State/Country | Indian states vs countries |

---

## 💡 Tips, Tricks & Shortcuts

### Tip 1: Think in Categories First
Before analyzing each option, think: What category could these belong to?

### Tip 2: For Numbers — Check These in Order
1. Prime or composite?
2. Perfect square or cube?
3. Even or odd?
4. Sum of digits?
5. Divisibility by specific number?

### Tip 3: GK is Often Needed
These questions test GK indirectly — knowing capitals, rivers, scientists, etc. helps.

### Tip 4: Elimination Works Best
If you can't find the pattern, eliminate the most obviously different one.

### PYQ 2 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Find the odd one: (A) 8 (B) 27 (C) 64 (D) 100**

**Answer: (D) 100** — 8=2³, 27=3³, 64=4³ are perfect cubes; 100 is a perfect square (10²).
