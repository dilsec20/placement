## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Direction Basics

```
            North (N)
              ↑
              |
West (W) ←---+---→ East (E)
              |
              ↓
          South (S)
```

**Between directions:**
- North-East (NE): Between N and E (45°)
- North-West (NW): Between N and W
- South-East (SE): Between S and E
- South-West (SW): Between S and W

### 2. Turn-Based Problems

**Right Turn from each direction:**
| Facing | Turn Right → Now Facing |
|--------|------------------------|
| North | East |
| East | South |
| South | West |
| West | North |

**Left Turn from each direction:**
| Facing | Turn Left → Now Facing |
|--------|------------------------|
| North | West |
| West | South |
| South | East |
| East | North |

**Opposite directions:**
N ↔ S, E ↔ W, NE ↔ SW, NW ↔ SE

### 3. Shadow-Based Direction

**Morning (before 12 PM):** Sun is in the **East** → Shadow falls to the **West**
**Afternoon/Evening (after 12 PM):** Sun is in the **West** → Shadow falls to the **East**
**Noon (12 PM):** Sun is overhead → No shadow (or very short)

**Example:** At 8 AM, a man faces the sun. His shadow falls behind him. Which direction is he facing?
- Morning → Sun in East → He faces **East** → Shadow behind = **West** ✓

### 4. Distance Calculation (Shortest Distance)

When a person moves in multiple directions, find the shortest/straight-line distance using **Pythagoras Theorem**.

**Example:** A walks 3 km North, then 4 km East. Shortest distance from start?
```
    End
     |  \
  3km|   \ ?
     |    \
   Start---→
      4 km
```
Shortest = √(3² + 4²) = √(9+16) = √25 = **5 km**

### 5. Clock-Based Directions

**At 6 PM, if the minute hand points North, hour hand points:**
- 6 o'clock → minute at 12 (N), hour at 6 → **South**

### 6. Map-Based Questions

Given a map with landmarks, find relative positions. Use the compass to determine directions.

## 💡 Tips & Shortcuts

1. **Always draw the path** — Mark each turn with direction
2. **Shadow trick:** Morning = shadow West, Evening = shadow East
3. **Use graph paper mentally** — North = up, East = right
4. **Pythagorean triplets** (3,4,5), (5,12,13) save time
5. **Expected time:** 45–60 seconds

## 📝 RRB NTPC PYQs

### PYQ 1 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**A walks 5 km South, turns left, walks 3 km, turns left, walks 5 km. How far and in which direction is he from the start?**
(A) 3 km East (B) 3 km West (C) 5 km North (D) 8 km East
**Answer: (A) 3 km East**
*Solution:* South 5 → Left(East) 3 → Left(North) 5. Net: 3 km East of start.

### PYQ 2 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**B faces North, turns right, then turns right again, then turns left. Which direction is B facing now?**
(A) North (B) South (C) East (D) West
**Answer: (B) South**
*Solution:* N → Right(E) → Right(S) → Left(E). Wait: N→R→E, E→R→S, S→L→E. Hmm. Let me redo: N→right=E, E→right=S, S→left=E. **Answer: (C) East**. Actually checking again: Turn right = clockwise 90°. N→E→S, then turn left = anticlockwise 90° from S = E. **Answer: (C) East**.

### PYQ 3 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**At 4 PM, a man walks towards the sun. Which direction is he facing?**
(A) East (B) West (C) North (D) South
**Answer: (B) West** → Afternoon sun is in the West.

### PYQ 4 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**A man walks 4 km North, 3 km East, 4 km South. How far is he from starting point?**
(A) 3 km (B) 4 km (C) 5 km (D) 11 km
**Answer: (A) 3 km** → N and S cancel (4−4=0), only 3 km East remains.

### PYQ 5 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**P walks 10 km East, turns left, walks 6 km, turns left, walks 10 km. How far from start?**
(A) 6 km (B) 10 km (C) 16 km (D) 26 km
**Answer: (A) 6 km** → E10 → Left(N)6 → Left(W)10. East-West cancel. 6 km North.

### PYQ 6 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Early morning, A's shadow fell to the left. Which direction was A facing?**
(A) North (B) South (C) East (D) West
**Answer: (B) South** → Morning sun in East, shadow to West. If shadow is to LEFT, the person faces South (left of south-facing = west = shadow side ✓).

### PYQ 7 (RRB NTPC 2020)
**A walks 6 km East, then 8 km North. Find shortest distance from start.**
(A) 14 km (B) 10 km (C) 12 km (D) 8 km
**Answer: (B) 10 km** → √(6²+8²) = √(36+64) = √100 = 10.

### PYQ 8 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Starting from a point, X walks 2 km North, turns right, walks 3 km, turns right, walks 2 km, turns right, walks 8 km. How far from starting point?**
(A) 3 km (B) 5 km (C) 8 km (D) 13 km
**Answer: (B) 5 km** → N2, E3, S2, W8. N-S cancel. E3-W8 = 5 km West. Distance = 5 km.
