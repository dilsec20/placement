## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Types of Graphs/Charts

#### Bar Graph
- Rectangular bars representing data
- Can be vertical or horizontal
- Each bar = one category/period
- Height/length = value

**How to read:** Check Y-axis scale carefully. Each bar's height corresponds to its value.

#### Line Graph
- Points connected by lines showing trends
- X-axis = time/categories, Y-axis = values
- Used for showing changes over time

**How to read:** Look at the slope — steep up = rapid increase, steep down = rapid decrease, flat = constant.

#### Pie Chart
- Circle divided into sectors
- Each sector = percentage or proportion
- Total = 360° = 100%

**Formulas:**
- Angle = (Value/Total) × 360°
- Value = (Angle/360°) × Total
- Percentage = (Angle/360°) × 100%

#### Table
- Data in rows and columns
- Most straightforward — read values directly

#### Histogram
- Similar to bar graph but for continuous data
- No gaps between bars (continuous ranges)

### 2. Key Skills for Data Interpretation

1. **Read the title and labels** — understand what's being shown
2. **Check the scale** — Y-axis might not start from 0!
3. **Compare values** — higher/lower, increase/decrease
4. **Calculate percentages** — (part/whole) × 100
5. **Find averages** — sum/count
6. **Calculate ratios** — simplify to lowest terms
7. **Identify trends** — increasing, decreasing, fluctuating

### 3. Common Calculations

| Calculation | Formula |
|-------------|---------|
| Percentage change | [(New−Old)/Old] × 100 |
| Average | Sum of values / Number of values |
| Ratio | A:B = A/B (simplify) |
| Percentage of total | (Part/Total) × 100 |
| Degree in pie chart | (Value/Total) × 360° |

### 4. Common Question Types

1. **"What is the value of X in year Y?"** — Direct reading
2. **"In which year was X maximum/minimum?"** — Compare all values
3. **"What is the percentage increase from A to B?"** — [(B−A)/A] × 100
4. **"What is the ratio of X to Y?"** — X/Y simplified
5. **"What is the average over N years?"** — Sum/N
6. **"In how many years was X greater than Y?"** — Count instances

---

## 💡 Tips & Shortcuts

1. **Always read scales carefully** — A bar that looks "small" might be large if scale is compressed
2. **Approximate when possible** — Exact calculation isn't always needed
3. **For pie charts:** 25% = 90°, 50% = 180°, 12.5% = 45°
4. **For percentage change:** Use the OLD value as base, not the new one
5. **Look at all answer options first** — They can hint at the level of precision needed

## 📝 RRB NTPC PYQs

### PYQ 1 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**A pie chart shows expenses: Food=90°, Rent=72°, Transport=54°, Others=144°. If total is ₹20,000, what is spent on Food?**
(A) ₹4000 (B) ₹5000 (C) ₹6000 (D) ₹7200
**Answer: (B) ₹5000** → (90/360) × 20000 = 5000.

### PYQ 2 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Bar graph shows production: 2019=400, 2020=350, 2021=500, 2022=450. What is % increase from 2020 to 2021?**
(A) 42.86% (B) 30% (C) 50% (D) 35%
**Answer: (A) 42.86%** → (500−350)/350 × 100 = 150/350 × 100 ≈ 42.86%.

### PYQ 3 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**From the table: Marks of 5 students: A=75, B=82, C=90, D=68, E=85. What is the average?**
(A) 78 (B) 80 (C) 82 (D) 85
**Answer: (B) 80** → (75+82+90+68+85)/5 = 400/5 = 80.

### PYQ 4 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**In a pie chart, if Salary expense is 25%, what is the angle?**
(A) 25° (B) 72° (C) 90° (D) 100°
**Answer: (C) 90°** → 25% of 360° = 90°.

### PYQ 5 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Line graph shows temperature: Mon=30°C, Tue=32°C, Wed=28°C, Thu=35°C, Fri=33°C. On which day was the highest temperature recorded?**
(A) Monday (B) Tuesday (C) Thursday (D) Friday
**Answer: (C) Thursday** → 35°C is the highest.

### PYQ 6 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Production in 2020 was 200 units and in 2021 was 250 units. What is the percentage increase?**
(A) 20% (B) 25% (C) 30% (D) 50%
**Answer: (B) 25%** → (250−200)/200 × 100 = 25%.

### PYQ 7 (RRB NTPC 2020)
**In a pie chart with total 600, if a sector has angle 60°, what is its value?**
(A) 60 (B) 100 (C) 120 (D) 150
**Answer: (B) 100** → (60/360) × 600 = 100.

### PYQ 8 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Ratio of sales in Jan to Feb is 3:5. If Jan sales = 1500, Feb sales = ?**
(A) 2000 (B) 2500 (C) 3000 (D) 3500
**Answer: (B) 2500** → 3/5 = 1500/x → x = 2500.
