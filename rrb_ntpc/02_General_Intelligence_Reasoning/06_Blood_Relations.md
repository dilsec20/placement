## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Standard Relationship Chart

**One Generation UP (Parents' level):**
| Relation | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| Father's/Mother's brother | Uncle (Chacha/Mama) |
| Father's/Mother's sister | Aunt (Bua/Mausi) |
| Father's father | Grandfather (Dada) |
| Father's mother | Grandmother (Dadi) |
| Mother's father | Maternal Grandfather (Nana) |
| Mother's mother | Maternal Grandmother (Nani) |

**Same Generation:**
| Relation | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| Parent's son | Brother |
| Parent's daughter | Sister |
| Uncle/Aunt's son | Cousin (brother) |
| Uncle/Aunt's daughter | Cousin (sister) |
| Husband's/Wife's brother | Brother-in-law |
| Husband's/Wife's sister | Sister-in-law |

**One Generation DOWN:**
| Relation | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| Son's wife | Daughter-in-law |
| Daughter's husband | Son-in-law |
| Brother's son | Nephew |
| Brother's daughter | Niece |
| Sister's son | Nephew |
| Sister's daughter | Niece |

### 2. Key Gender Indicators

| Word | Gender |
|------|--------|
| Father, Brother, Son, Uncle, Nephew, Grandfather, Husband, King, Monk | **Male** |
| Mother, Sister, Daughter, Aunt, Niece, Grandmother, Wife, Queen, Nun | **Female** |

> ⚠️ **"Cousin", "Spouse", "Parent", "Child", "Sibling"** are **gender-neutral**!

### 3. Solving Approach — Step by Step

**Step 1:** Identify the starting person and ending person
**Step 2:** Break the relationship chain into parts
**Step 3:** Build the family tree from given information
**Step 4:** Trace the relationship

**Example:** "A is the brother of B. B is the sister of C. C is the father of D. How is A related to D?"

```
Family Tree:
    A (M) ---- B (F) ---- C (M)
    (siblings)  (siblings)    |
                              D
```

A and C are siblings. C is D's father. So A is D's **Uncle**.

### 4. Coded Blood Relations

Symbols replace relationships:
- A + B means A is father of B
- A − B means A is mother of B
- A × B means A is brother of B
- A ÷ B means A is sister of B

**Example:** If P + Q × R, find how P is related to R.
- P is father of Q, Q is brother of R
- So P is R's **Father**

### 5. Pointing-Based Questions

"Pointing to a photograph, A said, 'He is the son of my father's only daughter.'"
- Father's only daughter = A herself (if A is female)
- Son of A = A's son
- So the person in photograph is **A's son**

### 6. Common Tricky Phrases

| Phrase | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Father's only son | The person himself (if male) |
| Mother's only daughter | The person herself (if female) |
| Father's only daughter | The person's sister (or herself if female) |
| Only son of my grandfather | My father |
| Son of my father's brother | Cousin |
| My mother's husband | My father |
| My father's wife | My mother |

## 📐 Key Concepts & Methods

### Family Tree Building Rules:
1. **Males** → Use square □ or (M)
2. **Females** → Use circle ○ or (F)
3. **Marriage** → Horizontal line connecting husband and wife
4. **Parent-child** → Vertical line going down
5. **Siblings** → Connected horizontally below the same parents
6. **Always work from the KNOWN person outward**

### Quick Resolution Table:

| Chain | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Father's father | Grandfather |
| Mother's mother | Grandmother |
| Father's brother's son | Cousin |
| Mother's sister's daughter | Cousin |
| Son's son | Grandson |
| Daughter's daughter | Granddaughter |
| Brother's wife | Sister-in-law |
| Sister's husband | Brother-in-law |
| Wife's mother | Mother-in-law |
| Husband's father | Father-in-law |

## 💡 Tips, Tricks & Shortcuts

### Tip 1: Draw the Family Tree — ALWAYS
Never try to solve blood relation problems mentally. Draw it out.

### Tip 2: "Only" is a Key Word
"Father's only son" = the person himself, "Mother's only daughter" = the person herself.

### Tip 3: Gender from Context
If gender isn't stated, look for he/she/his/her clues in the question.

### Tip 4: Break Complex Chains
"My mother's brother's wife's daughter" → Break into: Mother's brother = Uncle → Uncle's wife = Aunt → Aunt's daughter = Cousin

### Tip 5: Photograph Questions
The person in the photo is always someone else. Trace the relationship step by step.

## 📝 RRB NTPC Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

### PYQ 1 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Pointing to a man, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the woman related to the man?**

(A) Mother (B) Sister (C) Grandmother (D) Aunt

**Answer: (A) Mother**
*Solution:* "Only daughter of my mother" = the woman herself. "His mother" = the woman. So the woman is the man's **Mother**.

---

### PYQ 2 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**A is the father of B. C is the daughter of B. D is the brother of B. How is A related to C?**

(A) Father (B) Uncle (C) Grandfather (D) Brother

**Answer: (C) Grandfather**
*Solution:* A → father of B → B has daughter C. So A is C's Grandfather.

---

### PYQ 3 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**If P × Q means P is brother of Q, P + Q means P is father of Q, P − Q means P is sister of Q, then what does A + B × C mean?**

(A) A is uncle of C (B) A is father of C (C) A is grandfather of C (D) A is brother of C

**Answer: (B) A is father of C**
*Solution:* A + B means A is father of B. B × C means B is brother of C. If A is father of B, and B and C are siblings, then A is also father of C.

---

### PYQ 4 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**Introducing a girl, Raj said, "She is the daughter of my mother's only son." How is Raj related to the girl?**

(A) Uncle (B) Father (C) Brother (D) Cousin

**Answer: (B) Father**
*Solution:* "My mother's only son" = Raj himself. "She is the daughter of Raj." So Raj is her Father.

---

### PYQ 5 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**A is B's sister. C is B's mother. D is C's father. E is D's mother. How is A related to D?**

(A) Granddaughter (B) Grandmother (C) Daughter (D) Grandfather

**Answer: (A) Granddaughter**
*Solution:* D is C's father. C is B's mother (and A's mother). So D is A's grandfather. A is D's granddaughter.

---

### PYQ 6 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**If X is the husband of Y, and Y is the sister of Z, how is X related to Z?**

(A) Brother (B) Brother-in-law (C) Uncle (D) Father

**Answer: (B) Brother-in-law**
*Solution:* X married Y. Y is Z's sister. So X is Z's brother-in-law (sister's husband).

---

### PYQ 7 (RRB NTPC 2020)
**Pointing to a photograph, Arun said, "I have no brother or sister but that man's father is my father's son." Who is in the photograph?**

(A) Arun himself (B) Arun's father (C) Arun's son (D) Arun's uncle

**Answer: (C) Arun's son**
*Solution:* "My father's son" = Arun himself (no siblings). "That man's father" = Arun. So the man is Arun's son.

---

### PYQ 8 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Ram is the son of Shyam. Shyam is the brother of Mohan. Sita is the mother of Mohan. How is Ram related to Sita?**

(A) Son (B) Nephew (C) Grandson (D) Cousin

**Answer: (C) Grandson**
*Solution:* Sita is mother of Mohan. Mohan is brother of Shyam. So Sita is also mother of Shyam. Ram is son of Shyam. Ram is Sita's grandson.

### PYQ 9 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**If MOTHER is coded as NPUIFS, how is FATHER coded?**

Wait — this is coding, not blood relations. Let me give a proper one.

**A's father is B's brother. B's daughter is C. How is A related to C?**

(A) Cousin (B) Brother (C) Uncle (D) Cannot be determined

**Answer: (A) Cousin**
*Solution:* A's father and B are brothers. C is B's daughter. So A and C are cousins.

### PYQ 10 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**In a family, there are 6 members: A, B, C, D, E, F. A is the mother of B. B is the husband of C. D is the brother of A. E is the daughter of C. F is the father of A. How is E related to F?**

(A) Granddaughter (B) Daughter (C) Great-granddaughter (D) Niece

**Answer: (C) Great-granddaughter**
*Solution:* F → father of A → A is mother of B → B married C → E is daughter of C (and B). F is A's father, A is B's mother, B is E's father. So E is F's great-granddaughter.

5. **Expected time:** 60–90 seconds
