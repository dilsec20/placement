## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Ranking & Order

**From Left/Top:** Position from the beginning
**From Right/Bottom:** Position from the end

**Total = Left position + Right position − 1**

**Example:** In a row, Ram is 7th from left and 13th from right. Total students = 7 + 13 − 1 = **19**

#### Interchanging Positions
If A and B interchange positions:
- A's new position = B's old position
- B's new position = A's old position

#### Finding Position Between Two People
People between A and B = |Position_A − Position_B| − 1

### 2. Linear Seating Arrangement

People sit in a **straight row**, all facing same direction.

**If facing North:** Left means West direction, Right means East
**If facing South:** Left means East, Right means West

**Approach:**
1. Fix definite positions first (end positions, exact positions)
2. Use "immediate left/right" and "between" clues
3. Use negative clues ("not adjacent") for elimination

### 3. Circular Seating Arrangement

People sit around a **circular table**.

**If facing center:** Clockwise = to the Left, Anticlockwise = to the Right
**If facing outside:** Clockwise = to the Right, Anticlockwise = to the Left

**Approach:**
1. Fix one person first (reference point)
2. Place others relative to the fixed person
3. Use "opposite" (directly across) relationships

### 4. Floor/Building Arrangement

People live on different floors of a building.

**Approach:**
1. Draw floors vertically (bottom = 1 or ground)
2. Use "above/below" clues
3. Count floors between two people if asked

### 5. Comparison/Ordering

**Height, Weight, Age, Marks** — Arrange in order

**Use > and < symbols to build chains:**
A > B, C > A, D < B → Chain: C > A > B > D

## 📐 Key Concepts & Methods

| Problem Type | Key Formula/Method |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Total in row | Left pos + Right pos − 1 |
| Between two | \|Pos₁ − Pos₂\| − 1 |
| Circular: opposite | n/2 positions away |
| Circular: total between (shorter side) | Count the shorter arc |

---

## 💡 Tips & Shortcuts

1. **Linear:** Fix ends first → middle positions fill easier
2. **Circular:** Always fix one person as anchor → place others relative
3. **If two cases arise:** Try both, eliminate one using remaining clues
4. **"Not adjacent"** — Save for last, use to eliminate
5. **Draw. Draw. Draw.** Never solve arrangement problems mentally

## 📝 RRB NTPC Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

### PYQ 1 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**In a row of students, A is 12th from the left and 18th from the right. How many students are there?**
(A) 28 (B) 29 (C) 30 (D) 31
**Answer: (B) 29** → 12 + 18 − 1 = 29

### PYQ 2 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**A is taller than B. C is shorter than D. D is shorter than B. Who is the shortest?**
(A) A (B) B (C) C (D) D
**Answer: (C) C** → A > B > D > C

### PYQ 3 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**In a row, Ravi is 15th from left end. After shifting 5 places to the right, he becomes 8th from right. Total students?**
(A) 27 (B) 26 (C) 25 (D) 28
**Answer: (A) 27** → New left position = 15+5 = 20. Total = 20 + 8 − 1 = 27

### PYQ 4 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**P, Q, R, S, T sit in a row facing North. P sits at one end. Q sits next to P. R is not at any end. T sits next to S. S sits in the middle. Who sits at the end opposite to P?**
(A) Q (B) R (C) S (D) T
**Answer: (D) T** → P Q _ S T or P Q S _ T. S is in middle (pos 3). So: P Q S R T. T is at the other end.

### PYQ 5 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Among 5 friends, A is heavier than B, C is lighter than D, B is heavier than C, E is the heaviest. Arrange in descending order.**
(A) E>A>B>C>D (B) E>A>B>D>C (C) E>D>A>B>C (D) E>A>D>B>C
**Answer: (B) E>A>B>D>C** → E heaviest, A>B, B>C, D>C. We know A>B>C and D>C. But where is D relative to A and B? D>C but we don't know D vs B. Need more info. However, given A>B>C and D>C, and no comparison of D with A or B... checking options: B fits if D is between B and C.

### PYQ 6 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**Six friends sit in a circle facing center. A is to the left of B. C is opposite to A. D is between B and E. F is to the right of E. Who is to the left of C?**
(A) D (B) E (C) F (D) A
**Answer: (C) F** → Clockwise: A, B, D, E, F, C (with A left of B, C opposite A)

### PYQ 7 (RRB NTPC 2020)
**In a class, Renu's rank is 16th from top and 49th from bottom. Total students?**
(A) 64 (B) 63 (C) 65 (D) 66
**Answer: (A) 64** → 16 + 49 − 1 = 64

### PYQ 8 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**If positions of the 3rd and 7th letters of REASONING are interchanged, and also 1st and 5th, then what is the new word?**
Original: R E A S O N I N G (1-9)
Swap 3↔7: R E I S O N A N G
Swap 1↔5: O E I S R N A N G
**Answer: OEISRNANG** → Match with options.

### PYQ 9 (RRB NTPC 2019)
**A is 7th from left, B is 12th from right. If they interchange, A is 22nd from left. Total?**
(A) 32 (B) 33 (C) 34 (D) 28
**Answer: (B) 33** → After swap, A is at B's old position = 22nd from left. B was 12th from right. Total = 22 + 12 − 1 = 33.

### PYQ 10 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**5 people A-E sit on different floors (1=ground to 5=top). C is above A. B is on top floor. D is between A and C. E is on ground floor. On which floor is D?**
(A) 2 (B) 3 (C) 4 (D) 1
**Answer: (B) 3** → E=1, B=5. C above A, D between A and C → A=2, D=3, C=4.
