## 📖 Complete Theory

### What is Decision Making?
Decision Making questions give a set of **conditions/criteria** and a **case/candidate**. You must decide if the candidate **qualifies** based on the given conditions.

### Common Format:
A company/institution has criteria for selection:
1. Age between X and Y years
2. Minimum Z% marks in graduation
3. Work experience of at least N years
4. Must have cleared entrance test
5. Willing to sign a bond

If ALL conditions are met → **Selected**
If some conditions have exceptions → Check the exception rules
If a condition is not met and no exception applies → **Rejected**

### Types of Decisions:

| Scenario | Decision |
|----------|----------|
| All conditions met | Selected |
| One condition not met, exception applies | Referred to higher authority / Conditionally selected |
| One condition not met, no exception | Rejected |
| Data insufficient to check a condition | Data inadequate |

### Example:
**Criteria for Clerk Post:**
1. Age: 21–30 years as on 01/01/2025
2. 10+2 pass with 50% marks
3. Graduation with 45% marks
4. Computer typing speed ≥ 30 WPM

**Exception:** If condition (3) not met but has 3+ years experience → Refer to Manager

**Candidate:** Ravi, DOB: 05/03/1997, 10+2: 65%, Graduation: 40%, Typing: 35 WPM, Experience: 4 years

- Age = 27 ✅ (between 21-30)
- 10+2 = 65% ✅ (≥50%)
- Graduation = 40% ❌ (below 45%) BUT has 4 years experience → **Refer to Manager**
- Typing = 35 WPM ✅ (≥30)

**Decision: Refer to Manager**

---

## 📐 Key Method

### Step-by-Step Approach:
1. **Read ALL conditions carefully** (including exceptions)
2. **Check each condition one by one** for the candidate
3. **Mark ✅ or ❌** for each
4. If all ✅ → Selected
5. If any ❌ → Check if exception applies → If yes, follow exception rule
6. If ❌ and no exception → Rejected
7. If information is missing → Data inadequate

---

## 💡 Tips

1. **Read exceptions FIRST** — They change the outcome
2. **Date/Age calculation** — Be careful with cutoff dates
3. **"At least" means ≥**, "More than" means >**, "Below" means <**
4. **Check ALL conditions** — Don't stop at the first ❌
5. **Expected time:** 60–90 seconds

## 📝 RRB NTPC PYQs

### PYQ 1 (RRB NTPC 2021)
**For a job, criteria are: (1) Age 25-35, (2) B.Tech with 60%+, (3) 2 years experience. Candidate: Age 28, B.Tech 65%, 1 year experience. Exception: If <2 years experience but score >70% → Refer to Director.**
**Decision:**
(A) Selected (B) Rejected (C) Referred (D) Data inadequate

**Answer: (B) Rejected** → Experience <2 years, but marks are 65% (not >70%), so exception doesn't apply → Rejected.
