### PYQ-R1. Letter Series

Complete the series: QMS, NJP, KGM, HDJ, __.

A. FEI  
B. IAG  
C. GEI  
D. EAG

**Answer:** D. EAG

**Explanation:** Each letter moves three places backward at each step: H→E, D→A, and J→G.

**Related fact / rule:** For multi-letter series, track each character position separately; wrap around from A to Z when required.

### PYQ-R2. Dictionary Ordering

Put these words in dictionary order: Practical, Practise, Praise, Prank, Prayer.

A. Practical, Practise, Praise, Prank, Prayer  
B. Praise, Practical, Prank, Prayer, Practise  
C. Practical, Prank, Prayer, Practise, Praise  
D. Praise, Practical, Prayer, Practise, Prank

**Answer:** A. Practical, Practise, Praise, Prank, Prayer

**Explanation:** Compare letters from left to right. “Practical” and “Practise” precede “Praise”; the “Pra…” words then sort by c, n, and y.

**Related fact / rule:** In dictionary ordering, stop at the first letter where two words differ.

**Source:** [RRB NTPC 2021 CBT-1, 18 Jan Shift 2 paper (Prepp PDF)](https://cdn-images.prepp.in/public/image/RRBNTPC2021CBT1PreviousYearPaper18Jan2021Shift2_3b2114a823f008b22f49e2c2dbf09baf.pdf)

## Questions

### Q001. Analogies

Complete the analogy: 4 : 16 :: 6 : ?

A. 12
B. 38
C. 36
D. 34

**Answer:** C. 36

**Explanation:** The first number is squared: 4²=16; therefore 6²=36.

**Related fact / rule:** Check whether the relation is multiplication, square/cube, part-whole, or function-purpose.

### Q002. Number and Alphabetical Series

Find the next number: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, __.

A. 18
B. 21
C. 15
D. 24

**Answer:** A. 18

**Explanation:** Each term increases by 3; the next term is 15+3=18.

**Related fact / rule:** For a series, first test constant differences, then second differences and alternating patterns.

### Q003. Coding and Decoding

In a code, every letter is shifted 2 place(s) forward. How is RAILB written?

A. RAILB
B. DNKCT
C. TCKNZ
D. TCKND

**Answer:** D. TCKND

**Explanation:** Shift each letter forward 2: RAILB → TCKND.

**Related fact / rule:** To decode a forward Caesar shift, move each letter back by the same amount.

### Q004. Mathematical Operations

**Related fact / rule:** In symbol-substitution problems, replace every operator before applying precedence.

### Q007. Analytical Reasoning

A student is 6th from the top and 11th from the bottom. How many students are in the group?

A. 15
B. 17
C. 18
D. 16

**Answer:** D. 16

**Explanation:** Total = top rank + bottom rank − 1 = 6+11−1 = 16.

**Related fact / rule:** Subtract 1 because the same student is counted from both ends.

### Q008. Syllogism

C. Some vehicles are trees
D. All trees are buses

**Answer:** B. No bus is a tree

**Explanation:** Every bus belongs to the vehicle set, and the vehicle set does not overlap trees.

**Related fact / rule:** A valid conclusion must follow in every diagram that satisfies the statements.

### Q009. Jumbling and Ordering

B. APPLE

### Q010. Venn Diagrams

A. 35
B. 34
C. 36
D. 42

**Answer:** A. 35

**Explanation:** Inclusion-exclusion: tea + coffee − both = 25+17−7=35.

**Related fact / rule:** For two overlapping sets, |A∪B|=|A|+|B|−|A∩B|.

### Q011. Puzzle and Data Sufficiency

A is older than B. B is older than C. Who is youngest?

A. A
B. B
C. C
D. Cannot be determined

**Answer:** C. C

**Explanation:** The order is A > B > C, so C is youngest.

**Related fact / rule:** Translate comparisons into a consistent chain before answering a ranking puzzle.

### Q013. Decision Making
