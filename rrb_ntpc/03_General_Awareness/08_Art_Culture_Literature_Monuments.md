## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Classical Dances of India

| Dance | State | Features |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Bharatanatyam** | Tamil Nadu | Oldest classical dance, temple dance |
| **Kathak** | Uttar Pradesh | Mughal influence, footwork, spins |
| **Kathakali** | Kerala | Male dancers, elaborate makeup, stories from Ramayana/Mahabharata |
| **Odissi** | Odisha | Tribhangi pose, temple sculptures |
| **Kuchipudi** | Andhra Pradesh | Dance-drama tradition |
| **Manipuri** | Manipur | Raas Leela, gentle movements |
| **Mohiniyattam** | Kerala | Female solo, graceful |
| **Sattriya** | Assam | Vaishnavite tradition, added to classical list in 2000 |
