### 1. Indian Railways (🔥 Very Important for RRB!)

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Established | 16 April 1853 (first train: Mumbai to Thane) |
| Headquarters | New Delhi |
| Zones | 18 Railway Zones |
| Track length | ~68,000+ km (4th largest in world) |
| Largest employer | One of the largest in the world |
| Railway Budget | Merged with Union Budget since 2017 |

### 2. National Highways

| Highway | Route |
|---------|-------|
| NH-44 (longest) | Srinagar to Kanyakumari (3,745 km) |
| NH-27 (2nd longest) | Porbandar to Silchar |
| NH-48 (old NH-8) | Delhi to Mumbai |

**NHAI:** National Highways Authority of India (builds/manages NHs)
