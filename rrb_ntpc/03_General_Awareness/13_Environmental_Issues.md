## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Climate Change & Global Warming

| Concept | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Greenhouse Effect | Trapping of heat by greenhouse gases (CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, CFCs) |
| Global Warming | Rise in Earth's average temperature |
| Ozone Layer | O₃ layer in stratosphere, protects from UV rays |
| Ozone Depletion | Caused by CFCs (chlorofluorocarbons) |
| Montreal Protocol (1987) | Ban on CFCs to protect ozone |
| Kyoto Protocol (1997) | Greenhouse gas emission reduction |
| Paris Agreement (2015) | Limit global temp rise to 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels |

### 2. Types of Pollution

| Type | Causes | Effects |
|------|--------|---------|
| Air | Vehicles, industries, burning fossil fuels | Respiratory diseases, acid rain |
| Water | Industrial waste, sewage, pesticides | Waterborne diseases, eutrophication |
| Soil | Pesticides, industrial waste, deforestation | Loss of fertility, contamination |
| Noise | Traffic, industries, loudspeakers | Hearing loss, stress |

**Major pollutants:** CO₂, SO₂, NO₂, CO, particulate matter (PM 2.5, PM 10)
**Acid Rain:** Caused by SO₂ and NO₂ → forms H₂SO₄ and HNO₃

### 3. Biodiversity

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Biodiversity | Variety of living organisms in an area |
| Hotspot | Area with high biodiversity under threat (India has 4: Western Ghats, Eastern Himalayas, Indo-Burma, Sundaland) |
| Endemic species | Found only in a specific region |
| Endangered species | At risk of extinction |
| Red Data Book | Record of endangered species (by IUCN) |
