## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation)

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Founded | 1969 |
| Headquarters | Bengaluru |
| Founder | Vikram Sarabhai (Father of Indian Space Program) |
| Current Chairman | S. Somanath (as of 2024) |
| First satellite | Aryabhata (1975) |
| Launch site | Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota (Andhra Pradesh) |

### 2. Major ISRO Missions

| Mission | Year | Achievement |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Aryabhata | 1975 | First Indian satellite |
| SLV-3 | 1980 | First indigenous launch vehicle (by APJ Abdul Kalam) |
| INSAT series | 1983+ | Communication satellites |
| IRS series | 1988+ | Remote sensing satellites |
| Chandrayaan-1 | 2008 | India's first lunar mission (discovered water on Moon) |
| Mangalyaan (MOM) | 2013-14 | Mars Orbiter Mission — India first Asian nation to reach Mars; cheapest Mars mission |
| Chandrayaan-2 | 2019 | Orbiter success, lander (Vikram) crash |
| Chandrayaan-3 | 2023 | **Successful soft landing on Moon's south pole** — India 4th country to land on Moon |
| Aditya-L1 | 2023 | India's first solar observation mission (Lagrange point L1) |
| Gaganyaan | Upcoming | India's first crewed space mission |
| NAVIC (IRNSS) | Operating | India's own GPS-like navigation system |

### 3. Important Satellites

| Satellite | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| INSAT | Communication, weather |
| IRS | Remote sensing |
| GSAT | Geostationary communication |
| Cartosat | Mapping |
| RISAT | Radar imaging |
| IRNSS/NAVIC | Navigation |
| AstroSat | Space telescope |

### 4. Indian Nuclear Program

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Father of Nuclear Program | Homi J. Bhabha |
| Nuclear research center | BARC (Bhabha Atomic Research Centre), Mumbai |
| First nuclear test | Smiling Buddha, Pokhran (1974) — Peaceful Nuclear Explosion |
| Second nuclear tests | Pokhran-II (1998) — Operation Shakti (under PM Vajpayee) |
| Nuclear doctrine | No First Use (NFU) policy |
| Nuclear Regulatory Board | AERB (Atomic Energy Regulatory Board) |

### 6. World Space Agencies

| Agency | Country | Notable |
|--------|---------|---------|
| NASA | USA | Apollo Moon missions, Mars rovers |

| ESA | Europe | Collaborative space agency |

### 7. Space Firsts

| First | Name | Year |
|-------|------|------|
| First human in space | Yuri Gagarin (USSR) | 1961 |
| First woman in space | Valentina Tereshkova (USSR) | 1963 |
