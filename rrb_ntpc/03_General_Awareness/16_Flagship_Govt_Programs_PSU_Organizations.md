## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Major Government Schemes

| Scheme | Year | Objective |
|--------|------|-----------|
| **MGNREGA** (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act) | 2005 | 100 days guaranteed employment to rural households |
| **PM Jan Dhan Yojana** | 2014 | Financial inclusion — bank accounts for all |
| **Swachh Bharat Mission** | 2014 | Clean India — eliminate open defecation, waste management |
| **Make in India** | 2014 | Boost manufacturing in India |
| **Digital India** | 2015 | Digital empowerment, e-governance |
| **Skill India** | 2015 | Skill development (PMKVY) |
| **Start-up India** | 2016 | Promote entrepreneurship |
| **PM Awas Yojana** | 2015 | Housing for all by 2022 |
| **PM Ujjwala Yojana** | 2016 | Free LPG connections to BPL women |
| **Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)** | 2018 | Health insurance ₹5 lakh/family/year |
| **PM Kisan Samman Nidhi** | 2019 | ₹6,000/year to farmers (3 installments) |
| **Beti Bachao Beti Padhao** | 2015 | Girl child protection & education |
| **PM Mudra Yojana** | 2015 | Loans for micro/small enterprises (Shishu, Kishore, Tarun) |
| **Atal Pension Yojana** | 2015 | Pension scheme for unorganized sector |
| **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana** | 2015 | Savings scheme for girl child |
| **PM Fasal Bima Yojana** | 2016 | Crop insurance for farmers |
| **Saubhagya Scheme** | 2017 | Electricity to all households |
| **Jal Jeevan Mission** | 2019 | Tap water to every rural household |
| **PM SVANidhi** | 2020 | Micro loans for street vendors |
| **PM Gati Shakti** | 2021 | Infrastructure connectivity master plan |
| **Agnipath Scheme** | 2022 | Short-term military recruitment (Agniveers) |
