## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Indian Constitution — Basics

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Drafted by | Constituent Assembly (1946-1949) |
| Chairman of Drafting Committee | Dr. B.R. Ambedkar |
| President of Constituent Assembly | Dr. Rajendra Prasad |
| Adopted | 26 November 1949 |
| Came into effect | 26 January 1950 |
| Longest written constitution | ~470 Articles, 25 Parts, 12 Schedules |
| Borrowed from | UK, USA, Ireland, Canada, Australia, Germany, USSR, France, South Africa, Japan |

### 2. Sources of the Constitution

| Feature | Borrowed From |
|---------|--------------|
| Parliamentary system, Rule of Law, Writs | **UK** |
| Fundamental Rights, Judicial Review, Supreme Court | **USA** |
| Directive Principles, Election of President | **Ireland** |
| Federation with strong center, Residuary powers | **Canada** |
| Concurrent List, Joint Sitting of Parliament | **Australia** |
| Emergency provisions, Suspension of FR | **Germany** |
| Fundamental Duties | **USSR** |
| Procedure of Amendment | **South Africa** |
| Republic, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity | **France** |

### 3. Preamble

"WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA, having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC..."

- **Socialist, Secular** added by **42nd Amendment (1976)**
- Keywords: Justice, Liberty, Equality, Fraternity

### 4. Fundamental Rights (Part III, Articles 12-35)

| Right | Articles | Key Points |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Right to Equality | 14-18 | Equality before law, no discrimination, abolition of untouchability & titles |
| Right to Freedom | 19-22 | 6 freedoms (speech, assembly, movement, residence, profession, association), protection of life & liberty |
| Right against Exploitation | 23-24 | No forced labor, no child labor (<14 in hazardous jobs) |
| Right to Freedom of Religion | 25-28 | Freedom of conscience, religious practice |
| Cultural & Educational Rights | 29-30 | Minorities' rights to conserve culture, establish institutions |
| Right to Constitutional Remedies | 32 | Right to move Supreme Court (Ambedkar: "Heart and Soul of Constitution") |

**Note:** Right to Property was removed as FR by **44th Amendment (1978)** — now a Legal Right (Art. 300A).

### 5. Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV, Articles 36-51)

Not justiciable (not enforceable by court) but fundamental in governance.

Key DPSPs:
- Equal pay for equal work (Art. 39d)
- Free legal aid (Art. 39A)
- Uniform Civil Code (Art. 44)
- Free and compulsory education for children (Art. 45)
- Protection of environment (Art. 48A)
- Prohibition of intoxicating drinks (Art. 47)

### 6. Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, Article 51A)

Added by **42nd Amendment (1976)**, originally 10, now **11** (11th added by 86th Amendment, 2002).

Key duties: Respect Constitution & national symbols, cherish ideals of freedom struggle, defend the country, promote harmony, protect environment, develop scientific temper, safeguard public property, provide education to children (6-14 years).

### 7. Parliament

| House | Also Called | Members | Term | Head |
|-------|-----------|---------|------|------|
| Lok Sabha | House of the People | 543 elected + 2 nominated | 5 years | Speaker |
| Rajya Sabha | Council of States | 245 (233 elected + 12 nominated) | Permanent (1/3 retire every 2 years) | Vice President (Chairman) |

**Joint Session:** Called by President when deadlock between houses (Art. 108). Presided by Speaker.

### 8. President of India

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Election | Electoral College (MPs + MLAs) |
| Term | 5 years |
| Minimum age | 35 years |
| Qualification | Citizen of India, eligible for Lok Sabha membership |
| Impeachment | By Parliament (Art. 61) |
| First President | Dr. Rajendra Prasad |

**Powers:** Executive, Legislative, Judicial (pardoning), Emergency, Diplomatic, Military (Supreme Commander)

### 9. Prime Minister & Council of Ministers

- PM appointed by President (leader of majority party in Lok Sabha)
- Council of Ministers: Cabinet Ministers, Ministers of State, Deputy Ministers
- Collective responsibility to Lok Sabha (Art. 75)

### 10. Judiciary

| Court | Head | Appointment | Retirement |
|-------|------|-------------|-----------|
| Supreme Court | Chief Justice of India (CJI) | By President | 65 years |
| High Court | Chief Justice | By President | 62 years |
| District Court | District Judge | By Governor | — |

**Writs issued by SC/HC:**
| Writ | Meaning | Use |
|------|---------|-----|
| Habeas Corpus | "Produce the body" | Against illegal detention |
| Mandamus | "We command" | Orders public official to do duty |
| Prohibition | "Forbid" | Stops lower court proceedings |
| Certiorari | "To be certified" | Transfer case to higher court |
| Quo Warranto | "By what authority?" | Challenges unauthorized office |

### 11. Emergency Provisions

| Type | Article | Declared By | Ground |
|------|---------|-------------|--------|
| National Emergency | 352 | President | War, external aggression, armed rebellion |
| President's Rule (State) | 356 | President | Failure of constitutional machinery in state |
| Financial Emergency | 360 | President | Financial stability threatened (never used) |

### 12. Important Amendments

| Amendment | Year | Provision |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| 1st | 1951 | Restrictions on Fundamental Rights |
| 42nd | 1976 | "Mini Constitution" — Added Socialist, Secular, Duties |
| 44th | 1978 | Right to Property removed as FR |
| 52nd | 1985 | Anti-Defection Law |
| 61st | 1989 | Voting age: 21 → 18 |
| 73rd | 1992 | Panchayati Raj institutions |
| 74th | 1992 | Municipalities/Urban local bodies |
| 86th | 2002 | Right to Education (Art. 21A), 11th Fundamental Duty |
| 101st | 2016 | GST |
