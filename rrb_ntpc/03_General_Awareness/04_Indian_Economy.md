## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. Basic Economic Concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **GDP** | Gross Domestic Product — Total value of goods & services produced within the country |
| **GNP** | GDP + Income from abroad − Income sent abroad |
| **NNP** | GNP − Depreciation |
| **Per Capita Income** | National Income / Population |
| **Inflation** | General rise in price levels |
| **Deflation** | General fall in price levels |
| **Fiscal Deficit** | Total Expenditure − Total Receipts (excluding borrowings) |
| **Revenue Deficit** | Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipts |

### 2. Five Year Plans

| Plan | Period | Focus |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1st | 1951-56 | Agriculture (Harrod-Domar Model) |
| 2nd | 1956-61 | Industrialization (Mahalanobis Model) |
| 3rd | 1961-66 | Self-reliance |
| 4th | 1969-74 | Growth with stability |
| 5th | 1974-79 | Poverty removal (Garibi Hatao) |
| 8th | 1992-97 | Liberalization, human development |
| 12th | 2012-17 | Last Five Year Plan; replaced by NITI Aayog |

**NITI Aayog** replaced Planning Commission in **2015** (Chairman: PM, Vice Chairman appointed).

### 3. Indian Banking System

| Institution | Role | Established |
|------------|------|-------------|
| **RBI** | Central bank, monetary policy, currency | 1935 (Nationalized 1949) |
| **SBI** | Largest commercial bank | 1955 (from Imperial Bank) |
| **NABARD** | Rural/agricultural credit | 1982 |
| **SIDBI** | Small industries development | 1990 |
| **SEBI** | Securities & stock market regulation | 1988 (statutory 1992) |

**RBI Governor:** Controls repo rate, reverse repo rate, CRR, SLR

### 4. Key Economic Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Repo Rate** | Rate at which RBI lends to banks |
| **Reverse Repo Rate** | Rate at which RBI borrows from banks |
| **CRR** | Cash Reserve Ratio — % of deposits banks keep with RBI |
| **SLR** | Statutory Liquidity Ratio — % of deposits in liquid assets |
| **Bank Rate** | Rate for long-term lending by RBI |

### 5. Taxes

| Tax Type | Examples | Collected By |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **Direct Tax** | Income Tax, Corporate Tax, Wealth Tax | Central |
| **Indirect Tax** | GST (replaced VAT, Excise, Service Tax) | Central + State |
| **GST** | Goods & Services Tax (101st Amendment, 2017) | GST Council |

**GST Slabs:** 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%

### 6. Green Revolution & White Revolution

| Revolution | Related To | Father/Key Person |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| Green Revolution | Agriculture (wheat, rice) | M.S. Swaminathan |
| White Revolution (Operation Flood) | Milk/Dairy | Verghese Kurien |
| Blue Revolution | Fisheries | — |
| Yellow Revolution | Oilseeds | Sam Pitroda |
| Golden Revolution | Fruits/Horticulture | Nirpakh Tutej |

### 7. Economic Organizations

| Organization | Headquarters |
|-------------|-------------|
| World Bank | Washington DC |
| IMF | Washington DC |
| WTO | Geneva |
| ADB (Asian Development Bank) | Manila |
| AIIB | Beijing |
| New Development Bank (BRICS) | Shanghai |

### 8. Indian Budget

- Presented by Finance Minister on **1st February**
- Railway Budget merged with Union Budget since **2017**
- Parts: Revenue Budget + Capital Budget

### 9. Census & Population

| Census | Year | Key Facts |
|--------|------|-----------|
| First Census | 1872 | Under British |
| First systematic | 1881 | Regular decennial |
| Latest | 2011 | Population: 121.06 crore |
| Literacy (2011) | 74.04% | Male: 82.14%, Female: 65.46% |

### 10. Poverty & Unemployment

- **Poverty Line:** Tendulkar Committee methodology — ₹816/month (rural), ₹1000/month (urban) [2011-12]
- **MGNREGA:** Guarantees 100 days of employment per rural household per year
- **Types of unemployment:** Structural, cyclical, seasonal, disguised, frictional
