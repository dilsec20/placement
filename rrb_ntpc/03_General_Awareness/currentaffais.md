# Current Affairs for RRB NTPC / SSC

## Coverage

**Period covered:** 1 January–15 November 2026.  
**Exam focus:** India, government programmes, economy, science and technology, international relations, awards, sports, and major reports.  
**Update note:** Current affairs keep changing. This revision file uses information published by 15 November 2026; check the linked official releases for later corrections or updates.

This is a selective, exam-focused digest rather than a day-by-day transcript of every announcement. Facts are dated so that annual rankings, estimates, and scheme figures are not mistaken for timeless facts. The revision notes come first. The MCQ set, with answers and explanations, is at the end.

## January 2026

### 77th Republic Day

- India celebrated its **77th Republic Day on 26 January 2026** at Kartavya Path, New Delhi. President **Droupadi Murmu** led the celebrations.
- The chief guests were **António Costa**, President of the European Council, and **Ursula von der Leyen**, President of the European Commission. Remember their offices separately: Costa leads the European Council; von der Leyen leads the European Commission.
- The parade highlighted **150 years of Vande Mataram**. [PIB: Republic Day 2026 programme and chief guests](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2218449&lang=1&reg=6)

### Padma Awards 2026

- The President approved **131 Padma Awards**, including two duo cases (each duo counts as one award): **5 Padma Vibhushan, 13 Padma Bhushan, and 113 Padma Shri**.
- Padma Vibhushan recognises exceptional and distinguished service; Padma Bhushan recognises distinguished service of a high order; Padma Shri recognises distinguished service in any field.
- The awards are announced around Republic Day and conferred by the President at investiture ceremonies. [PIB: Padma Awards 2026 list and categories](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2218547&lang=1&reg=3)

## February 2026

### Union Budget 2026–27

- Finance Minister **Nirmala Sitharaman** presented the Union Budget on **1 February 2026**. It was the first Budget prepared in **Kartavya Bhawan** and was framed around three *kartavyas*: accelerating sustainable economic growth; fulfilling aspirations and building capacity; and ensuring that every family, community, region, and sector can participate in development.
- Budget Estimates put total expenditure at about **₹53.5 lakh crore** and non-debt receipts at about **₹36.5 lakh crore**. Public capital expenditure was raised to **₹12.2 lakh crore** for FY 2026–27.
- The fiscal deficit target for FY 2026–27 was **4.3% of GDP**. The Budget accepted the 16th Finance Commission recommendation to retain the states’ vertical share in the divisible tax pool at **41%**.
- The Budget estimated real GDP growth at **7.4% for FY 2025–26**. This was an estimate for that year, not the FY 2026–27 growth outcome.
- The **Income-tax Act, 2025** was scheduled to come into effect on **1 April 2026**. [PIB: Budget 2026–27 highlights](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2221455&lang=1&reg=3) · [PIB: fiscal indicators and growth estimates](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2221389&lang=1&reg=3)

### India AI Impact Summit 2026

- India hosted the **India AI Impact Summit** in New Delhi, with the main programme from **16–21 February** and leaders’ sessions on 18–19 February, at **Bharat Mandapam**. It was the first global AI summit in the series hosted in the Global South.
- Its guiding theme was **“Sarvajan Hitaya, Sarvajan Sukhaya”** (“Welfare for All, Happiness for All”); its three *Sutras* were **People, Planet, and Progress**.
- Prime Minister Narendra Modi presented the **M.A.N.A.V.** vision: Moral and Ethical Systems; Accountable Governance; National Sovereignty; Accessible and Inclusive; Valid and Legitimate.
- The **New Delhi Declaration on AI Impact** was endorsed by **92 countries and international organisations**. The summit also announced more than **US$250 billion** in expected investment commitments across the AI value chain. [PIB: summit outcome and declaration](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2238195&lang=1&reg=1) · [PIB: M.A.N.A.V. vision](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/feb/doc2026219796801.pdf)

### Other February items

- The Union Cabinet approved **Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0**, with a corpus of **₹10,000 crore**. It is intended to mobilise venture capital, particularly for deep-tech, innovative manufacturing, and early-growth startups. Funding is channelled through eligible Alternative Investment Funds, which then invest in startups. [PIB: Startup India FoF 2.0](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2227994&lang=2&reg=48)

## March 2026

### India wins ICC Men’s T20 World Cup

- India defeated **New Zealand by 96 runs** in the final at Ahmedabad on **8 March 2026**.
- India became the **first team to defend** the Men’s T20 World Cup title and the **first team to win three** Men’s T20 World Cup titles. **Sanju Samson** was named Player of the Tournament. [ICC: final and record](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/live-india-new-zealand-chase-world-cup-glory?appview=true) · [ICC: Player of the Tournament](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/sanju-samson-adjudged-t20-world-cup-player-of-the-tournament?appview=true)

### PM-KISAN 22nd instalment

- On **13 March**, the Prime Minister released the 22nd instalment from **Guwahati, Assam**: over **₹18,640 crore** to more than **9.32 crore farmer families** through Direct Benefit Transfer.
- PM-KISAN is a central sector income-support scheme for eligible landholding farmer families. The instalment number, amount, beneficiary count, and release location are date-specific. [PIB: 22nd PM-KISAN instalment](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2238588&lang=1&reg=6)

### BHAVYA industrial parks

- The Cabinet approved **Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana (BHAVYA)** with **₹33,660 crore** to develop **100 plug-and-play industrial parks** across India in partnership with States and private-sector players.
- The aim is to provide ready industrial infrastructure and support manufacturing-led investment and employment. [PIB: BHAVYA approval](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2241814&lang=2&reg=48)

### India’s 2031–2035 climate commitments

- The Cabinet approved India’s updated **Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC)** for 2031–2035, to be communicated under the **UN Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC)** and the **Paris Agreement**.
- Key 2035 targets, measured from the stated baselines: reduce GDP emissions intensity by **47% from 2005**; achieve **60% non-fossil share** of cumulative installed electric power capacity; and create a forest-and-tree-cover carbon sink of **3.5–4.0 billion tonnes of CO₂ equivalent** from the 2005 level.
- An NDC is a country’s climate action commitment under the Paris Agreement. [PIB: India’s NDC for 2031–2035](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2245209&lang=1&reg=1)

## April 2026

### India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement signed

- India and New Zealand signed an FTA on **27 April 2026**. The agreement provides for duty-free access for Indian exports to New Zealand once it enters into force; it did **not** become effective on the signing date.
- The agreement entered into force later, on **20 October 2026** (see October notes). [Ministry of Commerce: FTA factsheet](https://www.commerce.gov.in/files/2026-04/final_1.pdf) · [PIB: effective date announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2325235&lang=1&reg=3)

### Startup India FoF 2.0 notified

- The government notified the **₹10,000-crore Startup India Fund of Funds 2.0** in April, following Cabinet approval in February. Its focus includes deep-tech, early-growth, and technology-driven manufacturing startups. [PIB: notification](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2251638&lang=1&reg=3)

## May 2026

### Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme 5.0

- The Cabinet approved **ECLGS 5.0** to provide additional credit support in response to liquidity pressures linked to the West Asia situation. It targets additional credit flow of **₹2.55 lakh crore**, including ₹5,000 crore for airlines.
- Guarantee coverage is **100% for eligible MSMEs** and **90% for eligible non-MSMEs and airlines**. This is a credit guarantee to lenders, not a grant paid directly to every business. [PIB: ECLGS 5.0](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2258114&lang=1&reg=1)

### Mission for Cotton Productivity

- Approved for **FY 2026–27 to FY 2030–31** with a total outlay of **₹5,659.22 crore**, the Mission for Cotton Productivity (also called **KAPAS KANTI**) aims to improve cotton productivity and quality, develop improved seeds and production practices, and strengthen traceability and competitiveness.
- The mission is associated with the government’s **5F** vision: Farm to Fibre to Factory to Fashion to Foreign. [PIB: Mission for Cotton Productivity](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2258111&lang=1&reg=3)

### State and Union Territory Assembly elections

- In 2026, the Election Commission of India conducted Assembly elections in **Assam, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, and the Union Territory of Puducherry**.
- Polling dates included **9 April** in Assam, Kerala, and Puducherry; **23 April** in Tamil Nadu; and **23 and 29 April** in West Bengal. Counting was scheduled for **4 May 2026**. [Election Commission of India: 2026 Assembly election schedule](https://www.eci.gov.in/election-details/2026/S25/3)

## June 2026

### India–Oman CEPA takes effect

- The **India–Oman Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA)** took effect on **1 June 2026**, according to the Ministry of Commerce’s FTA implementation table.
- CEPA means **Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement**. It is a bilateral trade and economic agreement; do not confuse it with CETA (Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement), the India–UK pact. [PIB: FTA implementation status](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2287009&lang=1&reg=3)

## July 2026

### India–UK CETA enters into force

- The **India–United Kingdom Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA)** and its accompanying **Agreement on Social Security / Double Contribution Convention** entered into force on **15 July 2026**.
- PIB said CETA provides zero-duty access for nearly **99% of India’s exports** to the UK. The Double Contribution Convention helps eligible temporary workers avoid paying social-security contributions in both countries for the covered period. [PIB: India–UK CETA in force](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2285085&lang=1&reg=48)

### Semicon 2.0 and mobile manufacturing

- The Cabinet approved **Semicon 2.0** with a total outlay of **₹1,27,500 crore** to support India’s semiconductor design and manufacturing ecosystem, including design, fabs, advanced packaging, materials, equipment, research, and skills.
- The **Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (MPMS)** was approved with **₹62,500 crore** for five years, FY 2026–27 to FY 2030–31. It aims to expand domestic production and value addition and strengthen supply chains. [PIB: Semicon 2.0](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2284784&lang=1&reg=22) · [PIB: MPMS](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2284789&lang=1&reg=20)

### PM-KISAN continuation

- In July the Cabinet approved continuation of PM-KISAN from FY 2026–27 through FY 2030–31, with an outlay of **₹3.15 lakh crore**. This is a separate fact from the March 22nd-instalment release. [PIB Cabinet decision index](https://www.pib.gov.in/EventDetail.aspx?ID=1209&lang=1&reg=1)

## August 2026

### Commonwealth Games, Glasgow

- The 2026 Commonwealth Games took place in **Glasgow, Scotland, 23 July–2 August**. India finished **fourth** with **39 medals: 13 gold, 17 silver, and 9 bronze**.
- India’s standout facts included **seven boxing gold medals** and Mirabai Chanu’s third consecutive Commonwealth Games title. [PIB: India’s Games performance](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2294008&lang=1&reg=22) · [PIB: Games overview](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2289962&lang=2&reg=48)

### GOBARdhan National Circular Bioenergy Scheme

- On **6 August**, the Cabinet approved **GOBARdhan**, the National Circular Bioenergy Scheme, with an outlay of **₹23,731 crore** for FY 2026–27 to FY 2035–36.
- It is designed to convert cattle dung, agricultural residues, press mud, municipal organic waste, and other biomass into compressed biogas, manure, and value for rural communities. [PIB: GOBARdhan approval](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295480&lang=1&reg=3)

## September 2026

### 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi

- India hosted the **18th BRICS Summit** in **New Delhi, 12–13 September 2026**. The summit theme was **“Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability.”**
- The summit adopted the **New Delhi BRICS Leaders’ Declaration**. BRICS is a cooperation grouping; it is not a treaty-based military alliance. [PIB: New Delhi Declaration](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2309505&lang=1&reg=3)

### 20th Asian Games: Aichi–Nagoya 2026

- The Asian Games were held in **Aichi–Nagoya, Japan, 19 September–4 October**. India finished **fourth** with **85 medals: 21 gold, 27 silver, and 37 bronze**.
- India won two gold medals each in cricket, hockey, and kabaddi. Archery contributed five golds. [PIB: India’s Asian Games medal table](https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?Id=151080&lang=1&reg=3)

## October 2026

### India–New Zealand FTA comes into force

- The India–New Zealand FTA entered into force on **20 October 2026**. The Ministry of Commerce said **100% of Indian exports** receive duty-free access in New Zealand from the start of the agreement, subject to the agreement’s product and origin rules.
- Exam distinction: **signed 27 April; in force 20 October 2026**. [PIB: entry into force](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2325235&lang=1&reg=3) · [New Zealand legislation commencement order](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2026/289/en/latest/)

### India Chem 2026

- The **14th India Chem** was held **22–24 October in Mumbai** under the theme **“Advantage Bharat: An Emerging Global Chemicals Hub.”** Japan was the partner country.
- It was a biennial chemicals and petrochemicals industry exhibition and conference organised by the Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals with FICCI. [PIB: India Chem 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2325901&lang=1&reg=1)

## November 2026 (through 15 November)

### India S&T Scoreboard

- **NITI Aayog** launched the **India Science and Technology (S&T) Scoreboard** in New Delhi on **3 November**. It brings together state and Union Territory information on higher-education and R&D institutions, deep-tech startups, incubators, publications, patents, research quality, impact, collaboration, and open access.
- Purpose: allow comparison and evidence-based planning of State and UT S&T ecosystems. [PIB: India S&T Scoreboard](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2332136&lang=1&reg=48)

### VISTAR statistical modernisation scheme

- On **4 November**, the Cabinet approved **Viksit Bharat Initiative for Statistical Transformation, Analytics & Research (VISTAR)** for **FY 2026–27 to FY 2030–31**, with an outlay of **₹4,371.10 crore**.
- VISTAR is a Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) scheme. Its focus includes timelier official statistics, harmonised and interoperable data, survey modernisation, research, and data governance.
- Do not confuse VISTAR with **Bharat-VISTAAR**, an agricultural advisory tool proposed in the Union Budget. [PIB: VISTAR approval](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2332480&lang=1&reg=3)

### Critical Mineral Processing CAPEX Support Scheme

- The Cabinet approved this scheme on **4 November** with an outlay of **₹2,814 crore** for FY 2026–27 to FY 2030–31.
- It offers a **30% capital subsidy** for four processing plants: two lithium plants and two nickel plants. Cobalt may also be recovered as a by-product of nickel processing. The goal is to build domestic processing capacity and reduce import dependence for critical mineral materials.
- CAPEX means **capital expenditure**. [PIB: scheme details](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2332476&lang=1&reg=3)

### Export performance, April–October 2026–27

- PIB reported estimated total exports of **US$568.84 billion** for April–October 2026–27, **16.66% higher** than the corresponding period a year earlier.
- The total includes merchandise and services exports. Merchandise exports were estimated at US$309.06 billion and services exports at US$259.78 billion. October services data were estimates based on the latest available data, so treat the figures as provisional estimates, not final audited totals. [PIB: April–October export figures](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2335914&lang=2&reg=48)
