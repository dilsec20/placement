## 📖 Complete Theory

### 1. United Nations (UN)

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Founded | 24 October 1945 |
| Headquarters | New York, USA |
| Secretary General | António Guterres (since 2017) |
| Member States | 193 |
| Official Languages | English, French, Spanish, Russian, Chinese, Arabic |
| Charter signed at | San Francisco Conference (June 1945) |

### 2. Principal Organs of UN

| Organ | Function |
|-------|----------|
| **General Assembly** | All 193 members, one vote each, meets annually |
| **Security Council** | 15 members (5 permanent + 10 non-permanent), peace & security |
| **ICJ (International Court of Justice)** | Judicial organ, The Hague (Netherlands) |
| **Secretariat** | Administrative, headed by Secretary General |
| **ECOSOC** | Economic & Social Council |
| **Trusteeship Council** | Suspended (1994) |

### 3. UN Security Council — Permanent Members (P5)

| Country | Veto Power |
|---------|-----------|
| USA | ✅ |
| UK | ✅ |
| France | ✅ |
| Russia | ✅ |
| China | ✅ |

**Mnemonic:** "**FRUSC**" — France, Russia, USA, (China → S for "Zhongguo" doesn't work)... Better: **"FRUCC"** = France, Russia, USA, China, (UK→ Count as C for "Country")... Just memorize: **USA, UK, France, Russia, China**.
