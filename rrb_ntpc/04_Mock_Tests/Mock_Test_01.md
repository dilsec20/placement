## Detailed Solutions

### Section 1: General Awareness

**Q1. (A) Lord Mountbatten**  
Lord Mountbatten was the first Governor-General of independent India (1947–1948). C. Rajagopalachari was the last Governor-General (1948–1950). Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the first President.

**Q2. (C) Karnataka**  
The Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N) passes through 8 Indian states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, and Mizoram. Karnataka lies south of the Tropic of Cancer.

**Q3. (B) Article 17**  
Article 17 abolishes untouchability and forbids its practice in any form. Article 14 deals with equality before law.

**Q4. (C) Liver**  
The liver is the largest gland in the human body (weighing about 1.5 kg). The pancreas is the second largest gland.

**Q5. (C) Bengaluru**  
Bengaluru (Karnataka) is called the Silicon Valley of India due to its concentration of IT companies.

**Q6. (B) Uttarakhand**  
The Chipko Movement started in 1973 in Chamoli district of Uttarakhand (then part of Uttar Pradesh), led by Sunderlal Bahuguna and Gaura Devi.

**Q7. (A) Gross Domestic Product**  
GDP = Gross Domestic Product — the total value of goods and services produced within a country's borders in a specific period.

**Q8. (B) Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay**  
'Vande Mataram' was written by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay in his novel 'Anandamath' (1882). Tagore composed 'Jana Gana Mana' (National Anthem).

**Q9. (C) Mars**  
Mars is called the Red Planet because of iron oxide (rust) on its surface, giving it a reddish appearance.

**Q10. (C) Football**  
The Durand Cup is the oldest existing football tournament in Asia, started in 1888.

**Q11. (C) NaHCO₃**  
Baking soda = Sodium Bicarbonate = NaHCO₃. Na₂CO₃ is washing soda. NaCl is common salt.

**Q12. (C) Shah Jahan**  
Shah Jahan built the Taj Mahal (1632–1653) in Agra in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

**Q13. (B) Brahmaputra**  
The Brahmaputra originates in Tibet (Mansarovar Lake area) as the Tsangpo river. It enters India through Arunachal Pradesh.

**Q14. (C) 1951**  
India's first Five Year Plan was launched in 1951 under PM Jawaharlal Nehru, based on the Harrod-Domar model. It focused on agriculture and irrigation.

**Q15. (C) Nitrogen**  
Nitrogen makes up approximately 78% of the Earth's atmosphere. Oxygen is about 21%.

**Q16. (C) 6 years**  
Rajya Sabha members serve 6-year terms. One-third of the members retire every 2 years.

**Q17. (B) Aryabhata**  
Aryabhata was India's first satellite, launched on 19 April 1975 from Kapustin Yar, Russia, using a Soviet Kosmos-3M rocket.

**Q18. (B) Right to Property**  
Right to Property was a Fundamental Right under Article 31 but was removed by the 44th Amendment (1978). It is now a legal right under Article 300A.

**Q19. (B) Wular Lake**  
Wular Lake in Jammu & Kashmir is the largest freshwater lake in India. Chilika Lake (Odisha) is the largest brackish water lagoon.

**Q20. (B) Tamil Nadu**  
Bharatanatyam originated in Tamil Nadu and is one of the oldest classical dance forms. Kathakali is from Kerala.

**Q21. (B) 7**  
Pure water has a pH of 7 (neutral). pH < 7 = acidic, pH > 7 = alkaline.

**Q22. (A) 1935**  
RBI was established on 1 April 1935 based on the recommendations of the Hilton Young Commission (1926). It was nationalized in 1949.

**Q23. (C) Tarapur**  
Tarapur Atomic Power Station (Maharashtra) was India's first nuclear power plant, commissioned in 1969. Bhakra Nangal and Hirakud are hydroelectric projects.

**Q24. (C) A.P.J. Abdul Kalam**  
Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam is known as the 'Missile Man of India' for his work on ballistic missile and launch vehicle technology (Agni, Prithvi missiles).

**Q25. (D) Vitamin D**  
Vitamin D (calciferol) is synthesized in the skin upon exposure to ultraviolet-B (UVB) rays from sunlight.

**Q26. (A) 1757**  
The Battle of Plassey (23 June 1757) was fought between Siraj-ud-Daulah and the British East India Company (led by Robert Clive). Battle of Buxar was in 1764.

**Q27. (C) New York**  
The UN headquarters is in New York City, USA. Geneva houses the UN European headquarters.

**Q28. (B) Annie Besant**  
Annie Besant was the first woman President of the INC (1917, Calcutta session). Sarojini Naidu was the first Indian woman to hold the post (1925).

**Q29. (B) Goa**  
Goa is the smallest state by area (3,702 km²). Sikkim is the second smallest (7,096 km²).

**Q30. (B) Software program**  
A computer virus is a malicious software program that replicates itself and spreads from one computer to another.
