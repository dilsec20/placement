## Detailed Solutions (Selected)

**Q42:** CP = SP × 100/(100+P%) = 600 × 100/120 = ₹500
**Q43:** Total = 5 × 42 = 210. New total = 4 × 40 = 160. Excluded = 210 − 160 = 50
**Q45:** Speed = 200/20 = 10 m/s = 10 × 18/5 = 36 km/h
**Q47:** SI = 5000 × 8 × 3/100 = ₹1200
**Q50:** Together = (10×15)/(10+15) = 150/25 = 6 days
**Q52:** (x+1/x)² = x² + 2 + 1/x² → 25 = x² + 1/x² + 2 → x² + 1/x² = 23
**Q54:** Double means SI = P. T = 100/R → 5 = 100/R → R = 20%
**Q55:** Total distance = 300 km. Total time = 120/40 + 180/60 = 3+3 = 6 hrs. Avg = 300/6 = 50 km/h
**Q57:** LCM(12,15,20): 12=2²×3, 15=3×5, 20=2²×5. LCM = 2²×3×5 = 60
**Q59:** sin 30° + cos 60° = 1/2 + 1/2 = 1
**Q61:** Downstream = 24/3 = 8 km/h. Upstream = 16/4 = 4 km/h. Still water = (8+4)/2 = 6 km/h
**Q62:** 2x+3x+5x = 180 → 10x = 180 → x=18. Largest = 5×18 = 90°
**Q63:** CI = 8000[(1.1)²−1] = 8000 × 0.21 = ₹1680
**Q71:** 15 = 3×5 (composite); rest are prime
**Q74:** Father's only son = Raju. Girl is Raju's daughter.
**Q76:** Geometric: ×3 each time. 81 × 3 = 243
**Q78:** 8×3 ÷ 6 + 2 − 1 = 24/6 + 2 − 1 = 4 + 2 − 1 = 5
**Q85:** 2023 is non-leap (365 days = 52 weeks + 1 day). So 1 Jan 2024 = Monday
**Q93:** At 3:15: Hour hand = 90 + 7.5 = 97.5°. Minute hand = 90°. Angle = 7.5°
**Q99:** 143 is not a perfect square (11²=121, 12²=144). 143 = 11×13
**Q100:** Original area = s². New area = (2s)² = 4s². So 4 times.
